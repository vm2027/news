# U.S. Calls Bukele a "Valued Partner," Plans Trade Summit as Rights Groups Tally 91,000 Arrests

> **Source:** Latin Times  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01  
> **URL:** https://www.latintimes.com/us-calls-bukele-valued-partner-plans-trade-summit-rights-groups-tally-91000-arrests-598882

## Summary

The United States described El Salvador's President Nayib Bukele as a "valued partner" and announced plans for a bilateral economic summit to deepen trade, security, and migration cooperation. The article contrasts Washington's warm diplomatic language with rights groups' criticism of Bukele's security campaign, noting that around 91,000 people have been arrested under the ongoing state of exception.
