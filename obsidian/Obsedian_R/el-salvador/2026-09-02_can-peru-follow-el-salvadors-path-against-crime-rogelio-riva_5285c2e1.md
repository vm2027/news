# Can Peru Follow El Salvador’s Path Against Crime? Rogelio Rivas Weighs In

> **Source:** RPP Noticias (Video via Facebook)  
> **Published:** 2026-09-02  
> **URL:** https://www.facebook.com/rppnoticias/videos/-puede-per%C3%BA-seguir-el-camino-que-tom%C3%B3-el-salvador-frente-al-crimen-rogelio-rivas/1739630763962365/

## Summary

In this televised interview, Rogelio Rivas Polanco, a former security minister under Nayib Bukele, discusses whether Peru could adopt El Salvador’s hardline approach to combating crime. He draws parallels between Peru’s current wave of insecurity and El Salvador’s pre-crackdown situation, arguing that states facing entrenched gangs may need exceptional measures similar to Bukele’s state of exception. The conversation explores the political and legal implications of implementing mass-incarceration strategies abroad and reflects El Salvador’s growing influence in regional security debates.
