# Around 1,000 soldiers from El Salvador have deployed to Haiti, source says

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-10-27  
> **URL:** https://wsau.com/2026/10/27/around-1000-soldiers-from-el-salvador-have-deployed-to-haiti-source-says/

## Summary

Around 1,000 Salvadoran soldiers were reportedly sent to Haiti after four Salvadoran aviation technicians serving with a UN-backed force went missing. President Nayib Bukele ordered the deployment and launched an operation to recover the personnel.
