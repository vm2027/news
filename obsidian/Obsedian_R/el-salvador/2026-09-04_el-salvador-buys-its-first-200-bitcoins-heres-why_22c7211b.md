## Summary

Although focusing on El Salvador's earlier adoption of bitcoin, this article, republished with fresh date tags, recounts Bukele’s announcement that the country purchased its first 200 bitcoins ahead of making the cryptocurrency legal tender. It explains Bukele’s rationale, including lowering remittance costs for Salvadorans abroad and positioning the country at the forefront of digital finance. The piece revisits the economic and political stakes of the decision, which continue to shape debates over El Salvador’s financial policy.
