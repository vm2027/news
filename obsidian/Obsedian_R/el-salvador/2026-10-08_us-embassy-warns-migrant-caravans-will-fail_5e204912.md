# US Embassy Warns Migrant Caravans Will Fail

> **Source:** The Rio Times  
> **Published:** 2026-10-08  
> **URL:** https://www.riotimesonline.com/us-embassy-el-salvador-migrant-caravan-warning-2026/

## Summary

The U.S. Embassy in El Salvador warned migrants that attempting to enter the United States illegally would not succeed, whether traveling with smugglers, alone, or in a caravan. The warning was issued on October 6, 2026.
