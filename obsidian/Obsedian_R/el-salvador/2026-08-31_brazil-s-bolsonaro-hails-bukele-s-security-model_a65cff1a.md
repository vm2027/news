# Brazil's Bolsonaro hails Bukele's security model

> **Source:** The Manila Times  
> **Published:** 2026-08-31  
> **URL:** https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/09/01/world/americas-emea/brazils-bolsonaro-hails-bukeles-security-model/2415059

## Summary

Brazilian presidential candidate Flávio Bolsonaro praised President Nayib Bukele's hardline security model in El Salvador after meeting Salvadoran officials, highlighting the sharp reduction in homicide rates as a success story. The article details how Bukele's aggressive anti-gang policies, including mass arrests and a prolonged emergency regime, have become a reference point for right-wing politicians in Latin America despite human rights concerns.
