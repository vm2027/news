# El Salvador’s Bitcoin Treasury Hits 7,762 BTC Despite IMF Deal

> **Source:** KuCoin News  
> **Published:** 2026-09-03  
> **URL:** https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/el-salvador-s-bitcoin-treasury-hits-7-762-btc-despite-imf-deal

## Summary

This report states that El Salvador’s bitcoin treasury has grown to 7,762 BTC as the government continues its policy of buying one bitcoin per day. It notes that the accumulation persists despite the recent IMF financing agreement and interprets the strategy as evidence of Bukele’s long-term commitment to holding Bitcoin as part of the country’s reserves.
