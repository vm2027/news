# El Salvador Prepares Power Grid for Global Tech and Infrastructure Investments with $1B Plan

> **Source:** El Salvador in English  
> **Published:** 2026-08-29  
> **URL:** https://elsalvadorinenglish.com/2026/08/29/el-salvador-prepares-power-grid-for-global-tech-and-infrastructure-investments-with-1b-plan/

## Summary

El Salvador announced a $1 billion plan to upgrade its power grid to attract global technology and infrastructure investments, according to ETESAL President Edwin Núñez. The article highlights that over 750 megawatts of new generation capacity have been added under Bukele’s administration, driven by growing private investment and supportive government policy, reflecting a broader economic development push alongside security reforms.
