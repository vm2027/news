# 1,000 soldiers from El Salvador sent to Haiti after 4 UN-backed force members go missing

## Summary

About 1,000 Salvadoran soldiers were sent to Haiti after four members of a UN-backed security force disappeared during a gang ambush. The deployment followed Bukele’s announcement of a military operation to recover the missing personnel.
