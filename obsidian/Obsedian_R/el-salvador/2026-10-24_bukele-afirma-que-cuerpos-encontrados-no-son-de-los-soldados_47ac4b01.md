# Bukele afirma que cuerpos encontrados no son de los soldados salvadoreños desaparecidos en Haití

> **Source:** Associated Press via The Morning Call  
> **Published:** 2026-10-24  
> **URL:** https://www.mcall.com/2026/10/24/bukele-afirma-que-cuerpos-encontrados-no-son-de-los-soldados-salvadoreos-desaparecidos-en-hait/

## Summary

Bukele said remains received by Salvadoran officials at a Haitian airport did not belong to the four Salvadorans reported missing after a gang attack. El Salvador had sent special forces to support the rescue effort and threatened a more forceful response.
