# El Salvador President Nayib Bukele to seek third term in office

> **Source:** ITV News  
> **Published:** 2026-11-20  
> **URL:** https://www.itv.com/news/2026-11-20/el-salvador-president-nayib-bukele-to-seek-third-term-in-office

## Summary

Bukele officially launched his candidacy for a third term after submitting paperwork to El Salvador’s Supreme Electoral Tribunal. His New Ideas party controls a congressional supermajority and enabled his candidacy after constitutional changes removed presidential term limits.
