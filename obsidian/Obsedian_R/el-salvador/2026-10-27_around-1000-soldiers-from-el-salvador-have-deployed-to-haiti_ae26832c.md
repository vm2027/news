# Around 1000 soldiers from El Salvador have deployed to Haiti after four members of a UN-backed security mission went missing

> **Source:** Yahoo News  
> **Published:** 2026-10-27  
> **URL:** https://uk.news.yahoo.com/around-1-000-soldiers-el-172815445.html

## Summary

Reuters reported that El Salvador deployed roughly 1,000 troops to Haiti after four Salvadoran members of an international security mission went missing. The personnel disappeared after escaping a burning vehicle during an October 18 gang ambush.
