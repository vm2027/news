# Salvadorans to Keep Temporary Protected Status for Now, Trump Officials Say

> **Source:** The New York Times  
> **Published:** 2026-09-09  
> **URL:** https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/09/us/tps-el-salvador-immigrants-trump.html

## Summary

The New York Times reports that Salvadorans in the U.S. under Temporary Protected Status will keep their protection for now. It says the Department of Homeland Security said a broader announcement will come later.
