# In El Salvador, mothers desperately search for the disappeared

> **Source:** Genocide Watch  
> **Published:** 2026-09-13  
> **URL:** https://www.genocidewatch.com/single-post/in-el-salvador-mothers-desperately-search-for-the-disappeared

## Summary

This report examines the situation of families, particularly mothers, searching for relatives who have gone missing amid years of gang violence and security operations in El Salvador.[6] It notes that President Bukele has claimed some 80,000 people are missing, while a proposed law to centralize efforts to locate the disappeared has been rejected by the Legislative Assembly, leaving families feeling abandoned.[6]
