# El Salvador considers releasing 40,000 suspected gang members under proposed amnesty

> **Source:** Sri Lanka Guardian  
> **Published:** 2026-10-15  
> **URL:** https://slguardian.org/el-salvador-considers-releasing-40000-suspected-gang-members-under-proposed-amnesty/

## Summary

The report says Bukele is considering an amnesty that could release roughly 40,000 people detained during the state of emergency. It describes the proposal as a possible shift in the government's hardline security policy, though its implementation and legal status remain unclear.
