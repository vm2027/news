# President Bukele defends Salvadoran forces in Haiti against allegations of criminal acts

> **Source:** Anadolu Agency  
> **Published:** 2026-11-01  
> **URL:** https://u.aa.com.tr/en/americas/president-bukele-defends-salvadoran-forces-in-haiti-against-allegations-of-criminal-acts/4107707

## Summary

President Nayib Bukele rejected allegations that Salvadoran forces deployed to Haiti carried out airstrikes against civilians. He said the helicopters arrived on October 29 and had not conducted any missions when the accusations were reported.
