## Summary

Congress is considering the future of U.S. immigration policy affecting more than 170,000 Salvadorans with Temporary Protected Status. The article also examines the expanding U.S.–El Salvador security relationship, concerns about human rights, prison conditions, and President Nayib Bukele’s consolidation of political power.
