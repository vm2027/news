# Bukele: “El Salvador Will Inspire the World,” AI Education Pilot Delivers Results Comparable to Sweden and Germany

> **Source:** Ground News  
> **Published:** 2026-09-10  
> **URL:** https://ground.news/article/bukele-el-salvador-will-inspire-the-world-ai-education-pilot-delivers-results-comparable-to-sweden-and-germany

## Summary

President Nayib Bukele has highlighted the results of an AI-based education pilot program in 171 public schools, saying that student performance in reading, math and science is now comparable to average outcomes in Sweden and Germany. The initiative, supported by the World Bank and xAI, uses virtual AI tutors and is intended to be scaled nationwide, positioning education reform and technology as central pillars of Bukele’s broader development strategy for El Salvador.
