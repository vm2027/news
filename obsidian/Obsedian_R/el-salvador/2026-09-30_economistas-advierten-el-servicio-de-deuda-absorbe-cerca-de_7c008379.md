# Economistas advierten el servicio de deuda absorbe cerca de 0.30 por cada dólar recaudado por el gobierno salvadoreño

> **Source:** Infobae  
> **Published:** 2026-09-30  
> **URL:** https://www.infobae.com/el-salvador/2026/09/30/economistas-advierten-el-servicio-de-deuda-absorbe-cerca-de-030-por-cada-dolar-recaudado-por-el-gobierno-salvadoreno/

## Summary

Economists warned that debt service consumes approximately 30 cents of every dollar collected by El Salvador's government. The report highlights the pressure that debt repayments place on public finances and the country's broader fiscal outlook.
