# El Salvador deploys 2,000 soldiers to Haiti amid search for missing technicians

> **Source:** Caribbean Broadcasting Corporation  
> **Published:** 2026-10-30  
> **URL:** https://www.cbc.bb/main-stories/el-salvador-deploys-2000-soldiers-to-haiti-amid-search-for-missing-technicians/

## Summary

El Salvador sent more than 2,000 soldiers to Haiti to locate four missing technicians assigned to the UN-authorized Gang Suppression Force. President Nayib Bukele said the operation was a rescue mission, not an invasion, and that El Salvador was covering most of its costs.
