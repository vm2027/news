## Summary

The article describes El Salvador’s push for energy autonomy through a diversified power generation mix and a $1 billion expansion plan led by state transmission company ETESAL. It notes comments by ETESAL’s president that the country is not at risk of electricity rationing, crediting infrastructure expansion and diversification under Bukele’s administration as key to supporting economic growth and investment.
