# El Salvador Economy Grows 5.1% in Second Quarter as Construction Leads

> **Source:** The Rio Times  
> **Published:** 2026-10-01  
> **URL:** https://www.riotimesonline.com/el-salvador-economy-grows-5-1-percent-q2-2026/

## Summary

El Salvador's economy grew 5.1% year over year in the second quarter of 2026, according to the Central Reserve Bank. Growth accelerated from 4.8% in the first quarter, with construction identified as a leading contributor.
