# Bukele Highlights World Bank Projections that Place El Salvador Among the Fastest Growing in the Region

## Summary

Bukele highlighted World Bank projections showing El Salvador’s gross domestic product could grow by 4.5%. The reported figure matches the projected average for East Asia and the Pacific and was presented as evidence of stronger regional economic performance.
