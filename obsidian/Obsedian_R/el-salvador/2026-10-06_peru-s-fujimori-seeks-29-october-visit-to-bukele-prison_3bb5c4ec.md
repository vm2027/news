# Peru's Fujimori Seeks 29 October Visit to Bukele Prison

> **Source:** The Rio Times  
> **Published:** 2026-10-06  
> **URL:** https://www.riotimesonline.com/peru-fujimori-bukele-prison-visit-2026/

## Summary

Peruvian President Keiko Fujimori’s government requested authorization for a visit to El Salvador from October 28 to 31. The planned itinerary includes CECOT and meetings with Salvadoran security officials to exchange experiences on security, justice, education, and health.
