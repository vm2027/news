# El Salvador deploys special forces to Haiti to help find 4 missing soldiers who came under attack

> **Source:** Texarkana Gazette  
> **Published:** 2026-10-23  
> **URL:** https://www.texarkanagazette.com/news/2026/oct/23/el-salvador-deploys-special-forces-to-haiti-to/

## Summary

El Salvador deployed 100 special-forces soldiers to Haiti to search for four aviation technicians who disappeared after gangs attacked their vehicle. The mission follows Bukele’s announcement that Salvadoran forces would attempt to rescue the missing personnel.
