# Haitian prime minister to discuss Salvadoran troops in Haiti with El Salvador government

## Summary

Haiti’s prime minister is expected to visit El Salvador to discuss the future of Salvadoran troops deployed in Haiti. The visit follows an operation against 400 Mawozo and the deployment of at least 2,500 Salvadoran forces.
