# El Salvador uses armed robot dogs in Haiti gang offensive

> **Source:** Jamaica Observer  
> **Published:** 2026-11-13

## Summary

Salvadoran forces reportedly used rifle-equipped robot dogs during an operation to clear a gang-controlled stadium in Haiti. The operation followed Bukele’s deployment of troops after several Salvadorans were kidnapped or went missing.
