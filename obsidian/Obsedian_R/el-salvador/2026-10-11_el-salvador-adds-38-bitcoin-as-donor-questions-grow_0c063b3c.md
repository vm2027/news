# El Salvador Adds 38 Bitcoin as Donor Questions Grow

> **Source:** The Rio Times  
> **Published:** 2026-10-11  
> **URL:** https://www.riotimesonline.com/el-salvador-bitcoin-donations-2026/

## Summary

El Salvador added another bitcoin to its national reserve on October 10, bringing the reported total to 7,801 coins. The purchase renewed questions about the government’s continued bitcoin accumulation after its staff-level agreement with the IMF.
