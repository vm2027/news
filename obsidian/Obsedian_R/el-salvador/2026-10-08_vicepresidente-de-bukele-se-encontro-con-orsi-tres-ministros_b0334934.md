# Vicepresidente de Bukele se encontró con Orsi, tres ministros, un senador oficialista, varios opositores y masones

> **Published:** 2026-10-08  
> **URL:** https://www.busqueda.com.uy/politica/vicepresidente-bukele-se-encontro-orsi-tres-ministros-un-senador-oficialista-varios-opositores-y-masones-n5418102

## Summary

El Salvador’s Vice President Félix Ulloa met with Uruguay’s president, ministers, lawmakers, opposition figures, and Freemasons. Ulloa defended Bukele’s security model and rejected criticism that the state of exception violates human rights, while saying Uruguay did not express interest in adopting it.
