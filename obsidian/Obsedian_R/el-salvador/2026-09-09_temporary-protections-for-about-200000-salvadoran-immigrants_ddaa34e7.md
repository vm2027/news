# Temporary protections for about 200,000 Salvadoran immigrants are in limbo as deadline nears

> **Source:** The Boston Globe  
> **Published:** 2026-09-09  
> **URL:** https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/09/09/nation/tps-el-salvador-immigration-protections/

## Summary

With the TPS deadline for El Salvador set for September 9, 2026, around 200,000 Salvadoran immigrants in the US face uncertainty about whether they will be able to remain legally. The article notes that President Joe Biden had previously extended protections for 18 months, but the current administration has yet to clarify its stance, raising questions for Salvadoran families and for El Salvador, which depends heavily on remittances from abroad.
