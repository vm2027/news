# Fighting organised crime: Brazilian voters the latest to eye Bukele security model with envy

> **Source:** RTL Today  
> **Published:** 2026-09-16  
> **URL:** https://today.rtl.lu/news/world/brazilian-voters-the-latest-to-eye-bukele-security-model-with-envy-1403767638

## Summary

This article reports that Brazilian voters and politicians are increasingly looking with admiration at Nayib Bukele’s security model in El Salvador as they contend with severe crime at home. It describes how Bukele reduced gang violence through mass arrests and tough prison policies, while also noting criticism from rights groups over arbitrary detentions, torture, and deaths in custody, highlighting the trade-off between security gains and civil liberties.
