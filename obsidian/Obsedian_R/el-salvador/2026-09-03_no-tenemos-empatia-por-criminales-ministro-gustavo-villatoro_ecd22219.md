# “No tenemos empatía por criminales”: Ministro Gustavo Villatoro defiende en Argentina el plan de Bukele contra pandillas

> **Source:** Infobae  
> **Published:** 2026-09-03  
> **URL:** https://www.infobae.com/el-salvador/2026/09/03/no-tenemos-empatia-por-criminales-ministro-gustavo-villatoro-defiende-en-argentina-el-plan-de-bukele-contra-pandillas/

## Summary

Security Minister Gustavo Villatoro, during a visit to Argentina, robustly defends President Nayib Bukele’s anti‑gang strategy, stating that the government has no empathy for criminals and emphasizing the model’s reported 98% approval among Salvadorans. He frames the trip as an effort to expand bilateral security cooperation while presenting Bukele’s security policies as a successful template for combating organized crime.
