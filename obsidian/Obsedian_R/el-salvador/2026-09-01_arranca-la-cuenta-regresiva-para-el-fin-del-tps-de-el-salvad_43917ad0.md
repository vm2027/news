# Arranca la cuenta regresiva para el fin del TPS de El Salvador

> **Published:** 2026-09-01  
> **URL:** https://www.eldiariodehoy.com/noticias/nacionales/entre-incertidumbre-y-esperanza-inicia-la-cuenta-regresiva-del-tps-para-el-salvador/91711/2026/

## Summary

The article explains that the start of September marks the countdown to the expiration of Temporary Protected Status (TPS) for Salvadorans in the United States on September 9. It assesses political, diplomatic, and economic factors influencing whether TPS will be extended, emphasizing Bukele’s close relationship with the Trump administration and recent efforts to deepen bilateral economic ties.
