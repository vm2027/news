# El Salvador deploys special forces on Haiti rescue mission

> **Source:** Yahoo News  
> **Published:** 2026-10-23  
> **URL:** https://www.yahoo.com/news/world/articles/el-salvador-deploys-special-forces-100843857.html

## Summary

El Salvador sent approximately 100 special-forces personnel to Haiti to help rescue four Salvadoran aviation technicians missing after a gang attack. The deployment followed Bukele’s announcement of a military operation and his warning of a stronger response if the men were not returned safely.
