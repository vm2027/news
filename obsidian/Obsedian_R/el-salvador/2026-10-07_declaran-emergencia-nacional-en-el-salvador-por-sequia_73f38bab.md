# Declaran emergencia nacional en El Salvador por sequía

> **Source:** Prensa Latina  
> **Published:** 2026-10-07  
> **URL:** https://www.prensa-latina.cu/2026/10/07/declaran-emergencia-nacional-en-el-salvador-por-sequia/

## Summary

El Salvador’s Legislative Assembly declared a national emergency over meteorological and agricultural drought attributed to El Niño. The six-month measure includes emergency assistance and requires the Civil Protection director to report actions to President Nayib Bukele.
