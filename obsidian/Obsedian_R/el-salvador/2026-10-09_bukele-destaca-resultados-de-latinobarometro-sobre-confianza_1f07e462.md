# Bukele destaca resultados de Latinobarómetro sobre confianza institucional de El Salvador

> **Source:** Diario El Mundo  
> **Published:** 2026-10-09  
> **URL:** https://diario.elmundo.sv/politica/bukele-destaca-resultados-de-latinobarometro-sobre-confianza-institucional-de-el-salvador

## Summary

Nayib Bukele highlighted favorable 2026 Latinobarómetro findings on confidence in El Salvador’s public institutions. The reported figures include high approval of the president, armed forces, police, public education, and the government, while the article notes that Bukele did not share data concerning support for nondemocratic government.
