# ‘And he never came back’: In El Salvador, mothers desperately search for the disappeared

> **Source:** El País (English edition)  
> **Published:** 2026-09-13  
> **URL:** https://english.elpais.com/international/2026-09-13/and-he-never-came-back-in-el-salvador-mothers-desperately-search-for-the-disappeared.html

## Summary

This article reports on Salvadoran mothers searching for relatives who vanished amid President Nayib Bukele’s sweeping anti‑gang crackdown. It notes that between March 2022 and August 2026, more than 92,000 people were imprisoned on gang membership charges, with mass trials launched for crimes accumulated over decades. The piece highlights the human rights concerns and the lack of information families face about detainees and the disappeared.
