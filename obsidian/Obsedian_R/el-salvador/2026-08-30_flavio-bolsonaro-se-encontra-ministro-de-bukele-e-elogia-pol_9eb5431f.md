# Flávio Bolsonaro se encontra ministro de Bukele e elogia política de segurança de El Salvador

> **Source:** O Tempo  
> **Published:** 2026-08-30  
> **URL:** https://www.otempo.com.br/eleicoes/2026/presidentes/2026/8/30/flavio-bolsonaro-se-encontra-ministro-de-bukele-e-elogia-politica-de-seguranca-de-el-salvador

## Summary

Brazilian senator and presidential candidate Flávio Bolsonaro met with Gustavo Villatoro, El Salvador’s minister of justice and public security, in São Paulo and praised Nayib Bukele’s security policy as one of the most effective recoveries of national sovereignty. Bolsonaro highlighted El Salvador’s permanent state of exception, which suspends rights such as due process and communication privacy and has led to mass arrests, while noting criticism from organizations like Human Rights Watch over torture and deaths in custody. The meeting indicates Bolsonaro’s interest in importing aspects of Bukele’s model to address crime in Brazil’s campaign context.
