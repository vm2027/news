# Bukele denies allegations of airstrikes by Salvadoran forces in Haiti

> **Source:** De Último Minuto  
> **Published:** 2026-11-01  
> **URL:** https://deultimominuto.com/en/international/bukele-denies-allegations-of-airstrikes-by-salvadoran-forces-in-haiti/

## Summary

President Nayib Bukele rejected allegations that Salvadoran forces in Haiti conducted airstrikes or helicopter attacks against civilian areas. He said the Salvadoran contingent had no bombs and that its helicopters arrived after the date cited in the allegations.
