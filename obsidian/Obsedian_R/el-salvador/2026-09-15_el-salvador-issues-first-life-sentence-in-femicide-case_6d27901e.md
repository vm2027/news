# El Salvador issues first life sentence in femicide case

> **Source:** UPI via Yahoo News  
> **Published:** 2026-09-15  
> **URL:** https://www.yahoo.com/news/world/articles/el-salvador-issues-first-life-161229211.html

## Summary

An El Salvador court has issued the country’s first life sentence for aggravated femicide under constitutional and criminal reforms championed by President Nayib Bukele. The article notes that Eduardo Mauricio Urbina García became the second person to receive a life term under the new framework, illustrating the rapid implementation of Bukele’s harsher sentencing policies.
