# Presidenta de Perú confirma visita a El Salvador

> **Source:** El Diario de Hoy  
> **Published:** 2026-09-28  
> **URL:** https://www.elsalvador.com/noticias/nacional/keiko-fujimori-bukele-seguridad/1295583/2026/

## Summary

Peruvian President Keiko Fujimori confirmed that she will visit El Salvador in late October to meet Nayib Bukele. Citizen security and the Salvadoran government’s experience confronting criminal groups are expected to be discussed.
