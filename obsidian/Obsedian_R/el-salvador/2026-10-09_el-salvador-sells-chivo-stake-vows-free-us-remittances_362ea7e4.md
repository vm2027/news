# El Salvador Sells Chivo Stake, Vows Free US Remittances

> **Source:** The Rio Times  
> **Published:** 2026-10-09  
> **URL:** https://www.riotimesonline.com/el-salvador-chivo-wallet-sale-free-remittances-2026/

## Summary

El Salvador and Linguard Labs announced that the payments company now controls a private majority stake in the Chivo wallet. The new owner plans to offer zero-fee remittances from the United States to users in Chivo’s regular category.
