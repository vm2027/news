# El Salvador Sets Date for 2027 Elections, Bukele Will Seek Third Term

> **Source:** Ground News

> **URL:** https://ground.news/article/elections-scheduled-for-28-february-in-el-salvador-nayib-bukele-will-run-for-a-third-term

## Summary

The report describes the electoral tribunal’s announcement that presidential elections will be held on February 28, 2027. It says Bukele will seek another term under a controversial constitutional amendment that permits indefinite presidential re-election.
