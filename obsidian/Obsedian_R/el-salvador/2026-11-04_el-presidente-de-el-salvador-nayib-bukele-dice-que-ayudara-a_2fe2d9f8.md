# El presidente de El Salvador, Nayib Bukele, dice que ayudará a los haitianos a "retomar el país"

> **Source:** EFE  
> **Published:** 2026-11-04  
> **URL:** https://efe.com/mundo/2026-11-04/bukele-el-salvador-ayuda-haitianos-recuperar-el-pais-soldados/

## Summary

Bukele said El Salvador would help Haitians retake their country and defeat armed gangs. His comments came after Haiti’s prime minister described Salvadoran support for the campaign against gangs and amid the search for four missing Salvadoran personnel.
