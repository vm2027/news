# Haitians rally behind Bukele, urge Salvadoran forces to drive gangs out of their communities

> **Source:** The Haitian Times  
> **Published:** 2026-11-07  
> **URL:** https://haitiantimes.com/2026/11/07/haitians-rally-behind-bukele-salvadoran-troops-gangs/

## Summary

Hundreds of Haitians reportedly rallied in Port-au-Prince, Belladère, and Jérémie in support of Salvadoran President Nayib Bukele. Demonstrators called for Salvadoran forces to take stronger action against gangs that have displaced residents.
