# Trump administration keeps TPS in place for El Salvador for now

> **Source:** The Hill  
> **Published:** 2026-09-10  
> **URL:** https://thehill.com/latino/6081697-trump-administration-el-salvador-tps/

## Summary

The Hill reports that the Trump administration has decided, at least temporarily, to keep TPS protections for El Salvador in place after a deadline passed without a formal decision. The article notes DHS’s statement that an announcement will be made "at the appropriate time" and that Salvadorans currently under TPS retain protection until then. It also highlights the political relationship between Trump and El Salvador’s President Nayib Bukele, including Bukele’s earlier agreement to imprison U.S. deportees in exchange for financial support, showing how bilateral politics intersect with immigration policy.
