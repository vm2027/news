# Bukele Holds 6,494 Bitcoin as IMF Deal Ends Compulsory Acceptance

> **Source:** The Rio Times  
> **Published:** 2026-09-05  
> **URL:** https://www.riotimesonline.com/el-salvador-bitcoin-bukele-2026/

## Summary

The article analyzes El Salvador's bitcoin strategy under President Nayib Bukele, reporting updated figures on the country's bitcoin holdings alongside changes tied to an agreement with the IMF. It notes that while compulsory acceptance of bitcoin has been phased out for many transactions, the government continues to maintain a substantial bitcoin reserve. The piece explores how these policy adjustments aim to balance Bukele's crypto agenda with international financial pressures and domestic economic stability.
