# Bukele alters Constitution to endorse life imprisonment in El Salvador

> **Source:** El País  
> **Published:** 2026-03-18  
> **URL:** https://english.elpais.com/international/2026-03-18/bukele-alters-constitution-to-endorse-life-imprisonment-in-el-salvador.html

## Summary

El País reports that El Salvador’s legislature approved a constitutional reform allowing life sentences for murderers, rapists, and terrorists. The piece also says the reform came amid criticism from international legal experts over human-rights abuses under the state of emergency.
