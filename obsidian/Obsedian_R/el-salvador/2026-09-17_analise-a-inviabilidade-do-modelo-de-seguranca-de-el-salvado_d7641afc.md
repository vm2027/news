## Summary

This analysis examines El Salvador’s Bukele-era "mano dura" security model, arguing that its heavy-handed tactics are incompatible with democratic norms and human rights.[4] It questions the long-term effectiveness of Bukele’s approach in confronting complex organized crime and discusses why replicating the Salvadoran model in Brazil would be problematic.[4]
