# Immigrants brace for Trump decision on El Salvador TPS

## Summary

The article examines anxiety among Salvadoran immigrants in the U.S. ahead of a decision on the future of Temporary Protected Status for El Salvador. It also details a previous agreement between Donald Trump and Nayib Bukele that sent migrants from U.S. detention to El Salvador’s high‑security CECOT prison, underscoring Bukele’s alignment with Trump on migration and security.
