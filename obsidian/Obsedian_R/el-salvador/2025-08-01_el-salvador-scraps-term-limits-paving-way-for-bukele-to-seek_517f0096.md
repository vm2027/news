# El Salvador scraps term limits, paving way for Bukele to seek re-election

> **Source:** BBC News  
> **Published:** 2025-08-01  
> **URL:** https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/czd04q87zryo

## Summary

El Salvador’s legislature eliminated presidential term limits and extended presidential terms to six years, enabling Bukele to seek indefinite re-election. The reform also aligns the election cycle for 2027.
