# US Judge Holds Homeland Security in Contempt Over CECOT Deportation

> **Source:** The Rio Times  
> **Published:** 2026-09-26  
> **URL:** https://www.riotimesonline.com/us-courts-el-salvador-cecot-deportations-contempt-2026/

## Summary

A U.S. federal judge held the Department of Homeland Security in civil contempt over the deportation of an asylum seeker to El Salvador’s CECOT prison. The ruling ordered the government to return the person and restore their documents, intensifying legal scrutiny of deportations to the facility created under Bukele.
