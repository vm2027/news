# El Salvador deploys 450 troops to Haiti after four aviation technicians disappear in a gang ambush

> **Source:** Latin Times  
> **Published:** 2026-10-26  
> **URL:** https://www.latintimes.com/el-salvador-troops-haiti-missing-technicians-600562

## Summary

El Salvador deployed approximately 450 special forces troops to Haiti after four aviation technicians went missing following an October 18 ambush. Bukele rejected claims that recovered remains belonged to the Salvadorans and said the search was continuing.
