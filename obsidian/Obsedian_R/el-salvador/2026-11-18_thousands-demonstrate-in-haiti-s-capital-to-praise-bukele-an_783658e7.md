# Thousands demonstrate in Haiti's capital to praise Bukele and Salvadoran troops fighting gangs

> **Source:** The Associated Press via Yahoo News  
> **Published:** 2026-11-18  
> **URL:** https://ca.news.yahoo.com/thousands-demonstrate-haiti-capital-praise-165123418.html

## Summary

Thousands marched in Port-au-Prince to support Bukele and Salvadoran troops fighting Haitian gangs. Haitian officials said the deployment would continue for an undetermined period, at least until elections next year.
