# Brazil's Flavio Bolsonaro Wants Gangs Declared Terrorists and a Security Ministry

> **Source:** The Rio Times  
> **Published:** 2026-08-31  
> **URL:** https://www.riotimesonline.com/flavio-bolsonaro-terrorist-gangs-security-ministry-2026/

## Summary

Brazilian presidential hopeful Flávio Bolsonaro has proposed creating a dedicated public security ministry and declaring gangs and militias terrorist organizations, citing El Salvador’s security model under President Nayib Bukele as inspiration. After meeting El Salvador’s justice and public security minister Gustavo Villatoro in São Paulo on August 30, Bolsonaro praised Bukele’s state-of-exception regime, which suspends constitutional guarantees and allows mass imprisonment. His proposals underscore the growing regional influence of Bukele’s hardline anti-crime policies on other right-wing politicians in Latin America.
