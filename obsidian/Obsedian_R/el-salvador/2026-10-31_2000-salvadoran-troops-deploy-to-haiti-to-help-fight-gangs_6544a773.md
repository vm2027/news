# 2,000 Salvadoran troops deploy to Haiti to help fight gangs

> **Source:** Africanews  
> **Published:** 2026-10-31  
> **URL:** https://www.africanews.com/2026/10/31/2000-salvadoran-troops-deploy-to-haiti-to-help-fight-gangs/

## Summary

El Salvador deployed approximately 2,000 troops to Haiti following an attack and the disappearance of four Salvadoran aviation technicians. The deployment is intended to help locate the missing personnel and confront armed gangs.
