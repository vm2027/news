# IMF Hands El Salvador $139M, Then Tells It to Stop Buying Bitcoin

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-10-02  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/crypto/articles/imf-hands-el-salvador-139m-104715888.html

## Summary

The IMF approved approximately $138–139 million for El Salvador after completing reviews of its lending program and granting waivers for missed conditions. The fund urged President Nayib Bukele’s government to stop accumulating Bitcoin and reduce the state’s involvement in cryptocurrency, while projecting economic growth of 4.5% in 2026 and 4% in 2027.
