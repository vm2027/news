# Mass Trials Put Hundreds in One Dock in El Salvador

> **Source:** The New York Times  
> **Published:** 2026-10-07  
> **URL:** https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/07/world/americas/el-salvador-mass-trials-bukele.html

## Summary

The article described court hearings in which hundreds of detainees appear simultaneously by video. It reported that mass trials under Bukele’s anti-gang campaign have produced near-universal convictions for gang-related offenses, according to Human Rights Watch and defense lawyers.
