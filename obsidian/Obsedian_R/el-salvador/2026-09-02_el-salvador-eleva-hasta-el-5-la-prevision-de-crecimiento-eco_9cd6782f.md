# El Salvador eleva hasta el 5% la previsión de crecimiento económico de 2026 por turismo y construcción

> **Source:** La Veu Lliure  
> **Published:** 2026-09-02  
> **URL:** https://laveulliure.ad/es/articulo/el-salvador-bukele-prevision-crecimiento-economico-2026-turismo-construccion

## Summary

This article reports that El Salvador has raised its 2026 economic growth forecast to between 4.5% and 5%, up from an earlier 3% estimate by the central bank, crediting construction, tourism, and investment as key drivers. Authorities link the improved outlook to Bukele’s government policies and to a better perception of security, which is seen as attracting foreign investment and boosting the tourism sector.
