# El Salvador deploys special forces to Haiti to rescue kidnapped troops

> **Source:** Anadolu Agency  
> **Published:** 2026-10-24  
> **URL:** https://www.aa.com.tr/en/americas/el-salvador-deploys-special-forces-to-haiti-to-rescue-kidnapped-troops/4099174

## Summary

El Salvador sent special forces to Haiti to rescue four military technicians missing after an attack by armed gangs. Bukele dismissed warnings about the operation and demanded that the soldiers be returned alive and unharmed.
