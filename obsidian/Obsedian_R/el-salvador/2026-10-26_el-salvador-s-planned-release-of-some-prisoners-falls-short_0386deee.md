# El Salvador's planned release of some prisoners falls short for many families

> **Source:** WLRN  
> **Published:** 2026-10-26  
> **URL:** https://www.wlrn.org/americas/2026-10-26/el-salvadors-planned-release-of-some-prisoners-falls-short-for-many-families

## Summary

Families criticized Bukele's proposed release program for prisoners detained during the government's anti-gang crackdown. The plan could review more than 10,000 cases involving alleged gang collaborators, with some detainees potentially receiving conditional release and community-service alternatives.
