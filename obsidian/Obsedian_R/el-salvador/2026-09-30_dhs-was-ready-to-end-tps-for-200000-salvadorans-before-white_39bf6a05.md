# DHS Was Ready to End TPS for 200,000 Salvadorans Before White House Stepped In

> **Source:** The Latin Times  
> **Published:** 2026-09-30  
> **URL:** https://www.latintimes.com/dhs-was-ready-end-tps-200000-salvadorans-before-white-house-stepped-599708

## Summary

The article reports that the U.S. Department of Homeland Security had prepared to end Temporary Protected Status for roughly 200,000 Salvadorans before intervention by the White House. It also discusses Bukele's efforts to maintain protections for Salvadorans in the United States.
