# At WTO World Trade & Tech Day, El Salvador Showcases AI Progress Under President Bukele

> **Published:** 2026-09-15  
> **URL:** https://ground.news/article/presents-to-the-wto-the-use-of-artificial-intelligence-in-el-salvadors-trade

## Summary

El Salvador’s Minister of Economy, María Luisa Hayem, highlighted the country’s rapid integration of artificial intelligence into core economic sectors during the WTO’s World Trade & Tech Day, framing it as a key pillar of the Bukele administration’s economic strategy.[5] The presentation underscored how AI is being used to enhance trade processes and competitiveness and cited domestic media reports from September 14, 2026, about the initiative.[5]
