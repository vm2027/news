# Temporary protected status for 200,000 Salvadoran immigrants in limbo as deadline passes

> **Source:** Associated Press  
> **Published:** 2026-09-10  
> **URL:** https://apnews.com/article/el-salvador-immigrants-temporary-protected-status-92be904bf69e3f1973a928f2733cbf0e

## Summary

AP reports that the Trump administration allowed a key deadline to pass without announcing whether it will end Temporary Protected Status (TPS) for about 200,000 immigrants from El Salvador. The lack of a decision has left Salvadoran nationals in the United States in legal limbo, as DHS says protections will remain in effect until an announcement is made but has not clarified whether an official extension has been granted. The article places this uncertainty in the context of Biden’s earlier 18‑month extension to September 9, 2026 and the broader U.S. debate over immigration and deportations affecting Salvadorans.
