# El Salvador se prepara ante posible riesgo alimentario por El Niño

> **Source:** La Nación  
> **Published:** 2026-10-08  
> **URL:** https://www.nacion.com/el-mundo/el-salvador-se-prepara-ante-posible-riesgo/QDWFKJZBEFHXTDMHTN6YWZCBA4/story/

## Summary

El Salvador approved a six-month emergency plan to address potential food-supply risks arising from El Niño-related drought. The plan emphasizes creating food reserves and providing humanitarian assistance to vulnerable families.
