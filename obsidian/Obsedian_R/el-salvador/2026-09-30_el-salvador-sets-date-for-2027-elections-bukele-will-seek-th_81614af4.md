# El Salvador sets date for 2027 elections, Bukele will seek third term

> **Source:** EFE  
> **Published:** 2026-09-30  
> **URL:** https://efe.com/english/latest-news/2026-09-30/el-salvador-sets-date-for-2027-elections-bukele-will-seek-third-term/

## Summary

El Salvador’s Supreme Electoral Tribunal announced that general elections will be held on February 28, 2027, covering 582 elected offices. Bukele is expected to seek a third consecutive term under constitutional amendments that permit immediate reelection, while economic concerns are becoming more prominent despite his continued popularity.
