# Ministro de El Salvador diz que facções “usurpavam” funções do Estado

> **Source:** Poder360  
> **Published:** 2026-08-31  
> **URL:** https://www.poder360.com.br/poder-internacional/ministro-de-el-salvador-diz-que-faccoes-usurpavam-funcoes-do-estado/

## Summary

El Salvador’s Minister of Justice and Public Security Gustavo Villatoro declared that criminal factions had "usurped" state functions before Bukele’s government launched its crackdown on organized crime. In an interview, he defended treating gangs as integrated criminal structures to retake territory and restore state control, underscoring the government’s narrative that the emergency regime is necessary for national security.
