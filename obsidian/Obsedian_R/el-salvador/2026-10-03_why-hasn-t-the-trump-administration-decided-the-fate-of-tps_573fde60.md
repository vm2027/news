# Why hasn't the Trump administration decided the fate of TPS for Salvadorans?

> **Source:** Los Angeles Times  
> **Published:** 2026-10-03  
> **URL:** https://www.latimes.com/politics/story/2026-10-03/why-hasnt-trump-administration-decided-fate-of-tps-for-salvadorans

## Summary

The article examines the Trump administration’s unresolved decision on Temporary Protected Status for Salvadorans in the United States. It reports that ending TPS could damage El Salvador’s remittance-dependent economy and that President Nayib Bukele has advocated for additional time.
