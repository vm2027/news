# US temporary protections for about 200000 Salvadoran immigrants in limbo

> **Source:** TimesLIVE  
> **Published:** 2026-09-10  
> **URL:** https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/world/2026-09-10-us-temporary-protections-for-about-200000-salvadoran-immigrants-in-limbo/

## Summary

TimesLIVE details how temporary protected status for roughly 200,000 Salvadoran immigrants in the United States has entered a period of uncertainty as a deadline for a decision on the program passes. The report recalls that former President Joe Biden extended TPS for El Salvador for 18 months, to September 9, 2026, and notes that it is now up to the Trump administration to decide whether to continue or terminate the protections. The piece highlights the political and humanitarian stakes of the decision for Salvadorans who have lived in the U.S. for years under TPS.
