# El Salvador's planned release of some prisoners falls short for many families

> **Source:** WLRN  
> **Published:** 2026-10-26  
> **URL:** https://www.wlrn.org/light/americas/2026-10-26/el-salvadors-planned-release-of-some-prisoners-falls-short-for-many-families

## Summary

Families criticized the government’s proposed release of some prisoners detained during El Salvador’s mass anti-gang crackdown. Bukele said authorities were reviewing thousands of cases involving alleged gang collaborators, but relatives said the plan would not address many unjust detentions.
