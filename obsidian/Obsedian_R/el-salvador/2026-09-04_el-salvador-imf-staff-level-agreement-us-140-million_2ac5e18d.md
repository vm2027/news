# El Salvador IMF staff-level agreement, US$140 million

> **Source:** The Rio Times  
> **Published:** 2026-09-04  
> **URL:** https://www.riotimesonline.com/el-salvador-imf-staff-level-agreement-2026/

## Summary

This piece reports that IMF staff and El Salvador have reached a staff-level agreement that could unlock around US$140 million, pending approval by the IMF’s executive board. It explains that the funds are tied to prior actions under the reform program and situates the deal within El Salvador’s broader economic policy under President Bukele.
