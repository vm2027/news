# El Salvador's Bukele plans mass release of prisoners in amnesty

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-10-15  
> **URL:** https://www.yahoo.com/news/world/articles/el-salvadors-bukele-plans-mass-114835045.html

## Summary

President Nayib Bukele indicated that an amnesty could allow the release of a large share of El Salvador's prisoners. A commission would review cases, with parole potentially available to people convicted of less serious offenses or who cooperated with authorities.
