# El Salvador: Um balanço dos 7 anos de Bukele

> **Source:** Brasil Comunista  
> **Published:** 2026-08-29  
> **URL:** https://brasilcomunista.org/um-balanci-dos-sete-anos-de-bukele/

## Summary

This long‑form analysis reviews Bukele’s seven years in power, emphasizing dramatic gains in public security alongside growing economic and fiscal vulnerabilities. It notes that while homicide rates have fallen to historic lows under an extended state of emergency, public debt has surged toward roughly 90% of GDP and the economy remains the weakest‑rated area of his administration.
