# Haitian leader to visit El Salvador to discuss future of Salvadoran troops' offensive in Haiti

> **Source:** Star Tribune  
> **Published:** 2026-11-10  
> **URL:** https://www.startribune.com/haitian-leader-to-visit-el-salvador-to-discuss-future-of-salvadoran-troops-offensive-in-haiti/601914717

## Summary

Haiti's prime minister is expected to visit El Salvador to discuss the future of Salvadoran forces operating in Haiti. The visit follows an escalation involving roughly 2,500 Salvadoran troops and operations against powerful Haitian gangs.
