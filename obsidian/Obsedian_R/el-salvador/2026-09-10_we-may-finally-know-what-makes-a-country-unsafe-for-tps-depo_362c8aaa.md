# We may finally know what makes a country 'unsafe' for TPS deportations: a bromance with Trump

> **Source:** WLRN  
> **Published:** 2026-09-10  
> **URL:** https://www.wlrn.org/light/commentary/2026-09-10/we-may-finally-know-what-makes-a-country-unsafe-for-tps-deportation-a-bromance-with-trump

## Summary

This is a lighter‑format version of WLRN’s commentary on how U.S. TPS decisions are shaped by political relationships, again focusing on El Salvador under Nayib Bukele. It reiterates that Trump’s border officials portray El Salvador as significantly safer under Bukele’s harsh anti‑gang measures, while acknowledging economic stagnation and the failure of the Bitcoin experiment. The piece suggests that Bukele’s image as the "world’s coolest dictator" and his alignment with Trump play an outsized role in determining whether El Salvador is treated as "unsafe" for deportations.
