# El posible retorno masivo de salvadoreños amenaza la estabilidad de la economía local por la alta dependencia de remesas, según un economista

> **Source:** Infobae  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01  
> **URL:** https://www.infobae.com/el-salvador/2026/09/01/el-posible-retorno-masivo-de-salvadorenos-amenaza-la-estabilidad-de-la-economia-local-por-la-alta-dependencia-de-remesas-segun-un-economista/

## Summary

The piece analyzes how a potential mass return of Salvadorans from the United States, amid uncertainty over the renewal of Temporary Protected Status (TPS), could destabilize El Salvador’s economy. It notes the country’s heavy dependence on remittances and warns that a sudden inflow of returnees would strain the labor market, public services, and social cohesion.
