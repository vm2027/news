# President Nayib Bukele Deploys Elite Forces Against Haitian Gangs

> **Source:** The Chosun Daily  
> **Published:** 2026-10-28  
> **URL:** https://www.chosun.com/english/world-en/2026/10/28/P7PTSWMNFRE6VCSOWPDKOIQH6E/

## Summary

Bukele sent 450 Salvadoran personnel, including 100 special operations troops, to Haiti after a gang attack involving Salvadoran military personnel. The deployment raised questions about whether El Salvador would conduct a broad anti-gang operation in Haiti.
