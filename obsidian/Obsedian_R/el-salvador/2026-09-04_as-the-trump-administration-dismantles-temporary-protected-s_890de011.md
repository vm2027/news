## Summary

This article examines how the dismantling of Temporary Protected Status (TPS) in the United States could affect Salvadorans whose protections are set to expire, raising the possibility of forced returns to El Salvador. It highlights concerns from human rights groups about the authoritarian tendencies of President Nayib Bukele’s government and what a large-scale return might mean for security and rights in the country.
