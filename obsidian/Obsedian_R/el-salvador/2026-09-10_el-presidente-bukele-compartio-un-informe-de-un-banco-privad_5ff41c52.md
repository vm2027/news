# El presidente Bukele compartió un informe de un banco privado que destaca las reformas fiscales y el acuerdo entre El Salvador y el FMI

> **Source:** Infobae  
> **Published:** 2026-09-10  
> **URL:** https://www.infobae.com/el-salvador/2026/09/10/el-presidente-bukele-compartio-un-informe-de-un-banco-privado-que-destaca-las-reformas-fiscales-y-el-acuerdo-entre-el-salvador-y-el-fmi/

## Summary

Infobae reports that President Nayib Bukele shared a report from Santander Corporate & Investment Banking praising El Salvador’s recent economic policies. The bank’s analysis highlights the technical agreement with the IMF, fiscal adjustment measures, and a program of structural reforms as key pillars of the country’s economic transformation. The article situates Bukele’s promotion of the report within his broader narrative that his government is modernizing and stabilizing the Salvadoran economy.
