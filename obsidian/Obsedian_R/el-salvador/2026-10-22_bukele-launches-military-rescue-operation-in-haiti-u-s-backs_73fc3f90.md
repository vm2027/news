# Bukele launches military rescue operation in Haiti; U.S. backs effort

> **Source:** UPI  
> **Published:** 2026-10-22  
> **URL:** https://www.upi.com/Top_News/World-News/2026/10/22/latam-haiti-elsalvador-Bukele-military-combat-operation/4161792687722/

## Summary

President Nayib Bukele launched a military operation to rescue four Salvadoran aviation technicians kidnapped by gangs in Haiti. The operation drew U.S. backing, and Bukele warned of a larger intervention if the hostages are not returned safely.
