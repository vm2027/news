# Trump suggests missing Salvadoran personnel in Haiti may have been killed

> **Source:** Anadolu Agency  
> **Published:** 2026-11-01  
> **URL:** https://www.aa.com.tr/en/americas/trump-suggests-missing-salvadoran-personnel-in-haiti-may-have-been-killed/4107806

## Summary

Donald Trump suggested that four Salvadoran aviation technicians missing in Haiti may have been killed after a gang ambush. El Salvador responded by deploying more than 2,000 soldiers to search for them and assist anti-gang operations.
