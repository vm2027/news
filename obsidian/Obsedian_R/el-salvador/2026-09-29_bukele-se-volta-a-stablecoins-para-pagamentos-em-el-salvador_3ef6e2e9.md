# Bukele se volta a stablecoins para pagamentos em El Salvador

> **Source:** Bloomberg  
> **Published:** 2026-09-29  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-29/bukele-se-volta-a-stablecoins-para-pagamentos-em-el-salvador

## Summary

Nayib Bukele's administration is developing a government-backed stablecoin application for payments and remittances. The move represents a significant change from the government's earlier attempt to make Bitcoin a commonly used form of money.
