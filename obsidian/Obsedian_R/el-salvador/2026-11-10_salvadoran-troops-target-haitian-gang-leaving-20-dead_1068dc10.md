# Salvadoran troops target Haitian gang, leaving 20 dead

> **Source:** Al Jazeera  
> **Published:** 2026-11-10  
> **URL:** https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/10/salvadoran-troops-target-haitian-gang-leaving-20-dead

## Summary

Salvadoran troops reportedly joined Haitian police in an operation against the 400 Mawozo gang that left 20 people dead. President Nayib Bukele said the operation destroyed the gang leader’s home and headquarters.
