# Villatoro defende exceção de Bukele e restringe imagens do CECOT

> **Source:** O Cafezinho  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01  
> **URL:** https://www.ocafezinho.com/2026/09/01/villatoro-defende-excecao-de-bukele-e-restringe-imagens-do-cecot/

## Summary

Justice and Public Security Minister Gustavo Villatoro of El Salvador defends Bukele’s ongoing state of exception as a constitutional tool against what he calls a parallel criminal state. Speaking at an event in São Paulo, he also addresses restrictions on images from the CECOT mega-prison, illustrating the government’s hardline security narrative and tensions with human rights groups.
