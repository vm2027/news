# Cox to build $2.9bn energy and water systems in El Salvador

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-10-15  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/news/cox-build-2-9bn-energy-110529147.html

## Summary

El Salvador signed an agreement with Spanish utility Cox for a projected $2.9 billion program covering energy and water infrastructure. The deal was signed by Bukele and Cox executive chairman Enrique Riquelme at the presidential palace.
