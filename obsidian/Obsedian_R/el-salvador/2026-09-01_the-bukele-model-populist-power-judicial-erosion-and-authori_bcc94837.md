# The Bukele Model: Populist Power, Judicial Erosion, and Authoritarian Consolidation in El Salvador

> **Source:** European Center for Populism Studies (ECPS)  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01  
> **URL:** https://www.populismstudies.org/the-bukele-model-populist-power-judicial-erosion-and-authoritarian-consolidation-in-el-salvador/

## Summary

This analytical article examines Nayib Bukele’s governing style as a case of populist rule that has eroded judicial independence and concentrated power in the presidency. It details how the security crackdown and state of exception have produced dramatic declines in homicides while simultaneously undermining checks and balances, civil liberties, and the rule of law. The piece situates El Salvador within global trends of democratic backsliding and warns that Bukele’s popularity and security gains may mask deeper institutional damage that will be harder to reverse.
