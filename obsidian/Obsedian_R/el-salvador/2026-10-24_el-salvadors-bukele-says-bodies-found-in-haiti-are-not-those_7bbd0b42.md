# El Salvador’s Bukele says bodies found in Haiti are not those of missing Salvadorans

> **Source:** Associated Press via Yakima Herald-Republic  
> **Published:** 2026-10-24  
> **URL:** https://www.yakimaherald.com/news/nation_and_world/nation/el-salvador-s-bukele-says-bodies-found-in-haiti-are-not-those-of-missing-salvadorans/article_dec30d26-af57-5e90-b1ae-f12259c8df97.html

## Summary

Bukele denied that human remains delivered to Salvadoran military teams in Haiti belonged to four Salvadorans missing since an Oct. 18 gang attack. More than 100 Salvadoran special forces personnel had been deployed to assist with the rescue.
