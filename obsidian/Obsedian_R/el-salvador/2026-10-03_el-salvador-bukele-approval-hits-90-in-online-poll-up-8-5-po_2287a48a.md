# El Salvador Bukele Approval Hits 90% in Online Poll, Up 8.5 Points in a Year

> **Source:** The Rio Times  
> **Published:** 2026-10-03  
> **URL:** https://www.riotimesonline.com/el-salvador-bukele-approval-90-percent-2026/

## Summary

A TResearch International online survey conducted from September 28 to October 2 placed Bukele’s approval rating at 90%. The survey reported especially strong support for the government’s security performance, while nearly half of respondents identified the economy as the country’s main problem.
