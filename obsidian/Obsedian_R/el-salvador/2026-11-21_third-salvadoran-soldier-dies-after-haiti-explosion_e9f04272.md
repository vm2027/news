# Third Salvadoran soldier dies after Haiti explosion

> **Source:** Jamaica Observer  
> **Published:** 2026-11-21  
> **URL:** https://www.jamaicaobserver.com/2026/11/21/third-salvadoran-soldier-dies-haiti-explosion/

## Summary

A third Salvadoran soldier died from injuries sustained in an explosion while troops handled explosives in Haiti. The deaths are the first reported casualties from Bukele’s intervention, which began as a rescue mission and expanded into operations against gangs.
