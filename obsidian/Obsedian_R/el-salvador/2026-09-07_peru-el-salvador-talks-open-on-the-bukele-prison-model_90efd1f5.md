# Peru El Salvador Talks Open on the Bukele Prison Model

> **Source:** The Rio Times  
> **Published:** 2026-09-07  
> **URL:** https://www.riotimesonline.com/peru-el-salvador-bukele-prison-model-talks-2026/

## Summary

Peru’s Prime Minister Luis Galarreta confirmed that his government has opened talks with El Salvador to study President Nayib Bukele’s security and prison model. The discussions aim to evaluate El Salvador’s gang-crackdown policies, including emergency powers, mass arrests, and the construction of the CECOT mega-prison. El Salvador has not yet publicly responded, and no formal agreement or timeline has been announced.
