# The airport cargo terminal will operate 24/7 starting November 9

> **Source:** Qué Pasa Today  
> **Published:** 2026-11-06  
> **URL:** https://quepasatoday.com/en/story/2026-11-06/airport-cargo-terminal-will-operate-24-7-starting/

## Summary

The article reports that El Salvador’s international airport cargo terminal will begin operating around the clock on November 9. The measure, announced by airport authorities, is intended to improve cargo handling and accommodate increased year-end demand.
