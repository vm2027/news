# El Salvador: Almost 90% of Those Arrested Under the Anti-Gang Regime Continue without Conviction

> **Source:** Ground News  
> **Published:** 2026-10-21  
> **URL:** https://ground.news/article/el-salvador-almost-90-of-those-arrested-under-the-anti-gang-regime-continue-without-conviction_047fdc

## Summary

Almost 90% of the 93,000 people detained under El Salvador’s state of exception reportedly remain without convictions. The report says only slightly more than 10,500 detainees have been convicted, while rights organizations have documented alleged arbitrary arrests, abuses, and deaths in custody.
