# El Salvador’s planned release of some prisoners falls short for many families

> **Source:** The Associated Press  
> **Published:** 2026-10-23  
> **URL:** https://apnews.com/article/elsalvador-bukele-prisoners-release-170906cb24a41d919e11109706e08013

## Summary

President Nayib Bukele’s government is considering releasing some people detained during its mass anti-gang crackdown. Families say the proposed releases do not address the broader issue of relatives they believe were unjustly imprisoned and with whom they have had little or no contact.
