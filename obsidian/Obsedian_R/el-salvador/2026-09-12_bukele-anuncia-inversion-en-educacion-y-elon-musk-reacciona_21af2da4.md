# Bukele anuncia inversión en educación y Elon Musk reacciona

> **Source:** El Diario de El Salvador / elsalvador.com  
> **Published:** 2026-09-12  
> **URL:** https://www.elsalvador.com/dinero-y-negocios/entorno-economico/bukele-educacion-elon-musk/1292946/2026/

## Summary

The piece reports that President Bukele announced El Salvador will allocate **8% of its GDP to education starting in 2027**, a level described by analysts as an almost unprecedented budgetary commitment in the region.[4] It notes that Elon Musk reacted to the announcement with a "Bravo" on X and cites an international document highlighting the planned increase as a major step in the country’s human‑capital strategy.[4]
