# Peru's Fujimori visits notorious Salvadoran prison CECOT

> **Source:** AP News  
> **Published:** 2026-10-30  
> **URL:** https://apnews.com/article/el-salvador-peru-bukele-fujimori-cecot-visit-26346b78e2582c0e9e4430d4ffb628ef

## Summary

Peruvian President Keiko Fujimori visited El Salvador’s CECOT maximum-security prison during a visit focused on security and state modernization. Salvadoran officials presented the facility’s surveillance and gang-isolation protocols, while Fujimori praised Bukele’s security model.
