# Bukele announces that El Salvador will invest 8% of its GDP in education starting in 2027

> **Source:** Dinero.com.sv  
> **Published:** 2026-09-12  
> **URL:** https://dinero.com.sv/en/breaking_news/bukele-announces-that-el-salvador-will-invest-8-of-its-gdp-in-education-starting-in-2027/

## Summary

The article reiterates President Bukele’s pledge that El Salvador will dedicate **8% of GDP to education from 2027 onward**, presenting it as a measure to strengthen and expand the country’s educational efforts.[10] It frames the policy as part of a broader strategy to improve educational quality and infrastructure under Bukele’s administration.[10]
