# El Salvador plans $3 billion education investment, highlighted by Elon Musk

> **Source:** Ground News  
> **Published:** 2026-09-14  
> **URL:** https://ground.news/article/elon-musk-reacts-to-president-bukeles-announcement-about-increased-investment-in-education

## Summary

President Bukele announced a plan for El Salvador to raise education spending to 8% of its economy by 2027, amounting to an estimated $3 billion investment in schools, infrastructure, and learning resources.[13] Elon Musk publicly reacted to and amplified the announcement, drawing international attention to Bukele’s push to reshape the country’s human-capital base through large-scale education spending.[13]
