# Bukele anuncia traslado de restos de Francisco Morazán a nuevo mausoleo

> **Source:** Diario El Mundo  
> **Published:** 2026-09-23  
> **URL:** https://diario.elmundo.sv/nacionales/bukele-anuncia-traslado-de-restos-de-francisco-morazan-a-nuevo-mausoleo

## Summary

Bukele announced that Francisco Morazán’s remains had been transferred to a new mausoleum in the Jardín Centroamérica. The mausoleum, located beside the National Palace in San Salvador’s historic center, was to open to visitors on September 24.
