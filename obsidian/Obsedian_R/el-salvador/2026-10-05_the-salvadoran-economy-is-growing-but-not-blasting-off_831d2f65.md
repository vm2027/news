> **Source:** Tim Muth  
> **Published:** 2026-10-05  
> **URL:** https://timmuth.substack.com/p/the-salvadoran-economy-is-growing

## Summary

The article examines El Salvador's projected 4.5% economic growth in 2026, increased tax receipts, and rising tourism. It also identifies persistent constraints including high public debt, legal insecurity, limited foreign investment, and weak productivity growth.
