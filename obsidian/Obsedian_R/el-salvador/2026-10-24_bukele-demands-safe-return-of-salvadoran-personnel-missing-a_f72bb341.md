# Bukele Demands Safe Return of Salvadoran Personnel Missing After Haiti Ambush

> **Source:** The Haitian Tribune  
> **Published:** 2026-10-24  
> **URL:** https://haitiantribune.com/bukele-demands-safe-return-of-salvadoran-personnel-missing-after-haiti-ambush/

## Summary

Bukele demanded that four Salvadoran military personnel missing after an ambush in Haiti be returned alive and unharmed. He said El Salvador had begun a military operation to bring them home, while the armed forces reported that the men had escaped the attack but remained unaccounted for.
