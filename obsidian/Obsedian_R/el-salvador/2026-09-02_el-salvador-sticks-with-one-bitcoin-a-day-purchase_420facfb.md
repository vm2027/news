## Summary

This crypto-focused report details El Salvador’s ongoing policy of buying one bitcoin every day in 2026 despite muted price action, underscoring Bukele’s steadfast commitment to the bitcoin experiment. It quantifies the country’s holdings and discusses how the strategy fits into broader efforts to brand El Salvador as a global crypto hub following its 2021 decision to make bitcoin legal tender. The article raises questions about fiscal risk, market exposure, and whether the accumulated position has produced tangible benefits for the Salvadorean economy and citizens.
