# Bukele Turns to Stablecoins as Bitcoin Payments Fail to Catch On

> **Source:** Bloomberg Law  
> **Published:** 2026-09-29  
> **URL:** https://news.bloomberglaw.com/business-and-practice/bukele-turns-to-stablecoins-as-bitcoin-payments-fail-to-catch-on

## Summary

El Salvador is developing a government-backed application with Modveon to let users send and hold dollar-backed stablecoins, including for remittances. The initiative marks a shift from the government's earlier effort to promote Bitcoin for everyday payments.
