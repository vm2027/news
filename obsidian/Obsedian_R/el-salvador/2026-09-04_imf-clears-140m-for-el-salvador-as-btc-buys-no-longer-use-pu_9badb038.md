# IMF Clears $140M for El Salvador as BTC Buys No Longer Use Public Funds

> **Source:** CryptoRank  
> **Published:** 2026-09-04  
> **URL:** https://cryptorank.io/news/feed/99e90-imf-clears-140m-for-el-salvador-as-btc-buys-no-longer-use-public-funds

## Summary

This cryptocurrency-focused report explains that the IMF and El Salvador reached a staff-level agreement covering the second and third reviews of a 40‑month Extended Fund Facility, clearing about $140 million pending board approval. It emphasizes that El Salvador’s bitcoin purchases are no longer financed with public funds, highlights stronger-than-expected GDP growth forecasts, and frames the deal as a sign of improving macroeconomic conditions under Bukele.
