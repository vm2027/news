# 233 arrested in environmental crime crackdown in Latin America and Caribbean

> **Source:** INTERPOL  
> **Published:** 2026-10-07  
> **URL:** https://www.interpol.int/en/News-and-Events/News/2026/233-arrested-in-environmental-crime-crackdown-in-Latin-America-and-Caribbean

## Summary

INTERPOL reported that 233 people were arrested during a regional crackdown on environmental crime. The operation included El Salvador along with Colombia, Costa Rica, the Dominican Republic, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico, Nicaragua, and Panama.
