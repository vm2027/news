# El Salvador: Mass Trials a Mockery of Justice

> **Source:** Human Rights Watch  
> **Published:** 2026-10-07  
> **URL:** https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/10/07/el-salvador-mass-trials-a-mockery-of-justice

## Summary

Human Rights Watch reported that Salvadoran courts have begun convicting hundreds of defendants at a time in mass trials. The organization said defendants detained under the state of emergency faced weak evidence and inadequate opportunities to defend themselves.
