# Fondo Monetario Internacional revisará el programa de financiamiento de El Salvador para autorizar próximo desembolso

> **Source:** Infobae  
> **Published:** 2026-09-30  
> **URL:** https://www.infobae.com/el-salvador/2026/09/30/fondo-monetario-internacional-revisara-el-programa-de-financiamiento-de-el-salvador-para-autorizar-proximo-desembolso/

## Summary

The IMF executive board is scheduled to review the second and third evaluations of El Salvador's financing program. The review follows a technical agreement and could authorize the next disbursement, with projected 2026 growth of 4.5% and improved fiscal targets.
