# Nayib Bukele Opens 70 More Schools in El Salvador Education Push

> **Source:** The Tico Times  
> **Published:** 2026-10-12  
> **URL:** https://ground.news/article/president-bukele-delivers-third-phase-of-70-schools-of-the-two-schools-per-day-program-in-acajutla

## Summary

El Salvador inaugurated 70 renovated public schools under President Bukele’s Dos Escuelas por Día program. The latest delivery brings the reported total to 210 completed schools since the initiative began in November 2025.
