# Press Secretariat highlights that Wednesday, November 18 ended with no homicides and credits the government’s security strategy

> **Published:** 2026-11-19

El Salvador’s National Civil Police reported zero homicides on November 18. The presidential press office attributed the result to the government’s security strategy and also reported developments involving public finances and customs administration.
