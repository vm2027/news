# Is Bukele's troop deployment in Haiti just a rescue mission?

> **Source:** RFI  
> **Published:** 2026-10-30  
> **URL:** https://www.rfi.fr/en/international-news/20261030-is-bukele-s-troop-deployment-in-haiti-just-a-rescue-mission

## Summary

More than 2,000 Salvadoran troops have arrived in Haiti, substantially increasing the foreign security presence there. Bukele ordered the deployment after four Salvadoran personnel disappeared in an October 18 ambush.
