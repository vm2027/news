# Bukele Wants to Free Half of El Salvador’s Gang Prisoners — With Strings Attached

> **Source:** Latin Times  
> **Published:** 2026-10-15  
> **URL:** https://www.latintimes.com/bukele-wants-free-half-el-salvadors-gang-prisoners-strings-attached-600206

## Summary

The article examines Bukele’s proposal for conditional early release of approximately half of those jailed for gang collaboration. Those released would remain convicted, retain criminal records, and serve the remainder of their sentences under government supervision.
