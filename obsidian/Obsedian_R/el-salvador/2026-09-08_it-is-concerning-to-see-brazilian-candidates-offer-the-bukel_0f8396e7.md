# 'It Is Concerning to See Brazilian Candidates Offer the Bukele Model as a Solution', Says Journalist Forced to Leave El Salvador

> **Source:** Folha de S.Paulo (English edition)  
> **Published:** 2026-09-08  
> **URL:** https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/internacional/en/world/2026/09/it-is-concerning-to-see-brazilian-candidates-offer-the-bukele-model-as-a-solution-says-journalist-forced-to-leave-el-salvador.shtml

## Summary

A Salvadoran journalist who went into exile warns Brazilian politicians against adopting Nayib Bukele’s security model, arguing that the apparent success in reducing crime hides severe human rights violations and authoritarian practices. He describes how the Salvadoran government has used emergency measures and a powerful propaganda apparatus to silence critics and erode democratic safeguards, presenting the Bukele model as a cautionary example rather than a solution.
