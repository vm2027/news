# Trump has ended TPS for 13 countries. Salvadorans are still waiting for an answer.

> **Source:** Politico  
> **Published:** 2026-09-16  
> **URL:** https://www.politico.com/news/2026/09/16/elsalvador-tps-immigration-status-uncertainty-01080983

## Summary

Politico examines the uncertainty facing Salvadorans in the United States as the Trump administration delays a decision on extending or terminating Temporary Protected Status (TPS). The article notes the economic importance of remittances for El Salvador’s economy, equivalent to about a quarter of its GDP, and discusses how a TPS termination could strain relations with President Bukele, a close ally who has lobbied Washington to maintain protections.
