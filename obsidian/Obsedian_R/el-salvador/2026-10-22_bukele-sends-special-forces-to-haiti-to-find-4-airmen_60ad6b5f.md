# Bukele Sends Special Forces to Haiti to Find 4 Airmen

> **Source:** Sociedad Media  
> **Published:** 2026-10-22  
> **URL:** https://www.sociedadmedia.com/el-salvador-special-forces-haiti-four-missing-technicians-bukele/

## Summary

El Salvador reportedly sent 100 special forces personnel to Haiti to search for four aviation technicians who disappeared after an attack on their armored vehicle. A second group of roughly 100 soldiers was expected to follow.
