# Asamblea Legislativa declara emergencia nacional por la sequía en El Salvador

> **Source:** Diario El Mundo  
> **Published:** 2026-10-07  
> **URL:** https://diario.elmundo.sv/politica/asamblea-legislativa-declara-emergencia-nacional-por-la-sequia-en-el-salvador

## Summary

The Salvadoran legislature approved a six-month national emergency over severe drought conditions associated with El Niño. The decree, passed with 59 votes, authorizes government institutions to administer funds and distribute aid to affected communities.
