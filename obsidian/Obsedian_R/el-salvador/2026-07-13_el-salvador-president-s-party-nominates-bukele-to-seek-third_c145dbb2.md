# El Salvador president's party nominates Bukele to seek third term

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-07-13

## Summary

Bukele’s ruling party nominated him to seek a third term in the 2027 election after constitutional changes enabled indefinite re-election. The move followed legal reforms that removed previous limits.
