# Bukele Deploys Troops to Haiti: Experts Warn of Risks Amid Gang War Expansion

> **Source:** Policy Wire  
> **Published:** 2026-11-09  
> **URL:** https://policy-wire.com/bukele-deploys-troops-to-haiti-experts-warn-of-risks-amid-gang-war-expansion/

## Summary

The article reports that President Nayib Bukele dispatched more than 2,000 Salvadoran soldiers and special forces to Haiti after four Salvadoran citizens were abducted. Experts warn that applying El Salvador’s aggressive anti-gang strategy in Haiti could lead to prolonged and dangerous urban warfare.
