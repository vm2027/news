# El Salvador's Bukele announces plan to free up to half of prisoners detained under state of emergency

> **Source:** Associated Press  
> **Published:** 2026-10-15  
> **URL:** https://www.yahoo.com/news/world/articles/el-salvadors-bukele-announces-plan-070159724.html

## Summary

Bukele announced a plan that could permit the conditional early release of up to half of those detained during the government's anti-gang crackdown. Individual cases would be reviewed, and released people could remain under monitoring.
