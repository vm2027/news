# Bukele says military operation launched to rescue Salvadorans held by gangs in Haiti

> **Source:** Georgia Public Broadcasting  
> **Published:** 2026-10-22  
> **URL:** https://www.gpb.org/news/2026/10/22/bukele-says-military-operation-launched-rescue-salvadorans-held-by-gangs-in-haiti

## Summary

Bukele said El Salvador had begun an operation to rescue four aviation technicians missing after gangs attacked an armored vehicle near Port-au-Prince. He demanded that the men be released alive and unharmed and threatened a stronger response.
