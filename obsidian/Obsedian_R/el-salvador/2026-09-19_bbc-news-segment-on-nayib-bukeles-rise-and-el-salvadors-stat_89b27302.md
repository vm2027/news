## Summary

This BBC News broadcast segment recounts Nayib Bukele’s political trajectory from left‑wing mayor to president and his consolidation of control over El Salvador’s Congress and judiciary. It explains how Bukele used a prolonged state of exception to give security forces broad powers, resulting in the world’s highest incarceration rate and a dramatic shift in the country’s security and political landscape.
