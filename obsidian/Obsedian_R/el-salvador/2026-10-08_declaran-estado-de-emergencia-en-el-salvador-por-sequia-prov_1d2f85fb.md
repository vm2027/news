# Declaran estado de emergencia en El Salvador por sequía provocada por fenómeno de El Niño

> **Source:** Associated Press  
> **Published:** 2026-10-08  
> **URL:** https://apnews.com/article/el-salvador-emergencia-clima-sequia-el-nino-30064b8b08cb73728d1c332af35e8f9a

## Summary

El Salvador’s legislature declared a renewable six-month national emergency because of drought attributed to the El Niño phenomenon. Opposition lawmaker Claudia Ortiz was the only legislator to vote against the measure and blamed the government for worsening conditions.
