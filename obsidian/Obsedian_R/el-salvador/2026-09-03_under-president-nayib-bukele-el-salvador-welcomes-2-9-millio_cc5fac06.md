# Under President Nayib Bukele, El Salvador Welcomes 2.9 Million International Visitors So Far in 2026

> **Source:** Ground News  
> **Published:** 2026-09-03  
> **URL:** https://ground.news/article/under-president-nayib-bukele-el-salvador-welcomes-29-million-international-visitors-so-far-in-2026_71f805

## Summary

This article reports that El Salvador has received 2.9 million international visitors so far in 2026 under President Bukele, highlighting tourism as a key driver of economic performance. It cites analysis from British firm EMFI, which upgrades its growth forecast for El Salvador to 3.9% for 2026, attributing the outlook partly to improved security and increased investor confidence in Bukele’s policies.
