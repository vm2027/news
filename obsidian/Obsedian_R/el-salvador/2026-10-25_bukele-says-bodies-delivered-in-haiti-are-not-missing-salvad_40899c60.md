# Bukele says bodies delivered in Haiti are not missing Salvadoran personnel

> **Source:** True News Line  
> **Published:** 2026-10-25  
> **URL:** https://truenewsline.com/article/el-salvador-president-says-bodies-found-in-haiti-are-not-those-of-hostages/

## Summary

Bukele rejected claims that four bodies delivered to Salvadoran forces in Haiti belonged to the missing aviation technicians. He said the personnel were primarily assigned to humanitarian duties, including medical evacuations, rather than combat operations.
