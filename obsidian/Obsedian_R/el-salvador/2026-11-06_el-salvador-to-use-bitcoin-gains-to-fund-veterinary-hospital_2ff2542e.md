# El Salvador to use bitcoin gains to fund veterinary hospital

> **Source:** The Daily Star  
> **Published:** 2026-11-06  
> **URL:** https://www.thedailystar.net/news/world/usa/news/el-salvador-use-bitcoin-gains-fund-veterinary-hospital-2195356

## Summary

President Nayib Bukele said El Salvador will use $4 million in gains from its bitcoin operations to build a veterinary hospital. The facility is expected to provide basic and emergency care as well as rehabilitation services.
