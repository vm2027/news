# Press Secretariat: Highlighted another day without homicides and credited it to President Bukele's security work

> **Source:** Qué Pasa Today  
> **Published:** 2026-10-29  
> **URL:** https://quepasatoday.com/en/official/2026-10-29/secprensasv-2115693693839761790/

## Summary

El Salvador's National Civil Police reported no homicides on October 28. The presidential press office attributed the result to Bukele's security policies and said it was the seventh consecutive day with the official report.
