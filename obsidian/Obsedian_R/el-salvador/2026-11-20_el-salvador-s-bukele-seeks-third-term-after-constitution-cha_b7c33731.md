# El Salvador's Bukele seeks third term after constitution change

> **Source:** Yahoo News  
> **Published:** 2026-11-20  
> **URL:** https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/el-salvador-bukele-seeks-third-082134380.html

## Summary

Bukele registered for the February presidential election after constitutional changes abolished presidential term limits and extended terms from five to six years. Critics described the move as part of a broader effort to consolidate power and weaken institutional checks.
