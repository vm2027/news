# Ministro Carlos Espá confirma fecha de la reunión entre Keiko Fujimori y Nayib Bukele en El Salvador

> **Published:** 2026-09-28

## Summary

Peru’s foreign minister said the meeting between Fujimori and Bukele is scheduled for October 29 in El Salvador. The talks are expected to address citizen security and transnational criminal organizations.
