# Asamblea decreta estado de emergencia ante sequía generada por El Niño en El Salvador

> **Source:** Diario1

> **URL:** https://diario1.com/politica/2026/10/asamblea-decreta-estado-de-emergencia-ante-sequia-generada-por-el-nino-en-el-salvador/

## Summary

The article reported that lawmakers declared a national emergency after drought endangered staple-grain production. It said the measure will last six months and noted that August 2026 was the driest August recorded in El Salvador since official records began in 1971.
