# Bukele’s Third Term? El Salvador’s Gamble on Continuity Requires Some Important Conditions

> **Source:** PJ Media (via Facebook post linking article)  
> **Published:** 2026-09-02  
> **URL:** https://www.facebook.com/PJMedia/posts/bukeles-third-term-el-salvadors-gamble-on-continuity-requires-some-important-con/1470897898407905/

## Summary

This commentary explores the prospect of Nayib Bukele seeking a third presidential term and what that would mean for El Salvador’s democracy and security policy. It argues that while the government’s heavy-handed crackdown has sharply reduced crime and remains popular, economic weaknesses and the erosion of institutional checks could undermine long-term stability. The piece frames a third term as a high-stakes bet on continuity, suggesting that without economic improvements and stronger institutions, the security gains may prove fragile.
