## Summary

The article reports that a specialized organized crime court in San Salvador sentenced 15 members of the Barrio 18 Revolucionarios gang to up to 114 years in prison for murder, attempted murder, robbery, and illicit association.[14] It situates the ruling within Bukele’s broader crackdown on gangs and highlights the severe sentences as part of El Salvador’s hard-line justice and security policies.[14]
