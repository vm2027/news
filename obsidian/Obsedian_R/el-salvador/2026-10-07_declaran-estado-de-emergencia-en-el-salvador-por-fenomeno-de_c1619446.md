# Declaran estado de emergencia en El Salvador por fenómeno de El Niño

> **Source:** La Prensa Gráfica  
> **Published:** 2026-10-07  
> **URL:** https://www.laprensagrafica.com/elsalvador/asamblea-declara-estado-de-emergencia-a-nivel-nacional-por-fenomeno-el-nino-20261007-0030.html

## Summary

The Legislative Assembly approved a national state of emergency in response to drought caused by El Niño. The declaration is valid for six months and can be extended if requested by the president and approved by the Assembly.
