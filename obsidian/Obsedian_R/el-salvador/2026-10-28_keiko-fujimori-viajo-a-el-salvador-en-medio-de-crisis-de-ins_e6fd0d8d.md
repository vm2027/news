## Summary

Peruvian President Keiko Fujimori traveled to El Salvador to meet Nayib Bukele and discuss citizen security and transnational organized crime. The article also examines criticism of El Salvador's state of exception and Bukele's proposal to consider conditional early release for some prisoners.
