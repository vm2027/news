# Bukele anunció que liberaría a algunos detenidos. Familiares de estos presos plantean reparos sobre el plan

> **Source:** CNN en Español  
> **Published:** 2026-10-19  
> **URL:** https://kvia.com/news/noticias/cnn-spanish/2026/10/19/familiares-de-detenidos-piden-claridad-sobre-el-plan-de-bukele-de-liberar-condicionalmente-a-acusados-de-ayudar-a-pandillas/

## Summary

Families and human-rights groups expressed concern about President Nayib Bukele’s proposed early-release plan for some detainees held under the state of exception. Bukele said the plan would target people accused of limited or occasional cooperation with gangs rather than active gang members.
