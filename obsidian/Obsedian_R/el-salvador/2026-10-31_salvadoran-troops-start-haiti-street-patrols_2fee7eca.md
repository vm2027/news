# Salvadoran Troops Start Haiti Street Patrols

> **Source:** The Rio Times  
> **Published:** 2026-10-31  
> **URL:** https://www.riotimesonline.com/haiti-salvadoran-soldiers-patrol-port-au-prince/

## Summary

Salvadoran soldiers began street patrols in Port-au-Prince nearly two weeks after four Salvadoran aviation technicians went missing. The deployment reflects Bukele’s effort to apply El Salvador’s anti-gang security approach abroad.
