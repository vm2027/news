# AES CAESS upgrades power grid serving over 70000 users in San Salvador

> **Source:** Dinero.com.sv  
> **Published:** 2026-10-06  
> **URL:** https://dinero.com.sv/en/business/aes-caess-upgrades-power-grid-serving-over-70000-users-in-san-salvador/

## Summary

AES CAESS replaced 8.8 kilometers of bare electrical conductor with semi-insulated cable in San Salvador’s metropolitan area. The infrastructure project is intended to improve operating conditions and benefit more than 70,000 users, including businesses in the capital’s financial district.
