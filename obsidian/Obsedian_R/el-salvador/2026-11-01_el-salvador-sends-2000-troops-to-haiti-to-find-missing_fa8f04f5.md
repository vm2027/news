# El Salvador sends 2,000 troops to Haiti to find missing

> **Source:** Latin American News  
> **Published:** 2026-11-01  
> **URL:** https://latin-american.news/el-salvador-sends-2000-troops-to-haiti-to-find-missing-soldiers/

## Summary

El Salvador sent more than 2,000 soldiers to Haiti to recover four Air Force technicians who disappeared after an ambush. The personnel had been serving with a UN-backed anti-gang mission.
