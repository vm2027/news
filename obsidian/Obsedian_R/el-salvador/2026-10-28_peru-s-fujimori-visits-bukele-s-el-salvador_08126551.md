# Peru's Fujimori Visits Bukele's El Salvador

> **Source:** The Rio Times  
> **Published:** 2026-10-28  
> **URL:** https://www.riotimesonline.com/peru-fujimori-visits-bukele-el-salvador-2026/

## Summary

Peruvian President Keiko Fujimori is scheduled to visit El Salvador and meet President Nayib Bukele. Her agenda includes meetings with ministers and visits to CECOT, a school, and Rosales Hospital.
