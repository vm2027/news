# El Salvador rushes some 1,000 troops to Haiti after gang ambush, source says

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-10-27  
> **URL:** https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/around-1000-soldiers-el-salvador-have-deployed-haiti-source-says-2026-10-27/

## Summary

El Salvador has deployed about 1,000 troops to Haiti after four Salvadoran members of a UN-backed security mission went missing in a gang ambush near Port-au-Prince. President Nayib Bukele announced a military operation to recover the missing personnel.
