# Scoop: Trump blesses El Salvador strongman's troop surge in Haiti

> **Source:** Axios  
> **Published:** 2026-10-30  
> **URL:** https://www.axios.com/2026/10/30/trump-bukele-haiti-troop-surge

## Summary

President Nayib Bukele is deploying thousands of Salvadoran troops to Haiti with the approval of Haiti’s government and apparent backing from President Donald Trump. The mission aims to combat gangs and locate four Salvadoran military personnel missing after an ambush.
