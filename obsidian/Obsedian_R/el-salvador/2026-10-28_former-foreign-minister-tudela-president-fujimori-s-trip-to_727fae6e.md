## Summary

Former Peruvian Foreign Minister Francisco Tudela described Keiko Fujimori's trip to El Salvador as a useful opportunity to study the country's approach to organized crime. The visit includes meetings with Bukele and Salvadoran officials, as well as a tour of CECOT.
