# Bukele Shares Footage of Rifle-Equipped Robotic Dogs in Haiti Stadium Operation

> **Source:** SSBCrack News  
> **Published:** 2026-11-15  
> **URL:** https://news.ssbcrack.com/bukele-shares-footage-of-rifle-equipped-robotic-dogs-in-haiti-stadium-operation/

## Summary

President Nayib Bukele shared footage showing rifle-equipped robotic dogs during an operation to retake Sylvio Cator Stadium in Port-au-Prince. The operation involved Haitian and Salvadoran security forces, and Bukele said El Salvador planned to establish an operational base at the stadium.
