# El Salvador: Asamblea Legislativa aprueba que el aguinaldo pueda pagarse desde el 1 de octubre

> **Source:** Infobae  
> **Published:** 2026-09-23  
> **URL:** https://www.infobae.com/el-salvador/2026/09/23/el-salvador-asamblea-legislativa-aprueba-que-el-aguinaldo-pueda-pagarse-desde-el-1-de-octubre/

## Summary

El Salvador’s Legislative Assembly approved reforms allowing employers to pay year-end bonuses beginning October 1. Bonuses and additional cash compensation of up to $1,500 were also made exempt from income-tax withholding.
