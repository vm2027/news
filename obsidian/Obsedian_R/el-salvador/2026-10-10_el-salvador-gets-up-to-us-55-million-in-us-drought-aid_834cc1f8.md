# El Salvador Gets Up to US$55 Million in US Drought Aid

> **Source:** The Rio Times  
> **Published:** 2026-10-10  
> **URL:** https://www.riotimesonline.com/el-salvador-us-drought-aid-2026/

## Summary

The United States is providing up to $55 million in drought assistance to El Salvador, according to the U.S. embassy in San Salvador. The report places the aid alongside the government’s six-month emergency declaration approved by the Legislative Assembly.
