# El Salvador Zero Homicides: October Opens Without Killings

> **Source:** The Rio Times  
> **Published:** 2026-10-04  
> **URL:** https://www.riotimesonline.com/el-salvador-zero-homicides-october-streak-2026/

## Summary

El Salvador recorded no homicides during each of the first three days of October, according to police figures reported by local media. The result extends the security gains the Bukele government attributes to its state of exception and anti-gang campaign.
