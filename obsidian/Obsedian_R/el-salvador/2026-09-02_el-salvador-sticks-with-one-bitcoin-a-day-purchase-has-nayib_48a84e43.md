# El Salvador Sticks With ‘One Bitcoin a Day’ Purchase – Has Nayib Bukele-Led Nation’s Crypto Bet Paid Off?

> **Source:** Benzinga via TradingView News  
> **Published:** 2026-09-02  
> **URL:** https://www.tradingview.com/news/benzinga:836e82b86094b:0-el-salvador-sticks-with-one-bitcoin-a-day-purchase-has-nayib-bukele-led-nation-s-crypto-bet-paid-off/

## Summary

The article reports that El Salvador, under President Nayib Bukele, has continued its policy of buying one bitcoin every day through 2026, bringing national holdings to roughly 7,762 BTC valued at over $600 million at current prices. It evaluates whether the controversial decision to adopt bitcoin as legal tender and accumulate large reserves has benefited the country, considering price volatility, international criticism, and potential gains on earlier purchases. The piece frames the strategy as a long-term speculative bet on digital assets that has yet to clearly translate into broad economic improvements.
