# El Salvador President Says Bodies Found in Haiti Do Not Belong to Ambushed Soldiers

> **Source:** Reuters via U.S. News  
> **Published:** 2026-10-24  
> **URL:** https://www.usnews.com/news/world/articles/2026-10-24/bodies-of-ambushed-salvadoran-soldiers-found-in-haiti-new-york-times-reports

## Summary

President Nayib Bukele said four bodies handed over to Salvadoran forces in Haiti were not the Salvadoran soldiers missing after an ambush near Port-au-Prince. The statement contradicted an earlier report that the remains were believed to belong to the missing troops.
