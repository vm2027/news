# El Salvador. Es un «Estado policial»

> **Source:** Resumen Latinoamericano  
> **Published:** 2026-09-11  
> **URL:** https://www.resumenlatinoamericano.org/2026/09/11/el-salvador-es-un-estado-policial/

## Summary

This article argues that El Salvador has become a police state under President Nayib Bukele. It describes how Bukele has consolidated control over the state by imposing a loyal attorney general, reshaping the constitutional court, and changing the electoral system in ways that make it harder for opposition parties to win seats. The piece frames Bukele’s security policies and institutional changes as eliminating checks and balances and deepening authoritarianism.
