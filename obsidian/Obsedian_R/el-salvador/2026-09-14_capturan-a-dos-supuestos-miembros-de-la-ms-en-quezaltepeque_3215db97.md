# Capturan a dos supuestos miembros de la MS en Quezaltepeque

> **Source:** El Urbano  
> **Published:** 2026-09-14  
> **URL:** https://elurbano.news/nacional/capturan-a-dos-supuestos-miembros-de-la-ms-en-quezaltepeque/

## Summary

El Urbano reports that the Armed Forces detained two alleged members of the MS-13 gang during a patrol in Quezaltepeque, La Libertad Norte, identifying them by their gang aliases and criminal records.[9] The men, with past charges including homicide, robbery, and extortion, were handed over to the National Civil Police, illustrating the continued militarized enforcement of Bukele’s anti-gang policies at the local level.[9]
