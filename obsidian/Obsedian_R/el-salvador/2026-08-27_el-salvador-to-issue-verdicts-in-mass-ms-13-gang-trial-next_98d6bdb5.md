# El Salvador to issue verdicts in mass MS-13 gang trial next week

> **Source:** Free Malaysia Today  
> **Published:** 2026-08-27  
> **URL:** https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/world/2026/08/27/el-salvador-to-issue-verdicts-in-mass-ms-13-gang-trial-next-week

## Summary

The article reports that a court in El Salvador is set to deliver verdicts next week in a mass trial of alleged MS-13 gang leaders, a process strongly backed by President Nayib Bukele. It explains that the defendants are accused of a wide range of crimes and that the trial is part of Bukele’s sweeping anti-gang crackdown conducted under a prolonged state of emergency. The piece underscores the security and political implications of the verdicts, as well as human-rights concerns over mass prosecutions.
