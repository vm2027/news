# Nayib Bukele Leads Ranking of Most Popular Central American Leaders

> **Source:** Ground News  
> **Published:** 2026-09-18  
> **URL:** https://ground.news/article/nayib-bukele-leads-ranking-of-most-popular-central-american-leaders

## Summary

This article covers a CB Global Data survey showing Nayib Bukele as the most popular president in Central America, with an approval rating of 66.2%.[13] It presents comparative ratings for other regional leaders and notes that Salvadoran outlet Diario La Página first reported the new ranking, underscoring Bukele’s enduring domestic support despite international criticism of his security and governance model.[13]
