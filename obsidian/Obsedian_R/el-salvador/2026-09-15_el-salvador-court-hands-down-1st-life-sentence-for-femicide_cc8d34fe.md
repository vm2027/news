# El Salvador court hands down 1st life sentence for femicide

> **Source:** DW  
> **Published:** 2026-09-15  
> **URL:** https://www.dw.com/en/el-salvador-court-hands-down-first-life-sentence-for-femicide/a-79268746

## Summary

A court in El Salvador has imposed the country’s first **life sentence** for aggravated femicide on a 39‑year‑old man, following recent legal reforms promoted by President Nayib Bukele that allow life imprisonment for severe crimes such as homicide, rape, femicide and terrorism. The ruling highlights the government’s tough-on-crime agenda and the expanded punitive powers of the justice system under Bukele’s security-focused political project.
