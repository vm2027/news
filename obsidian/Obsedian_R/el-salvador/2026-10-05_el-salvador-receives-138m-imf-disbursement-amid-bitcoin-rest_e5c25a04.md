# El Salvador Receives $138M IMF Disbursement Amid Bitcoin Restrictions

> **Source:** KuCoin  
> **Published:** 2026-10-05  
> **URL:** https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/el-salvador-receives-138m-imf-disbursement-amid-bitcoin-restrictions

## Summary

El Salvador received a $138 million IMF disbursement following the second and third reviews of its $1.4 billion loan program. The payment came amid restrictions and conditions related to the country’s Bitcoin policy.
