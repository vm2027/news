# El Salvador continues to deploy soldiers, equipment to Haiti as gangs threaten violence

> **Source:** The News & Observer  
> **Published:** 2026-10-26  
> **URL:** https://www.newsobserver.com/news/nation-world/world/article317789933.html

## Summary

El Salvador continued deploying soldiers and equipment to Haiti after four Salvadoran personnel went missing following a gang ambush. President Nayib Bukele ordered reinforcements for the Salvadoran contingent involved in efforts to locate them.
