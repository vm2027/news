# IMF Clears El Salvador Bitcoin: Private Donors Never Named or Quantified

> **Source:** TechTimes  
> **Published:** 2026-09-04  
> **URL:** https://www.techtimes.com/articles/326707/20260904/imf-clears-el-salvador-bitcoin-private-donors-never-named-quantified.htm

## Summary

This article examines the IMF’s September 3, 2026 decision clearing El Salvador’s bitcoin policy after authorities claimed all new bitcoin reserves since June 2025 came from private donations. It highlights that no donors or donation amounts have been disclosed publicly, even as the agreement paves the way for about $140 million in additional IMF disbursements and cites 4.5% GDP growth driven by investment, remittances, tourism, and improved security.
