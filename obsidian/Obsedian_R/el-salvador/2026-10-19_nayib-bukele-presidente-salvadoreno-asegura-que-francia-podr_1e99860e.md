# Nayib Bukele, presidente salvadoreño, asegura que Francia podría resolver su crisis de seguridad en una semana

> **Source:** Infobae  
> **Published:** 2026-10-19  
> **URL:** https://www.infobae.com/el-salvador/2026/10/19/nayib-bukele-presidente-salvadoreno-asegura-que-francia-podria-resolver-su-crisis-de-seguridad-en-una-semana/

## Summary

Nayib Bukele said France could overcome its security crisis within a week, invoking El Salvador’s crackdown on gangs as a model. His comments followed protests, alleged looting, and attacks on public buildings in several French cities.
