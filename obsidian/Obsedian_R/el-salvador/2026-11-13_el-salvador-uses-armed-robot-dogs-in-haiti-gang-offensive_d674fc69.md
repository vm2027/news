# El Salvador uses armed robot dogs in Haiti gang offensive

> **Source:** Jamaica Observer  
> **Published:** 2026-11-13  
> **URL:** https://www.jamaicaobserver.com/2026/11/13/el-salvador-uses-armed-robot-dogs-haiti-gang-offensive/

## Summary

El Salvador’s military used rifle-equipped robotic dogs during a joint operation to seize Haiti’s Sylvio Cator Stadium from gangs, according to Bukele. The operation followed El Salvador’s deployment of troops and special forces to Haiti after Salvadorans were reportedly kidnapped.
