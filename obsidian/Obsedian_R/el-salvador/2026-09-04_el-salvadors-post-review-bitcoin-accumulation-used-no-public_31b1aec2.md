# El Salvador’s post-review Bitcoin accumulation used no public funds: IMF

> **Source:** LCX  
> **Published:** 2026-09-04  
> **URL:** https://lcx.com/nl/cryptonews/el-salvadors-post-review-bitcoin-accumulation-used-no-public-funds-imf

## Summary

Following a Thursday statement from the IMF, this piece reports that documents from Salvadoran authorities verified that post‑review bitcoin accumulation came entirely from private donations rather than state resources. It situates the clarification within the broader IMF program with El Salvador and underscores the institutional acceptance of the Bukele government’s explanation as a condition for continued financial support.
