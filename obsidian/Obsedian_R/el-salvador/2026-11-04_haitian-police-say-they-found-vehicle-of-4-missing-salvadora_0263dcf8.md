# Haitian police say they found vehicle of 4 missing Salvadoran aviation technicians

> **Source:** AP News  
> **Published:** 2026-11-04  
> **URL:** https://apnews.com/article/haiti-el-salvador-gangs-bukele-0302160e058789506d72c9e2a6c1bfc9

## Summary

Haitian police found the burned remains of a vehicle used by four Salvadoran aviation technicians who disappeared after an attack in gang-controlled territory. The incident prompted Bukele to send more than 2,000 troops to Haiti to assist with the search.
