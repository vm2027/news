## Summary

This alternate publication page for the ECPS analysis on Bukele reiterates the core argument that El Salvador’s dramatic security improvements have come at the cost of judicial independence and liberal-democratic norms. It emphasizes international admiration for Bukele’s results, while detailing the institutional costs in terms of weakened courts, sidelined opposition, and curtailed fundamental rights. Together with the main article, it underscores that the “Bukele model” is increasingly being discussed and potentially emulated abroad despite its authoritarian characteristics.
