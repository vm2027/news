# El Salvador's Bukele makes a political gamble with a Haiti gang offensive ahead of an election

> **Source:** ABC News  
> **Published:** 2026-11-11  
> **URL:** https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/el-salvadors-bukele-makes-political-gamble-haiti-gang-138040031

## Summary

President Nayib Bukele's government has deployed about 2,500 Salvadoran soldiers to Haiti after four Salvadoran aviation technicians disappeared in a gang attack. Analysts say the operation could help Bukele counter international criticism and strengthen domestic political support before February elections.
