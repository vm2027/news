# Bukele denies bombings in Haiti and attacks NGOs

> **Source:** Demócrata  
> **Published:** 2026-11-01  
> **URL:** https://www.democrata.es/en/international/bukele-denies-salvadoran-bombings-in-haiti-and-accuses-ngos-of-lying/

## Summary

Bukele denied that Salvadoran forces carried out alleged bombings or helicopter attacks in Haiti and accused nongovernmental organizations of spreading false claims. More than 2,000 Salvadoran soldiers were deployed in an operation to rescue four missing personnel.
