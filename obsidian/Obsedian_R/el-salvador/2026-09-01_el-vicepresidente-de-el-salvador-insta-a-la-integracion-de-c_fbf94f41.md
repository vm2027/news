# El vicepresidente de El Salvador insta a la integración de Centroamérica en seguridad

> **Source:** Infobae / EFE  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01  
> **URL:** https://www.infobae.com/america/agencias/2026/09/01/el-vicepresidente-de-el-salvador-insta-a-la-integracion-de-centroamerica-en-seguridad/

## Summary

El Salvador’s Vice President Félix Ulloa calls for deeper Central American integration around security policy, citing his country’s sharp reduction in violence as an example to follow. Speaking about transnational organized crime, he argues that regional coordination is essential and promotes the Bukele government’s security model as a reference for neighboring states.
