# Dominican Republic provides logistical support for the deployment of Salvadoran military troops in Haiti

> **Source:** Dominican Today  
> **Published:** 2026-11-01  
> **URL:** https://dominicantoday.com/dominican-republic-provides-logistical-support-for-the-deployment-of-salvadoran-military-troops-in-haiti/

## Summary

The Dominican Republic is serving as a logistics point for El Salvador’s military deployment to Haiti. The operation involves more than 2,000 Salvadoran soldiers sent to locate four technicians missing after a gang ambush.
