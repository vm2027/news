# El Salvador's mass trials violate due process and human rights, Human Rights Watch says

> **Source:** JURIST  
> **Published:** 2026-10-10  
> **URL:** https://www.jurist.org/news/2026/10/el-salvadors-mass-trials-violate-due-process-and-human-rights-human-rights-watch-says/

## Summary

Human Rights Watch criticized El Salvador’s simultaneous mass trials as violations of due process and human rights. The proceedings form part of President Nayib Bukele’s sweeping security campaign against alleged gang members.
