# Legislative Assembly extends state of emergency by 30 days

> **Source:** Dinero.com.sv  
> **Published:** 2026-08-27  
> **URL:** https://dinero.com.sv/en/miscellaneous/legislative-assembly-extends-state-of-emergency-by-30-days/

## Summary

El Salvador’s Legislative Assembly has approved a new 30-day extension of the state of emergency, keeping it in force from August 29 to September 27, 2026. This marks the 54th renewal of the measure since it was first introduced on March 27, 2022 under President Nayib Bukele to confront gang violence. The continuation of the emergency regime reflects the administration’s ongoing reliance on extraordinary security powers that limit civil liberties in the name of public safety.
