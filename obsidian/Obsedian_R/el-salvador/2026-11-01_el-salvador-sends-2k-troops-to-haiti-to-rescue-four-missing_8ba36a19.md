# El Salvador sends 2K troops to Haiti to rescue four missing soldiers

> **Source:** Panay News  
> **Published:** 2026-11-01  
> **URL:** https://www.panaynews.net/el-salvador-sends-2k-troops-to-haiti-to-rescue-four-missing-soldiers/

## Summary

El Salvador deployed more than 2,000 soldiers to Haiti to search for four missing nationals and support security operations against gangs. About 450 soldiers had reportedly arrived in Port-au-Prince as the mission expanded.
