# Foreign Ministry Says Message Attributed to Bukele Against Dominican Republic Is False

> **Source:** Ground News  
> **Published:** 2026-10-31  
> **URL:** https://ground.news/article/foreign-ministry-denied-bukeles-alleged-statements-against-the-dominican-government-global-voices

## Summary

The Dominican foreign ministry denied social-media claims attributing hostile statements about the Dominican Republic and its armed forces to Bukele. Officials said the statements came from a nonexistent account and were not made by the Salvadoran president.
