# Uruguay bets on a 'democratic' strategy to combat crime

> **Source:** CNN  
> **Published:** 2026-09-11  
> **URL:** https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/11/americas/uruguay-crime-bukele-latam-intl

## Summary

This article examines Uruguay’s new security strategy and contrasts it with El Salvador’s hardline "Bukele model" of crime control. It notes that Bukele’s approach is credited by his government with transforming El Salvador from one of the most violent countries in the world to one of the safest, citing a reported homicide rate of 1.3 per 100,000 inhabitants. The piece situates El Salvador’s experience within wider Latin American debates over how to combat organized crime while preserving democratic norms.
