# El Salvador. Presidencia Bukele favoreció con $1.13 millones a empresa de Porfirio Chica

> **Source:** Resumen Latinoamericano  
> **Published:** 2026-09-22  
> **URL:** https://www.resumenlatinoamericano.org/2026/09/22/el-salvador-presidencia-bukele-favorecio-con-1-13-millones-a-empresa-de-porfirio-chica/

## Summary

The report alleges that the Bukele presidency awarded $1.13 million in nonrefundable cooperation to a company linked to Porfirio Chica. It describes Chica as a communications operator who has worked with governments from both the FMLN and Bukele administrations.
