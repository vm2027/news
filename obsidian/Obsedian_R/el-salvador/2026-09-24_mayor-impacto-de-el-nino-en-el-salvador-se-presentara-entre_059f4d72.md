# Mayor impacto de El Niño en El Salvador se presentará entre enero y abril de 2027

> **Source:** Agencia EFE  
> **Published:** 2026-09-24  
> **URL:** https://efe.com/medio-ambiente/2026-09-24/mayor-impacto-el-nino-el-salvador-2027/

## Summary

El Salvador’s Civil Protection authorities warned that El Niño’s strongest effects are expected between January and April 2027. The phenomenon has already contributed to drought, extreme heat, and reduced rainfall in the country.
