# Bukele seeks third term in El Salvador after re-election limits abolished

> **Source:** Proto Thema  
> **Published:** 2026-10-01  
> **URL:** https://en.protothema.gr/2026/10/01/bukele-seeks-third-term-in-el-salvador-after-re-election-limits-abolished/

## Summary

Nayib Bukele is set to seek a third presidential term in elections scheduled for February 28, 2027. The report says a 2025 constitutional reform abolished limits on consecutive terms, extended presidential terms from five to six years, and eliminated the runoff election.
