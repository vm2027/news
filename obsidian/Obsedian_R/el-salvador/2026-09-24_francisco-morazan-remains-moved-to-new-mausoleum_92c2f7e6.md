# Francisco Morazán Remains Moved to New Mausoleum

> **Source:** Ground News  
> **Published:** 2026-09-24  
> **URL:** https://ground.news/article/remains-of-francisco-morazan-are-transferred-to-new-mausoleum-in-the-historic-center-la-noticia-sv

## Summary

The article reports that the remains of Central American leader Francisco Morazán were transferred to a new mausoleum in San Salvador’s Historic Center. President Bukele announced the transfer, and the site was scheduled to open to the public on September 24.
