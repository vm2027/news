> **Published:** 2026-09-24  
> **URL:** https://lapagina.com.sv/politica/presidente-bukele-rinde-homenaje-a-procer-francisco-morazan/

## Summary

Nayib Bukele paid tribute to Francisco Morazán during the transfer of the Central American leader’s remains to a new mausoleum. The ceremony took place at the new Jardín Centroamérica beside El Salvador’s National Palace.
