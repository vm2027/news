# Bukele Denies El Salvador Transferred Its Bitcoin Reserve

> **Source:** Bitcoin.com News  
> **Published:** 2026-09-06  
> **URL:** https://news.bitcoin.com/crypto-news/bukele-denies-el-salvador-transferred-its-bitcoin-reserve/

## Summary

President Nayib Bukele publicly rejected media reports claiming El Salvador had handed control of its strategic bitcoin reserve to a private operator, calling the allegations "totally false." He clarified that only shares of the state-backed Chivo wallet were transferred to private management, not the country's bitcoin holdings, and accused some outlets of misrepresenting IMF documentation. The article situates Bukele's response within broader scrutiny of El Salvador's bitcoin policy and its impact on public finances and transparency.
