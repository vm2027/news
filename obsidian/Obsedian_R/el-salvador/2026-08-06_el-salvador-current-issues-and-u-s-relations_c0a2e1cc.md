# El Salvador: Current Issues and U.S. Relations

> **Source:** Congressional Research Service  
> **Published:** 2026-08-06  
> **URL:** https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF13012

## Summary

The report summarizes Bukele’s dominance in Salvadoran politics, support for his security agenda, and concerns about authoritarian drift. It also outlines economic weaknesses such as slow growth, poverty, low FDI, and dependence on remittances.
