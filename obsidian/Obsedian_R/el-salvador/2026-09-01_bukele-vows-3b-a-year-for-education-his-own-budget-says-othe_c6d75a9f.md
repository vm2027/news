# Bukele Vows $3B a Year for Education. His Own Budget Says Otherwise—for Now

> **Source:** Latin Times  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01  
> **URL:** https://www.latintimes.com/bukele-vows-3b-year-education-his-own-budget-says-otherwise-now-598898

## Summary

President Nayib Bukele promised El Salvador would eventually spend $3 billion a year on education, which he framed as the largest education budget in Latin American history and more than 8% of national output. The article compares this pledge to the 2026 enacted budget, which allocates about $1.68 billion to education, and analyzes whether the country’s debt levels, growth forecasts, and remittance flows can realistically support such a dramatic increase. It concludes that while tens of thousands of scholarships are funded and security-driven growth is improving market sentiment, fiscal space remains tight and the $3 billion figure is a long-term political target rather than a near-term reality.
