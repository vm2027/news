# Four Salvadoran technicians vanished after a Haiti ambush. Bukele responded by sending 2,500 troops

> **Source:** Latin Times  
> **Published:** 2026-11-09  
> **URL:** https://www.latintimes.com/salvadoran-technicians-missing-haiti-gang-ambush-601077

## Summary

Four Salvadoran aviation technicians working with the U.N.-backed Gang Suppression Force disappeared after an October ambush in Haiti. Bukele responded by sending more than 2,500 troops, and the technicians' burned vehicle was later found without confirmed evidence of their whereabouts.
