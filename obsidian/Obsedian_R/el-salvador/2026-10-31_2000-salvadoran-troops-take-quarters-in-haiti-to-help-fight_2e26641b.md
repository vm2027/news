# 2,000 Salvadoran troops take quarters in Haiti to help fight gangs

> **Source:** Africanews  
> **Published:** 2026-10-31  
> **URL:** https://www.africanews.com/2026/10/31/2000-salvadoran-troops-take-quarters-in-haiti-to-help-fight-gangs/

## Summary

El Salvador deployed 2,000 troops to Haiti to help fight gangs after four Salvadoran military technicians disappeared following an ambush. The soldiers were seen establishing positions in Port-au-Prince as the search operation continued.
