# El Salvador Jailed More Than 96,000 People for Gang Ties — 4 Years Later, Fewer Than 12% Have Been Convicted

> **Source:** Latin Post  
> **Published:** 2026-10-25  
> **URL:** https://www.latinpost.com/articles/167673/20261025/el-salvador-jailed-more-96000-people-gang-ties-4-years-later-fewer-12-have-been-convicted.htm

## Summary

More than 96,000 people detained under Bukele’s anti-gang emergency measures have been held, while fewer than 12% have received convictions, according to information presented by the attorney general. The Legislative Assembly has repeatedly extended the emergency framework, and the government is considering conditional releases for some detainees.
