# El Salvador's Bukele expected to seek unprecedented third term as leader

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-11-19  
> **URL:** https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/el-salvadors-bukele-expected-seek-unprecedented-third-term-leader-2026-11-19/

## Summary

Nayib Bukele was expected to register his candidacy for an unprecedented third presidential term. His Nuevas Ideas party has removed term limits, while critics warn the change could further weaken institutional checks; Bukele remains the clear favorite after his security crackdown.
