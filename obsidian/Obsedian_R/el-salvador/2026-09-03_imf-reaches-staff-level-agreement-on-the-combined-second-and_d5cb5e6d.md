# IMF Reaches Staff-Level Agreement on the Combined Second and Third Reviews under El Salvador’s Extended Fund Facility Arrangement

> **Source:** International Monetary Fund  
> **Published:** 2026-09-03  
> **URL:** https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2026/09/03/pr26285-el-salvador-imf-reaches-sla-on-combined-2nd-and-3rd-rev-under-eff-arrangement

## Summary

The IMF’s official release announces a staff-level agreement with El Salvador on combined second and third reviews of its Extended Fund Facility arrangement. The statement outlines the size of the facility, the amount that could be disbursed following board approval, and notes that reforms, improved security, and investor confidence are supporting better-than-anticipated economic performance.
