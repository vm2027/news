## Summary

El Salvador’s government is expanding international security cooperation by sharing its crime-fighting strategy with Chilean authorities, with officials describing the approach as the “Bukele Model.” The article details meetings between Salvadoran and Chilean ministers focused on combating organized crime, improving public safety, and the growing foreign interest in El Salvador’s tough security policies.
