# Ministro de Bukele chama organizações de direitos humanos de 'hipócritas'

> **Source:** UOL Notícias  
> **Published:** 2026-08-31  
> **URL:** https://noticias.uol.com.br/internacional/ultimas-noticias/2026/08/31/ministro-bukele-crime-el-salvador.ghtm

## Summary

Gustavo Villatoro, El Salvador's security minister, accused human rights organizations of being "hypocrites" when criticizing Bukele’s anti-gang campaign, arguing that the government is confronting dangerous criminals. The piece also recalls investigative reports alleging Bukele previously made secret pacts with gangs to reduce homicides, raising questions about the evolution and consistency of his security strategy.
