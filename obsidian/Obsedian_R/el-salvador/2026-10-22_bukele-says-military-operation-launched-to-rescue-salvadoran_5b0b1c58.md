> **Source:** NPR  
> **Published:** 2026-10-22  
> **URL:** https://www.npr.org/2026/10/22/nx-s1-6012029/bukele-military-operation-haiti

## Summary

Bukele announced an operation to rescue four Salvadoran aviation technicians allegedly kidnapped by gangs in Haiti. He threatened a more forceful response if the men were not released alive and unharmed.
