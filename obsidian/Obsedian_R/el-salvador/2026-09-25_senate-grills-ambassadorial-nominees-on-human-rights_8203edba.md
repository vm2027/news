# Senate Grills Ambassadorial Nominees on Human Rights

> **Source:** Legis1  
> **Published:** 2026-09-25  
> **URL:** https://legis1.com/news/el-salvador-ambassador-human-rights-senate-grills

## Summary

A U.S. Senate committee questioned ambassadorial nominees about human-rights conditions at El Salvador’s CECOT prison and the Bukele government’s security policies. Senators raised allegations involving repression, prison conditions, and possible cooperation with MS-13, while nominee Mark Abreu denied claims of abuses at the facility.
