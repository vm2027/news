# ¿Bukele contra Bukele? El líder salvadoreño propone un giro en su política de mano dura

> **Source:** El Confidencial  
> **Published:** 2026-10-19  
> **URL:** https://www.elconfidencial.com/mundo/2026-10-19/bukele-giro-politics-mano-dura-por-que-ahora_4446957/

## Summary

Bukele’s government is considering conditional, supervised release for a large number of people detained during the state of exception. The proposed shift could affect tens of thousands of detainees, although Bukele said the emergency regime itself would remain in place.
