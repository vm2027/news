> **Source:** Latin America Bureau

## Summary

The article examines President Nayib Bukele’s comments about potentially granting conditional early release to roughly half of those imprisoned for allegedly collaborating with gangs. It argues that the proposal exposes contradictions in El Salvador’s prolonged state of exception and Bukele’s concentration of judicial power.
