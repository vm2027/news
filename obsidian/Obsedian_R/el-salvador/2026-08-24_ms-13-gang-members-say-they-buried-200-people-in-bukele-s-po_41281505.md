# MS-13 gang members say they buried 200 people in Bukele's political cradle

> **Published:** 2026-08-24  
> **URL:** https://english.elpais.com/international/2026-08-24/ms-13-gang-members-say-they-buried-200-people-in-bukeles-political-cradle.html

## Summary

Three MS-13 members testified under a cooperation agreement that they tortured, murdered, and buried more than 200 people between 2013 and 2021. The case adds fresh scrutiny to gang violence and the crimes prosecuted under Bukele’s security agenda.
