# Turkish foreign minister meets counterparts from Venezuela, El Salvador, Guinea-Bissau in New York

Turkey’s Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan met El Salvador’s Foreign Minister Alexandra Hill in New York. The ministers signed an agreement intended to avoid double taxation between Turkey and El Salvador.
