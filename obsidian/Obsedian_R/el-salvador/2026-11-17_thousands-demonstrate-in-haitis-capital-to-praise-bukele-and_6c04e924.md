# Thousands demonstrate in Haiti’s capital to praise Bukele and Salvadoran troops fighting gangs

> **Source:** WSB-TV  
> **Published:** 2026-11-17  
> **URL:** https://www.wsbtv.com/news/world/thousands/GU7RSCCJZA2A5HB4QRY5LJQBEE/

## Summary

Thousands of Haitians demonstrated in Port-au-Prince in support of Salvadoran President Nayib Bukele and Salvadoran troops deployed to fight gangs in Haiti. Bukele sent roughly 2,500 troops after four Salvadoran personnel went missing in an October attack.
