# El Salvador’s Bukele unveils plan to free up to 50% arrested under state of emergency

> **Source:** World Online  
> **Published:** 2026-10-18  
> **URL:** https://www.wol.com/2026/10/15/el-salvadors-bukele-unveils-plan-to-free-up-to-50-arrested-under-state-of-emergency/

## Summary

President Nayib Bukele announced a plan that could release up to half of those imprisoned under El Salvador’s state of emergency. The proposal would focus on alleged gang collaborators and maintain monitoring while the emergency powers remain in effect.
