## Summary

The article reports on calls for President Nayib Bukele to release human rights lawyer and activist Ruth López, who has been imprisoned without trial for over a year. Her attorney, speaking at the United Nations, argues that Bukele should free her if he wants to show true leadership, highlighting concerns about due process and human rights under his security policies.
