# US greenlights El Salvador's troop surge into Haiti

> **Source:** Yahoo News  
> **Published:** 2026-11-01  
> **URL:** https://www.yahoo.com/news/us/articles/us-greenlights-el-salvadors-troop-222822395.html

## Summary

U.S. President Donald Trump endorsed El Salvador’s decision to send additional troops to Haiti. The deployment, involving as many as 2,000 soldiers, was presented as a response to the disappearance of four Salvadorans serving in the international force.
