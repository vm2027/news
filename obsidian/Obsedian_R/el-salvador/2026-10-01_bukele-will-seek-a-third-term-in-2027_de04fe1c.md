# Bukele will seek a third term in 2027

> **Source:** Demócrata  
> **Published:** 2026-10-01  
> **URL:** https://www.democrata.es/en/international/el-salvador-will-vote-for-president-on-february-28-2027-with-bukele-aspiring-to-a-third-term/

## Summary

El Salvador will hold presidential elections on February 28, 2027, with Nayib Bukele expected to pursue a third consecutive term. Candidates for the presidency, vice presidency, and legislature can register from October 1 through November 19.
