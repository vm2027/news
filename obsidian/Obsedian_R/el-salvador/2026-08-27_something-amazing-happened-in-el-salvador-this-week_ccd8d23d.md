## Summary

This article describes a major new education initiative by the Salvadoran government under President Nayib Bukele, centered on the announcement of 50,000 scholarships for public high school graduates. The program will fund university degrees, technical training, vocational courses, and some opportunities to study abroad, and is framed as part of Bukele’s broader project to transform El Salvador’s social and economic prospects. The piece highlights the political significance of the initiative for Bukele’s domestic image and El Salvador’s development strategy.
