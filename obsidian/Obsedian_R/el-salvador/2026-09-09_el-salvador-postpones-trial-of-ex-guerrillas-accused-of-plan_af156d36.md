# El Salvador Postpones Trial of Ex-Guerrillas Accused of Plan Against Bukele for a Second Time

> **Source:** Ground News  
> **Published:** 2026-09-09  
> **URL:** https://ground.news/article/el-salvador-postpones-trial-of-ex-guerrillas-accused-of-plan-against-bukele-for-a-second-time_d72262

## Summary

El Salvador has postponed for the second time the trial of about a dozen former guerrilla fighters accused of terrorism for allegedly planning an attack on President Nayib Bukele during his second inauguration in 2024. The delay has generated criticism from activists and family members, who see the repeated postponements as possible stalling tactics that raise concerns about due process and political interference in the judiciary.
