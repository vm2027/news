# El Salvador Continues Hunt for Missing Staff in Haiti

> **Source:** GlobalPost  
> **Published:** 2026-10-28  
> **URL:** https://globalpost.com/stories/el-salvador-continues-hunt-for-missing-staff-in-haiti/

## Summary

El Salvador increased its military presence in Haiti while searching for four technicians who disappeared after an armored vehicle ambush. The government said the personnel were part of a UN-backed gang-suppression force and primarily handled medical evacuations and other lifesaving work.
