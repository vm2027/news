# Bukele: El Salvador liberaría a colaboradores de maras con baja participación en delitos

> **Source:** EFE  
> **Published:** 2026-10-14  
> **URL:** https://efe.com/mundo/2026-10-14/nayib-bukele-el-salvador-liberacion-colaboradores-maras-pandillas/

## Summary

Bukele announced that the government is developing a plan for conditional early release of prisoners who collaborated with gangs but had limited participation in crimes. The proposal could affect roughly half of those detained under the emergency regime, though no implementation timetable was provided.
