# El Salvador-backed air assault destroys Haiti gang headquarters amid search for missing troops

> **Source:** Blink News  
> **Published:** 2026-11-12  
> **URL:** https://blinknews.org/news/el-salvador-backed-air-assault-destroys-haiti-gang-headquarters-amid-search-for-missing-troops

## Summary

An air assault targeted 400 Mawozo positions near Port-au-Prince, including the residence of gang leader Lanmò San Jou. The operation was carried out by Haitian police with Salvadoran support amid efforts to locate four missing Salvadoran personnel.
