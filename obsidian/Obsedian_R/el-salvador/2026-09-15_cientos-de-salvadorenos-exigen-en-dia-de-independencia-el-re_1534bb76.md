# Cientos de salvadoreños exigen en día de independencia el respeto a los derechos humanos

> **Source:** Infobae (EFE)  
> **Published:** 2026-09-15  
> **URL:** https://www.infobae.com/america/agencias/2026/09/15/cientos-de-salvadorenos-exigen-en-dia-de-independencia-el-respeto-a-los-derechos-humanos/?outputType=amp-type

## Summary

On El Salvador’s Independence Day, hundreds of Salvadorans marched through San Salvador demanding respect for human rights and denouncing abuses under President Bukele’s emergency regime, in place since March 2022.[12] The piece details mass detentions exceeding 92,000 people, acknowledged wrongful arrests of at least 8,000 individuals, and human‑rights groups’ reports of thousands of complaints and over 500 deaths in state custody, many showing signs of violence.[12]
