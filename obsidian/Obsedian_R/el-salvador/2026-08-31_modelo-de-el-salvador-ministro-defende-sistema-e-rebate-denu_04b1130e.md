# Modelo de El Salvador: Ministro defende sistema e rebate denúncias de violações de direitos humanos

> **Source:** G1 (Globo)  
> **Published:** 2026-08-31  
> **URL:** https://g1.globo.com/sp/sao-paulo/eleicoes/2026/noticia/2026/08/31/ministro-seguranca-publica-justica-el-salvador.ghtml

## Summary

During a presentation in Brazil, El Salvador’s Security and Justice Minister Gustavo Villatoro defended Bukele’s security model against accusations of human rights abuses, calling them "manipulation." The report explains how the emergency regime has been renewed more than 50 times, leading to over 91,500 arrests—around 1.5% of the population—while homicides have dropped sharply, making the policy both highly praised and heavily criticized.
