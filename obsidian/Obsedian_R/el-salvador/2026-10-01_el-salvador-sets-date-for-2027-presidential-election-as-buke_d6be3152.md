# El Salvador sets date for 2027 presidential election as Bukele seeks third term

> **Source:** Yahoo News  
> **Published:** 2026-10-01  
> **URL:** https://ca.news.yahoo.com/el-salvador-sets-2027-presidential-023245196.html

## Summary

The article reports that El Salvador will hold its next presidential election on February 28, 2027. The date clears the way for President Nayib Bukele to pursue a third term after constitutional reforms removed re-election limits.
