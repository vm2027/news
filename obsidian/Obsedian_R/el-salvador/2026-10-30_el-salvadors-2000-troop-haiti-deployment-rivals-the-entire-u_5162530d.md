# El Salvador’s 2,000-Troop Haiti Deployment Rivals the Entire UN Gang Suppression Force

> **Source:** SOFREP  
> **Published:** 2026-10-30  
> **URL:** https://www.sofx.com/el-salvadors-2000-troop-haiti-deployment-rivals-the-entire-un-gang-suppression-force/

## Summary

El Salvador deployed more than 2,000 soldiers to Haiti to recover four air force technicians missing since an October 18 gang ambush. The force is nearly as large as the UN-backed security mission already operating in Haiti.
