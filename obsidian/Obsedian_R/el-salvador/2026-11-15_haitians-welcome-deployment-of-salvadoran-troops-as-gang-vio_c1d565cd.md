# Haitians welcome deployment of Salvadoran troops as gang violence rages on

> **Source:** Africanews  
> **Published:** 2026-11-15  
> **URL:** https://www.africanews.com/2026/11/15/haitians-welcome-deployment-of-salvadoran-troops-as-gang-violence-rages-on/

## Summary

Haitians welcomed the deployment of Salvadoran troops to assist in the fight against gang violence. Salvadoran forces are guarding Port-au-Prince’s Sylvio Cator Stadium, which Haitian and allied forces recently recovered from gang control, and the site is expected to serve as a base for further operations.
