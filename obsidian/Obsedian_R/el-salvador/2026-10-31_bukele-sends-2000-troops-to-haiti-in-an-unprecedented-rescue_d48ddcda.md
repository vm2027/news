# Bukele Sends 2,000 Troops to Haiti in an Unprecedented Rescue Mission

> **Source:** Colombia One  
> **Published:** 2026-10-31  
> **URL:** https://colombiaone.com/2026/10/31/bukele-haiti/

## Summary

President Nayib Bukele has sent more than 2,000 Salvadoran troops to Haiti. The stated purpose is to find four Salvadoran soldiers kidnapped after serving with an international anti-gang force.
