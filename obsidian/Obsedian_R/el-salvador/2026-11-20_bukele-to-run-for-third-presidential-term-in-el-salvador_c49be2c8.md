# Bukele to run for third presidential term in El Salvador

> **Source:** Justice Info  
> **Published:** 2026-11-20  
> **URL:** https://www.justiceinfo.net/en/168722-bukele-to-run-for-third-presidential-term-in-el-salvador.html

## Summary

Bukele registered as a presidential candidate with the Supreme Electoral Court, according to AFP journalists. El Salvador amended its constitution to abolish presidential term limits, allowing him to seek another term in the February 28 election.
