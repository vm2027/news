# Bukele weighs conditional release for half of gang-linked inmates

> **Source:** UPI  
> **Published:** 2026-10-14  
> **URL:** https://www.upi.com/Top_News/World-News/2026/10/14/latam/7711791993508/

## Summary

President Nayib Bukele said El Salvador is considering conditional early release for about half of the prisoners detained during the country’s state of emergency who allegedly collaborated with gangs. Eligibility would be assessed individually, and those released would remain under monitoring.
