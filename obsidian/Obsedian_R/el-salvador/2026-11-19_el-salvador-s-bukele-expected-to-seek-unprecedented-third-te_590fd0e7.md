# El Salvador's Bukele expected to seek unprecedented third term as leader

> **Source:** Miami Herald  
> **Published:** 2026-11-19  
> **URL:** https://www.miamiherald.com/news/nation-world/world/article318161186.html

## Summary

Bukele registered to seek an unprecedented third presidential term in elections he is widely expected to win. Constitutional changes backed by his ruling party removed term limits, prompting criticism from opposition groups and rights organizations.
