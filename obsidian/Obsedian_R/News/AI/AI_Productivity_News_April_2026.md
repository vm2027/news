## 📈 Key Trends to Know

### Focus Has Shifted from Features to ROI
Buyers and employers are no longer asking "what can AI do?" — they're asking "what is it actually delivering?" The gap between demo-quality results and production-quality results is defining winners and losers in the enterprise market this quarter.

### Human–AI Collaboration Is the Winning Model
The most productive teams in 2026 are those where AI handles repetitive, data-heavy tasks (notes, status reports, scheduling, CRM updates) while humans focus on critical thinking, judgment, and creativity.

### AI Intensifies Work (Not Just Reduces It)
Research from *Harvard Business Review* (Feb 2026) notes that while AI handles more tasks, it simultaneously raises expectations and output volume — potentially increasing cognitive load. Organizations are finding that AI is a multiplier: applying it to broken processes doesn't fix the process.

### Governance Is Now a Core Requirement
As agentic AI embeds into daily operations, organizations are building governance into their operating models — with defined boundaries for autonomous action, human escalation paths, and model validation.
