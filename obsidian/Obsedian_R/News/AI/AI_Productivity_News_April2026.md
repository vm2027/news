## 🤖 The Agentic AI Revolution

The defining trend of 2026: AI agents are moving from labs into production. Unlike traditional AI that responds to prompts, **agentic AI acts like a project manager** — given an objective, it figures out a multi-step path to get there autonomously.

Key stats:
- The autonomous AI market is growing from **$1.5B (2025) → $41.8B (2030)**
- **80% of enterprise apps** expected to embed AI agents by end of 2026
- Organizations deploying agentic systems report an average **ROI of 171%** (US companies: 192%)
- By 2026, roughly **40% of business workflows** won't be managed by humans clicking buttons

**What's being automated:** customer service routing, internal operations, procurement, project management, demand forecasting, HR, finance, and IT.

**Important note:** The highest-performing setups aren't fully autonomous — they escalate exceptions, flag low-confidence decisions, and maintain clear human handoff points.
