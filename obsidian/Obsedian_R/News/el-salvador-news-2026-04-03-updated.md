## 4. Human Rights & Press Freedom

Amid the security crackdown and close U.S. cooperation, civil liberties in El Salvador have continued to deteriorate.

**Key concerns:**
- Human rights defenders and journalists face increasing government repression and harassment.
- Dozens of journalists have fled into exile under the Bukele administration; women journalists report elevated levels of digital violence and gender-based harassment.
- The Legislative Assembly passed a **Foreign Agents Law** requiring any individual or organization receiving foreign funds to register with the Interior Ministry — a measure critics say targets NGOs and press freedom groups.
- Freedom House and Human Rights Watch both flagged El Salvador's removal of institutional checks on executive power as a serious ongoing concern.
