# News Report: Vilchez Lazo of Peru
**Date of Report:** April 3, 2026

---

## Summary of Findings

The search identified **Orlando Vilchez Lazo**, a Peruvian national known as the "Rideshare Rapist" in a high-profile San Francisco, California case. Here is the latest news:

---

## Recent Conviction (2026)

Orlando Vilchez Lazo, 44, was recently **convicted by a jury on 11 felony counts** stemming from four sexual assaults that took place between 2013 and 2018 in San Francisco. The counts include:

- Two counts of kidnapping with intent to commit rape
- Three counts of kidnapping
- Four counts of rape by force/fear
- Two counts of sexual penetration with a foreign object

He is currently **in custody awaiting sentencing** and faces **more than 100 years to life in state prison**. San Francisco District Attorney Brooke Jenkins stated: "Justice has finally been served."

---

## Background

Vilchez Lazo, a Peruvian national who was in the U.S. illegally, was first arrested in **July 2018** after being accused of posing as a Lyft rideshare driver and targeting young women (ages 21–22) leaving bars and nightclubs, driving them to secluded locations to assault them. Lyft terminated him and subsequently strengthened its background check policies. ICE indicated it would seek deportation to Peru upon any release — though sentencing now makes that moot.
