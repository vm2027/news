## JPMorgan says AI boom may explain rising bond yields

Rising long-term bond yields may reflect investor expectations of an AI-driven productivity boom rather than just concerns over inflation and swelling government debt, according to Jacob Manoukian, U.S. head of investment strategy at JPMorgan Private Bank.

"The more positive interpretation of what the bond market is doing … is this idea that the bond market might be sniffing out a little bit of a better productivity cycle from all the AI investment that's taking place," Manoukian told the Reuters Global Markets Forum on Thursday.[reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/bond-market-may-be-pricing-ai-productivity-gains-yields-rise-jpmorgan-private-2026-08-28/)

## A Different Read on Rising Yields

The comments arrive as Treasury yields have surged to multi-year highs, with the 30-year yield recently topping 5.2%. Much of the market debate has centered on federal deficits, a flood of corporate debt, and uncertainty over monetary policy under Federal Reserve Chairman Kevin Warsh, whose Jackson Hole speech on Thursday was widely interpreted as hawkish. Manoukian's thesis offers a competing explanation: that elevated real rates signal the bond market is pricing in structural economic gains from massive AI capital expenditure.axios+3

AI-related debt issuance has topped $220 billion this year, double last year's total, while U.S. corporate bond issuance has reached $1.68 trillion, up nearly 27% from the same period in 2025. "AI-related issuance could be half of U.S. Treasury coupon issuance by the end of the year. So that's a material increase in global debt supply at the longer end of the curve," Manoukian said.[reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/bond-market-may-be-pricing-ai-productivity-gains-yields-rise-jpmorgan-private-2026-08-28/)

## Semiconductors Seen as Opportunity

JPMorgan Private Bank remains bullish on semiconductors despite a correction of more than 20% in the sector. Manoukian noted that the discount between two-year forward and trailing 12-month price-to-sales multiples has widened to 40%–50%, compared with a typical 20%, suggesting investors have already priced in an earnings peak. "We don't think that earnings have yet peaked," he said, arguing that companies realizing forecasted sales "could appreciate materially" if investors maintain current valuation multiples into 2028.investing+1

## Carry Over Duration

The combination of elevated long-end yields and uncertainty is shaping JPMorgan Private Bank's fixed-income positioning. The firm favors carry from shorter-duration credit over outright duration exposure, with Manoukian pointing to bank preferreds in the U.S. and high-yield credit in Europe as attractive opportunities.
