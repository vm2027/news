## Summary

This page from **FINSECA's Foundation** showcases a collection of digital white papers (eBooks) aimed at financial security professionals, covering recruiting, retention, leadership, and industry perception.

---

**Do Well by Doing Good**
This series provides recruiting guides for the financial security profession, offering insights into the rewards and challenges of the career as well as opportunities available within financial services.
- Do Well by Doing Good: Strategies for Recruiting Underrepresented Candidates into the Financial Security Profession
- Do Well by Doing Good: Should I Become an Advisor in the Financial Services Profession?

---

**Shifting Paradigms**
This series focuses on industry perception and talent pipelines, addressing how to attract the next generation and elevate the profession's public image.
- Shifting Paradigms: Attracting Gen Z to the Financial Security Profession
- Shifting Paradigms: Strategies for Elevating the Financial Security Profession

---

**Leading Change Series**
A three-part set for field leaders on navigating change — personally, as a team leader, and through the eyes of advisors facing evolving technology, regulations, and consumer expectations.
- Leading Change: Leading Yourself
- Leading Change: Leading Others
- Leading Change: Through the Advisor's Eyes

---

**Retention Series**
This series tackles advisor retention, covering how to keep new advisors on track, build a culture of passion and purpose, and use succession planning as a long-term retention strategy.
- Focusing New Advisors on Revenue-Producing Activities
- Creating a Culture of Passion and Purpose
- Succession Planning as a Retention Tool

---

**Recruiting Series**
Practical recruiting guidance for leaders, exploring how community involvement, social media, and targeted outreach can help build a stronger and more diverse advisor workforce.
- Recruiting Through Community Involvement
- Recruiting Through Social Media
- Recruiting Women to the Advisor Career
