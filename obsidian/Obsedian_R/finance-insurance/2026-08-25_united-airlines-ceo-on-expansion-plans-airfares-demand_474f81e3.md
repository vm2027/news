# United Airlines CEO on Expansion Plans, Airfares, Demand

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-25 18:21 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-25/united-airlines-ceo-on-expansion-plans-airfares-demand-video

## Summary

United Airlines Chief Executive Officer Scott Kirby discusses the largest international expansion in
the company’s history, the strength he sees in both customer demand and the US economy, and plans to
offset increased oil prices. The carrier will now fly to 10 more destinations in Europe and Asia.
Kirby speaks to Bloomberg's Lisa Abramowicz from United's hub at Newark Liberty International
Airport in New Jersey. (Source: Bloomberg)
