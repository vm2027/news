## Summary

Swiss Re shares edged lower on the SIX Swiss Exchange after Barclays raised its price target but maintained an underweight rating, signaling expected downside from current levels. The article situates the rating change against Swiss Re’s solid first-half 2026 results, including a 9% increase in net income to roughly $2.8 billion, and summarizes broader analyst sentiment and intraday trading dynamics.
