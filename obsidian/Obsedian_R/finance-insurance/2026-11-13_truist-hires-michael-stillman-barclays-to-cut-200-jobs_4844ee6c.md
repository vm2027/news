# Truist hires Michael Stillman, Barclays to cut 200 jobs

> **Source:** American Banker  
> **Published:** 2026-11-13  
> **URL:** https://www.americanbanker.com/news/truist-hires-michael-stillman-barclays-to-cut-200-jobs

## Summary

Truist hired Michael Stillman as a managing director in its industrials and services investment banking group. Barclays is preparing to eliminate about 200 positions across its investment bank, while Sumitomo Mitsui Financial Group plans a leadership appointment at its joint-venture brokerage with Jefferies.
