# Non-complying insurers won’t be allowed to operate

> **Source:** The Daily Star  
> **Published:** 2026-09-20  
> **URL:** https://www.thedailystar.net/business/economy/news/non-complying-insurers-wont-be-allowed-operate-4277221

## Summary

Bangladesh’s Insurance Development and Regulatory Authority said it would take stricter action against distressed insurance companies that fail to comply with regulatory requirements. The regulator indicated that non-compliant insurers could be prevented from operating.
