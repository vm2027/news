# FNBO to Acquire InBank, Expand Colorado Market

## Summary

First National of Nebraska, parent of FNBO, announced a definitive agreement to acquire Denver-based InBankshares Corp and its subsidiary InBank in a cash transaction.[8] The deal will expand FNBO’s presence in the Colorado market and reflects ongoing consolidation in regional banking and financial services.[8]
