# Irdai may roll out insurance distribution reforms in Jan 2027 amid backlash

> **Source:** Business Standard  
> **Published:** 2026-10-09  
> **URL:** https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/irdai-may-roll-out-insurance-distribution-reforms-in-jan-2027-amid-backlash-126100900175_1.html

## Summary

India’s insurance regulator may introduce major distribution reforms as early as January 2027. The proposed changes include caps on commissions paid to brokers and other distributors, a plan that has pressured shares of insurers and intermediaries.
