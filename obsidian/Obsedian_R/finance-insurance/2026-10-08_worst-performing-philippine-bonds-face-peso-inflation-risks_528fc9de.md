# Worst-Performing Philippine Bonds Face Peso, Inflation Risks

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-08 00:00 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-08/worst-performing-philippine-bonds-face-peso-inflation-risks

## Summary

Philippine bonds, the worst-performing in emerging markets since the Iran war began, are set for
further losses as faster inflation, a sliding peso and softer auction demand weigh on sentiment,
strategists say.
