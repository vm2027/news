# Oscar Health raises FY26 earnings guidance by $100 million

> **Source:** ScanX Trade  
> **Published:** 2026-09-16  
> **URL:** https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/companies/oscar-health-raises-fy26-earnings-guidance-100-million/51099737

## Summary

U.S. health insurer Oscar Health increased its earnings-from-operations guidance for FY2026 by $100 million, now projecting between $600 million and $800 million. The guidance update was released ahead of the company’s 2026 Investor Day, signaling stronger-than-expected operational performance and improved profitability outlook.
