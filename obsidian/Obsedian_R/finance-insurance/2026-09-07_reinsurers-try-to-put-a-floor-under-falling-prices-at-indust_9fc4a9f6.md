# Reinsurers try to put a floor under falling prices at industry meeting

> **Source:** MarketScreener  
> **Published:** 2026-09-07  
> **URL:** https://www.marketscreener.com/news/reinsurers-try-to-put-a-floor-under-falling-prices-at-industry-meeting-ce785bdbdf8ff725

## Summary

Global reinsurers at a key industry meeting are seeking to halt or slow the decline in reinsurance pricing that has followed several years of hard markets. Investors reacted negatively, with shares of Munich Re, Swiss Re and Hannover Re each down over 1.5%, reflecting concerns about future profitability if prices soften further. The negotiations over renewal terms are critical for capital allocation, risk appetite, and valuations across the global insurance and reinsurance sectors.
