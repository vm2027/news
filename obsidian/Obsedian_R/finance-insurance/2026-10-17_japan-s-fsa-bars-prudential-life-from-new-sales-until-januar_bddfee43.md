# Japan's FSA Bars Prudential Life From New Sales Until January 31 After an Employee Fraud Scandal

> **Source:** PolicyRix  
> **Published:** 2026-10-17  
> **URL:** https://www.policyrix.com/news/2026-10-17/japan-fsa-suspends-prudential-life-new-sales-until-january-2027-fraud

## Summary

Japan's Financial Services Agency ordered Prudential of Japan to stop soliciting new business through January 31, 2027. The action followed misconduct involving more than 100 employees and included separate restrictions on Gibraltar Life's employed sales channels.
