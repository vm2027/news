# Singapore Government Won’t Interfere in Decisions on Air India

> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-05/singapore-government-won-t-interfere-in-decisions-on-air-india

## Summary

The Singapore government said it will not interfere in any decision that Singapore Airlines Ltd.
makes in Air India Ltd., which is said to be seeking financial aid from its shareholders.
