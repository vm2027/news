# S&P 500 closes near flat after big banks post earnings and memory chip stocks decline

> **Source:** CNBC  
> **Published:** 2026-10-12  
> **URL:** https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/12/stock-market-today-live-updates.html

## Summary

The S&P 500 ended nearly unchanged as investors weighed large-bank earnings and weakness in memory-chip stocks. JPMorgan and Wells Fargo fell after mixed results, while concerns about valuations and Chinese competition pressured semiconductor shares.
