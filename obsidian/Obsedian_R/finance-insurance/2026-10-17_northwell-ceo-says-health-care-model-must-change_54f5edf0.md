# Northwell CEO Says Health Care Model Must Change

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-17 14:03 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-10-17/northwell-ceo-says-health-care-model-must-change-video

## Summary

Northwell Health President and CEO Dr. John D’Angelo tells Bloomberg This Weekend that the
traditional US health care model is unsustainable and that improving affordability will require a
greater focus on wellness, prevention and chronic disease. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Lisa
Mateo, D’Angelo discusses workforce shortages, the financial pressures facing nonprofit hospitals
and how AI could reduce administrative burdens while preserving the human role in consequential
medical decisions. (Source: Bloomberg)
