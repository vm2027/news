# Best's Market Segment Report: Focus on Digitization Bolsters Growth for Canada's Life/Annuity Insurers

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance / Business Wire  
> **Published:** 2026-09-18  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/best-market-segment-report-focus-145600126.html

## Summary

AM Best’s new market segment report finds that Canada’s life and annuity insurers delivered strong core operating results in 2025, supported by favorable investment returns and rising sales. The analysis attributes much of the growth to digitization and technology‑driven, customer‑centric business models, which are improving distribution efficiency, customer engagement and profitability.
