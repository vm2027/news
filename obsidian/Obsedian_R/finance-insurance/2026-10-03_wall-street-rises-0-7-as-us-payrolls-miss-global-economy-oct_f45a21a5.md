# Wall Street Rises 0.7% as US Payrolls Miss | Global Economy, Oct. 3

> **Source:** The Rio Times  
> **Published:** 2026-10-03  
> **URL:** https://www.riotimesonline.com/global-economy-briefing-october-3-2026/

## Summary

U.S. stocks rebounded after a weaker-than-expected payrolls report increased expectations that the Federal Reserve would hold rates at its next meeting. The S&P 500 and Nasdaq rose, while the dollar eased and euro-area inflation reached its highest level since 2023.
