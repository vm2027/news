# Asia stocks slide as U.S. tightens AI chip curbs ahead of Nvidia results

## Summary

Asian equities dropped after the United States tightened restrictions on China’s access to advanced AI chips. Semiconductor-heavy markets led losses as investors also considered weak Chinese demand, possible U.S. rate increases, and uncertainty surrounding AI-related valuations.
