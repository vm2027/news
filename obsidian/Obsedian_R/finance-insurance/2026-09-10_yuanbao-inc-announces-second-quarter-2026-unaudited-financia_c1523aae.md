# Yuanbao Inc. Announces Second Quarter 2026 Unaudited Financial Results

> **Source:** Markets Insider / GlobeNewswire

> **URL:** https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/stocks/yuanbao-inc-announces-second-quarter-2026-unaudited-financial-results-1036535398

## Summary

Chinese online insurance distributor Yuanbao Inc. reported second-quarter 2026 revenues of RMB 1,392.2 million, up 30.1% year-on-year, highlighting strong growth in its technology-driven distribution model. The company plans to discuss its financial and operating performance on an earnings call, signalling investor interest in digital insurance platforms. The results underscore the expansion of insurtech in China’s insurance and broader financial markets.
