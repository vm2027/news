# Canada Trade Can Help US Cool Inflation, Poilievre Says

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-11 14:14 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-11/canada-trade-can-help-us-cool-inflation-poilievre-says-video

## Summary

Conservative Party of Canada Leader Pierre Poilievre discusses the state of trade and tariffs with
the United States. He says Canada’s oil and mineral resources can help alleviate US inflation
pressures, but the Trump administration needs to back off its tariff policies against its northern
neighbor. He speaks on “Bloomberg Surveillance.” (Source: Bloomberg)
