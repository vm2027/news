# Thanksgiving Dinner Is Moving to Restaurants

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-11-21 15:28 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-11-21/thanksgiving-dinner-is-moving-to-restaurants-video

## Summary

Bloomberg Pursuits Food Editor Kate Krader tells Bloomberg This Weekend that more Americans are
choosing restaurants for Thanksgiving as higher grocery costs, cooking stress and cleanup make
dining out increasingly attractive. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Krader
explains how restaurants are responding with traditional feasts and unconventional menus as
Thanksgiving reservations continue to rise. (Source: Bloomberg)
