## Summary

A Dow Jones financial services roundup highlights that the Texas Stock Exchange is close to securing its first major listing from New York, signalling competitive shifts in U.S. equity market venues. The same report notes that U.S. home-insurance premiums have reached record highs, driven by mounting natural disaster risks and evolving insurer pricing models. These developments reflect both structural changes in capital markets and rising pressures in the property insurance segment.
