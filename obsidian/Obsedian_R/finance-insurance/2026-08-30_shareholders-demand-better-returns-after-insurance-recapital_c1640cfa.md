# Shareholders demand better returns after insurance recapitalisation

> **Source:** Gazette Nigeria  
> **Published:** 2026-08-30  
> **URL:** https://gazettengr.com/shareholders-demand-better-returns-after-insurance-recapitalisation/

## Summary

Shareholders in Nigeria’s insurance sector are calling for higher penetration, better dividends, stronger corporate governance and tighter regulatory oversight following a major recapitalisation drive. Fifty insurance and reinsurance firms have met new minimum capital thresholds, ranging from ₦10 billion for life insurers to ₦35 billion for reinsurers, sharpening focus on post-recapitalisation performance.
