# Nifty, Sensex Edge Down Despite Strong GDP Data

> **Source:** HDFC Sky  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01

## Summary

HDFC Sky’s market close report describes how Indian indices Nifty and Sensex ended marginally lower despite robust GDP figures, as sectoral losses in pharmaceuticals, banking, auto and realty offset gains in IT and FMCG. The piece underscores particular weakness in Nifty Auto, PSU Bank and Bank indices, reflecting pressure on financials and cyclical stocks.
