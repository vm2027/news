# Saudi Airport Hit Again as Riyadh Set to Host Major Conferences

## Summary

Saudi Arabia’s main airport came under renewed attack from Houthi rebels, a day after a strike on
the facility killed 12 people and heaped pressure on the Middle East’s biggest economy as it
prepares to host major events that seek to boost foreign investment.
