# What Prudential Financial (PRU) Said on Its Q3 Earnings Call

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-11-08  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/prudential-financial-pru-said-q3-131418074.html

## Summary

Prudential reported lower third-quarter adjusted operating earnings as the suspension of sales in Japan offset gains elsewhere. The company is narrowing its geographic focus, including plans to sell its Brazil and Mexico businesses, while asset-management inflows and U.S. annuity sales remained strong.
