# Antitrust opens probe into Intesa-MPS tender offer over competition risks

> **Source:** MarketScreener / Alliance News  
> **Published:** 2026-09-15  
> **URL:** https://www.marketscreener.com/news/antitrust-opens-probe-into-intesa-mps-tender-offer-over-competition-risks-ce785bddde89f023

## Summary

Italy’s Antitrust Authority has launched an investigation into Intesa Sanpaolo’s all‑cash‑and‑share takeover bid for Banca Monte dei Paschi di Siena, citing potential threats to competition in banking, asset management and insurance markets. Regulators are also examining links between Intesa and Generali and the possibility of sensitive information sharing, underscoring the deal’s implications for financial sector concentration.
