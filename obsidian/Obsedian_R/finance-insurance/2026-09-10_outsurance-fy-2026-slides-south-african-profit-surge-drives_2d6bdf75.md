## Summary

OUTsurance Group Limited reported audited results for the year ended June 30, 2026, showing normalised earnings growth of 18.5% to R5.6 billion at group level, driven primarily by strong performance in its South African operations.[10] The article notes that elevated natural peril losses in Australia and ongoing investment losses in Ireland were headwinds, but overall earnings growth remained robust.[10]
