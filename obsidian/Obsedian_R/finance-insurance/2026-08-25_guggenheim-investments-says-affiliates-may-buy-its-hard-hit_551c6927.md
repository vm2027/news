# Guggenheim Investments Says Affiliates May Buy Its Hard-Hit Loan

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-25 18:35 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-25/guggenheim-investments-says-affiliates-may-buy-its-hard-hit-loan

## Summary

Guggenheim Investments, the asset-management arm of Mark Walter’s Guggenheim Partners, is telling
lenders that affiliates may look to buy portions of a loan issued by its financing entity after the
debt’s value collapsed in recent weeks.
