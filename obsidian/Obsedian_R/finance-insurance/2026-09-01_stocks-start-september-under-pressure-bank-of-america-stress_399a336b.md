## Summary

CNBC reports that U.S. stock futures fell sharply at the start of September, with Dow futures down more than 300 points and S&P 500 contracts sliding 0.6%. The article discusses technical levels highlighted by Bank of America and how higher yields and macro concerns are driving caution among equity investors.
