# Probe of Mark Walter’s Insurers Spotlights Gaps in Regulatory Patchwork

> **Source:** Claims Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-11-19  
> **URL:** https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2026/11/19/341416.htm

## Summary

Federal prosecutors are investigating insurers associated with Mark Walter, including Delaware Life Insurance and Clear Spring Life and Annuity. The inquiry focuses on the companies’ failure to disclose more than $20 billion invested in entities financing Walter’s other ventures.
