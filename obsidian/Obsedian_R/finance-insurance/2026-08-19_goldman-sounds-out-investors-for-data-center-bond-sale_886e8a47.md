# Goldman Sounds Out Investors for Data Center Bond Sale

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-19 17:13 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/goldman-sounds-out-investors-for-coreweave-tied-data-center-bond

## Summary

Goldman Sachs Group Inc. is sounding out investor interest on a junk bond to fund construction of a
data center leased to CoreWeave Inc., adding to a wave of borrowing tied to the neocloud firm as it
seeks to capitalize on the artificial intelligence boom.
