# Stocks fall, squeezed by rising oil prices and Treasury yields

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-09-28  
> **URL:** https://www.reuters.com/world/china/global-markets-global-markets-2026-09-28/

## Summary

Global equities fell as higher oil prices and rising Treasury yields pressured markets. Investors priced in a 68% chance of a second consecutive Federal Reserve hike in October, while U.S. two-year yields were headed for their largest monthly increase since February 2023.
