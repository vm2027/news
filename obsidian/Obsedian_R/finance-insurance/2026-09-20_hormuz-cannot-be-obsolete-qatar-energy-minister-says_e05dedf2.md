# Hormuz ‘Cannot Be Obsolete,’ Qatar Energy Minister Says

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-20 17:01 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-20/hormuz-cannot-be-obsolete-qatar-energy-minister-says-video

## Summary

Qatar Energy Minister Saad al-Kaabi discusses the future of the Strait of Hormuz and says that to
call the strait obsolete is “wrong.” US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent commented on Sept. 1 that
the Strait of Hormuz will be like a worthless piece of water in two years. He spoke with Annmarie
Hordern at the 2026 Qatar Economic Forum, UNGA Special Edition; Powered by Bloomberg. (Source:
Bloomberg)
