# US Said to Plan Fresh NATO Push for Ukraine Arms Deliveries

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-11-02 22:08 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-02/us-said-to-plan-fresh-nato-push-for-ukraine-arms-deliveries

## Summary

The US is planning to ask NATO allies to contribute more money to a key weapons-delivery program for
Ukraine, people familiar with the matter said, a sign that US defense support for Kyiv continues
despite lingering tension between US President Donald Trump and Ukrainian President Volodymyr
Zelenskyy.
