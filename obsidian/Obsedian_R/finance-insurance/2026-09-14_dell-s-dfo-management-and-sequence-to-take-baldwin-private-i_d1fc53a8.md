# Dell's DFO Management and Sequence to take Baldwin private in $7.7 billion deal

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-09-14  
> **URL:** https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/dfo-management-sequence-take-baldwin-private-77-billion-deal-2026-09-14/

## Summary

Reuters reports that a consortium led by Dell’s DFO Management and Sequence is acquiring Baldwin Group, a major insurance brokerage, in a $7.7 billion all-cash transaction. The article outlines deal terms, strategic rationale, and how the take-private reflects ongoing private equity interest in insurance distribution and fee-based financial services.
