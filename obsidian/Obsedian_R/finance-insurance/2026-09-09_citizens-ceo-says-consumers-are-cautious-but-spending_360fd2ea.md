# Citizens CEO Says Consumers Are Cautious, But Spending

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-09 14:11 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-09/citizens-ceo-says-consumers-are-cautious-but-spending-video

## Summary

Citizens Financial Group CEO Bruce Van Saun says the US economy is strong and consumers are
spending, but they are cautious. He says customers have figured out how to absorb higher energy
costs. He also doesn't expect the Federal Reserve to raise interest rates soon. He speaks on
"Bloomberg Surveillance." (Source: Bloomberg)
