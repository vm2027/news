# UNGA Convenes While Questions Remain on Iran, Russia

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-21 19:20 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-21/unga-convenes-while-questions-remain-on-iran-russia

## Summary

Russia is set to extend a ban on most diesel exports beyond the end of September as Ukraine
continues to attack its refineries, according to people familiar with the matter. Russia enacted the
ban in July as a wave of Ukrainian strikes against its refineries dragged oil-processing rates to
multiyear lows. This comes as President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said he’ll meet President Donald Trump
during the UN General Assembly week in New York as the embattled Ukrainian leader looks to shore up
support to defend against stepped up Russian attacks. Zelenskyy said in a post on X that he had an
“important conversation” with Trump and said their upcoming meeting could “change a lot.” Heather
Conley, a senior fellow at the American Enterprise Institute and a former deputy assistant Secretary
of State speaks with Joe Mathieu and Kailey Leinz on Balance of Power. (Source: Bloomberg)
