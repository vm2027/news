# China Chipmaker YMTC Seeks to Raise $4.9 Billion in Shanghai IPO

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-21 14:44 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-21/china-chipmaker-ymtc-seeks-to-raise-4-9-billion-in-shanghai-ipo

## Summary

Chinese chipmaker Yangtze Memory Technologies Co., known as YMTC, plans to raise 33 billion yuan
($4.9 billion) in a Shanghai initial public offering, following in the footsteps of rival CXMT
Corp.’s near-record share sale.
