# PB Fintech's $3 billion rout leads Indian insurance sector's slump on commission-cap plan

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-09-24  
> **URL:** https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/india-insurance-reform-plan-tanks-distribution-linked-shares-threat-income-2026-09-24/

## Summary

Shares of Indian insurance distributors and lenders fell sharply after the insurance regulator proposed limits on commission payouts. PB Fintech dropped 36%, wiping out more than 314 billion rupees in market value, while the proposals could significantly reduce fee income across life, health, and motor insurance.
