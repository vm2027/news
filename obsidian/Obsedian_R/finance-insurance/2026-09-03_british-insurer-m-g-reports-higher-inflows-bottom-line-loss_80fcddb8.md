# British insurer M&G reports higher inflows, bottom-line loss

> **Source:** Reuters via Euronext  
> **Published:** 2026-09-03  
> **URL:** https://live.euronext.com/en/financial-news/british-insurer-mg-reports-higher-inflows-bottom-line-loss

## Summary

M&G reports stronger client cash inflows and beats half‑year operating profit forecasts, underscoring resilience in its insurance and asset management activities. However, the firm posts a bottom‑line loss due to a one‑off £325 million charge tied to UK reforms of ground rent assets, highlighting the impact of regulatory changes on balance‑sheet results despite solid underlying business flows.
