## Summary

The article notes that Indian equity indices held up thanks to strong gains in major insurance companies such as HDFC Life and SBI Life, even as banking stocks came under pressure. It links the outperformance of insurers to expectations of lower commissions paid to agents, while detailing sector‑specific movements across public‑sector and private‑sector banks.
