# UK Insurers Urge Bank of England to Rethink 'Dynamic' Stress Test

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-09-18  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/09/18/885631.htm

## Summary

Britain’s insurance industry is pressing the Bank of England’s Prudential Regulation Authority to reconsider its new live crisis “dynamic” stress test, arguing the exercise imposes excessive reporting burdens and relies on unrealistic scenarios. Insurers say the test pushed firms “to their absolute limit” and could distort risk management priorities, while regulators view it as vital for assessing resilience under severe market and liquidity shocks.
