# Profits up at reinsurance major Hannover Re

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-11-09  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/profits-reinsurance-major-hannover-104823991.html

## Summary

Hannover Re reported a 6.9% increase in nine-month net income to €2.1 billion and reaffirmed its full-year earnings outlook. Operating profit rose 16.5%, while property and casualty revenue declined and life and health reinsurance revenue increased.
