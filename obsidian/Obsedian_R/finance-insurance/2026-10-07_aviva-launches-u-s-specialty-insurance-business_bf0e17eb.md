# Aviva launches U.S. Specialty Insurance Business

> **Source:** Aviva  
> **Published:** 2026-10-07  
> **URL:** https://www.aviva.com/newsroom/news-and-research-overview/news-releases/2026/10/aviva-launches-us-specialty-insurance-business/

## Summary

Aviva announced the launch of Aviva Specialty, a wholly owned U.S. specialty-insurance platform headquartered in New York. The business plans to begin underwriting commercial-property risks in the fourth quarter of 2026 through brokers and program administrators.
