## Summary

This economic brief reports that all 13 sectoral indices on Nepal’s stock exchange rose, with the non-life insurance sub-index leading gains at 2.82% and strong advances also seen in finance, investment, and life insurance. It also details the Ministry of Finance’s Capital Market Strengthening and Revival Action Plan 2026, which introduces lower capital gains tax rates and lays groundwork for derivatives and short-selling mechanisms, aiming to boost domestic equity trading and private-sector participation.
