# BlackRock Turns Bullish on Short-Term Bonds, Downgrades Stocks

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-12 21:26 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-12/blackrock-turns-bullish-on-short-term-bonds-downgrades-stocks

## Summary

BlackRock Inc.’s research unit has turned more bullish US Treasuries, unwinding a “mainstay”
underweight stance for most of the past six years, as yields climbed to the highest levels in more
than two decades.
