# Orion180 Insurance valued at $1.14 billion as shares fall in debut

> **Published:** 2026-09-18

## Summary

Orion180 Insurance completed its U.S. initial public offering at a valuation of about $1.14 billion, but its shares fell on their first day of trading amid cautious investor sentiment. The IPO was priced below the initially targeted range, reflecting broader concerns over volatile market conditions and appetite for newly listed insurance and financial stocks.
