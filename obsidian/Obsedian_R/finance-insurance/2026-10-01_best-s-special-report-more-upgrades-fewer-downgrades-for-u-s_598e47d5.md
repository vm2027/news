# Best's Special Report: More Upgrades, Fewer Downgrades for U.S. Life/Health Insurers in First Half of 2026

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-10-01  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/best-special-report-more-upgrades-140900492.html

## Summary

AM Best reported that U.S. life and health insurers received twice as many rating upgrades as downgrades in the first half of 2026 compared with the same period a year earlier. Strong annuity sales contributed to growth in the life-insurance segment, while most rating actions were affirmations.
