# EQT to Buy Majority Stake in Insurance Broker McGill and Partners for $2 Billion

> **Source:** Dow Jones Newswires / Morningstar  
> **Published:** 2026-09-07  
> **URL:** https://www.morningstar.com/news/dow-jones/202609071847/dow-jones-top-financial-services-headlines-at-7-am-et-beijing-to-inject-more-than-50-billion-into-banks-insurers-how

## Summary

Swedish investment group EQT has agreed to acquire a majority stake in specialist insurance broker McGill and Partners in a deal valued at around $2 billion. The transaction highlights ongoing private equity interest in insurance distribution and advisory businesses amid a strong market for complex risk solutions.
