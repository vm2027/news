# How Water Scarcity Is Changing the Economics of the Southwest

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-23 14:02 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-23/the-water-crisis-reshaping-the-american-west-video

## Summary

The Colorado River provides water to seven states, contributing to an estimated $1.4 trillion in
annual economic activity. But decades of decline, record-low snowpack and shrinking reservoirs have
pushed the system toward crisis, with Lake Powell nearing levels that could threaten hydropower
production. As key rules expire and states fail to reach a long-term deal, the federal government is
stepping in with proposed cuts to lower-basin water use, hitting Arizona especially hard. Will the
Southwest be able to survive on a shrinking Colorado River? (Source: Bloomberg)
