# Aon close to acquiring USI Insurance from KKR in $17 billion deal, WSJ reports

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-08-30  
> **URL:** https://www.reuters.com/business/autodesk-forecasts-quarterly-profit-below-wall-street-estimates-shares-dip-2026-08-27/

## Summary

In a markets brief, Reuters reports that Aon Plc is close to agreeing a roughly $17 billion acquisition of insurance brokerage USI Insurance Services from KKR, including debt. Citing the Wall Street Journal, the note underscores the scale of the proposed deal and its potential to reshape the insurance brokerage landscape.
