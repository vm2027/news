# NYU's Suzy Welch on Career Ambition & Gen Z's Work Paradox

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-24 15:47 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-24/nyu-s-welch-on-career-ambition-gen-z-s-work-paradox-video

## Summary

NYU Stern professor Suzy Welch examines a growing contradiction among Gen Z: young workers
increasingly value achievement and career success while also wanting to spend less of their lives at
work. Welch discusses shifting attitudes toward ambition, work-life balance and what the trend means
for employers. She speaks with Bloomberg's Tom Keene and Paul Sweeney. (Source: Bloomberg)
