## Summary

Taiwan's top insurance regulator is pushing to reduce local insurers' heavy dependence on U.S. dollar assets, after currency swings previously erased billions in value from the sector. The move is part of a broader effort to reshape the investment strategies of Taiwan’s $1.2 trillion insurance industry and mitigate foreign exchange risk.
