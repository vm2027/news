# Quiet catastrophe year adds to renewal pressure on reinsurers

> **Source:** The Royal Gazette  
> **Published:** 2026-10-30  
> **URL:** https://www.royalgazette.com/reinsurance/business/article/20261030/quiet-catastrophe-year-adds-to-renewal-pressure-on-reinsurers/

## Summary

Global insured catastrophe losses reached about $91 billion through September, according to Aon, with no single event exceeding $10 billion. The relatively quiet catastrophe year is adding pressure to reinsurance renewals, although fourth-quarter activity could push annual losses above $100 billion.
