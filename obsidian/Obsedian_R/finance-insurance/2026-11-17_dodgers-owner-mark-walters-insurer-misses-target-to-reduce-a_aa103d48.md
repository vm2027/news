# Dodgers Owner Mark Walter’s Insurer Misses Target to Reduce Affiliated Assets

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-11-17  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/dodgers-owner-mark-walter-insurer-105700018.html

## Summary

Delaware Life reduced the share of investments tied to Mark Walter’s affiliated businesses to 35% in the third quarter, falling short of its stated target. The insurer said it had exchanged approximately $2.7 billion in assets with Walter’s holding company and was pursuing additional transactions.
