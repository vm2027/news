# Fed Looks Set to Pause After September Rate Hike

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-25 13:48 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-10-25/fed-looks-set-to-pause-after-september-rate-hike-video

## Summary

On Bloomberg This Weekend, Bloomberg News Federal Reserve reporter Maria Eloisa Capurro previews the
Fed’s upcoming rate decision, with policymakers widely expected to pause after September’s first
rate increase since 2023 despite inflation remaining above target. Speaking with hosts David Gura
and Christina Ruffini, Capurro explains why investors will scrutinize Chair Kevin Warsh’s language
for clues about another potential rate increase and his efforts to reshape how the central bank
approaches monetary policy. (Source: Bloomberg)
