# Barclays turns bearish on European insurers as rally outpaces earnings growth

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-11-17  
> **URL:** https://au.finance.yahoo.com/news/barclays-turns-bearish-european-insurers-103137287.html

## Summary

Barclays downgraded its view of the European insurance sector to negative, arguing that the recent share-price rally has outpaced expected earnings growth. The bank said insurer earnings are expected to grow more slowly than the broader market and cut ratings on several companies, including Mapfre and Swiss Life.
