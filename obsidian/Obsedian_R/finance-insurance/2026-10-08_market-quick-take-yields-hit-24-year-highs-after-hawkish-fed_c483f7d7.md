## Summary

The 10-year U.S. Treasury yield reached an intraday level above 5.36%, its highest point in 24 years, before easing. Equities weakened as investors absorbed hawkish Federal Reserve minutes, with European banks and Korean chipmakers among the hardest-hit groups.
