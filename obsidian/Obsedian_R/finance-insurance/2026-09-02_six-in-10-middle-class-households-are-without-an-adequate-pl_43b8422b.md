# Six in 10 Middle-Class Households Are Without an Adequate Plan for Long-Term Care

> **Source:** American Council of Life Insurers (ACLI)  
> **Published:** 2026-09-02  
> **URL:** https://www.acli.com/Newsroom/News-Releases/2026/09-02-Long-Term-Care-Planning

## Summary

New ACLI research finds that roughly 60% of middle-class households lack sufficient plans or coverage for long-term care needs, exposing them to significant future financial strain. The study highlights gaps in awareness and affordability and argues that life insurers and policymakers can expand the use of long-term care and hybrid insurance products to bolster household resilience.
