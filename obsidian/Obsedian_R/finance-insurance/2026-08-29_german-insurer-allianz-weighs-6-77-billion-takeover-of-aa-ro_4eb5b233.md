# German insurer Allianz weighs $6.77 billion takeover of AA roadside rescue giant

> **Source:** Global Banking & Finance / Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-08-29  
> **URL:** https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/german-insurer-allianz-weighs-6-77-billion-takeover-aa/

## Summary

Germany-based insurance and financial services group Allianz is considering a roughly £5 billion (about $6.77 billion) takeover of AA, the UK breakdown recovery firm. The deal is reported to be at an early stage, signaling Allianz’s interest in expanding its presence in roadside assistance and associated insurance services. A successful acquisition would mark a major consolidation move in the European insurance and automotive service markets.
