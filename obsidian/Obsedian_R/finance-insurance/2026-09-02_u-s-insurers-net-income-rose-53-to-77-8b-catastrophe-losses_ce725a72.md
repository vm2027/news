# U.S. insurers’ net income rose 53% to $77.8B. Catastrophe losses fell.

## Summary

The article reports that U.S. property and casualty insurers’ net income surged 53% to $77.8 billion, driven by strong underwriting gains and lower catastrophe losses through the first half of 2026. It underscores how reduced cat losses improved combined ratios and profitability across the sector, with implications for capital adequacy, pricing, and investor sentiment in the insurance market.
