# Insurers Are Buying Back More Stock as Pricing Softens

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-09-03  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/insurers-buying-back-more-stock-023500858.html

## Summary

This piece analyzes how insurers are increasing share buybacks at a time when global insurance pricing is starting to soften. It notes Marsh data showing overall insurance rates fell 6% in Q2, with casualty rates up 2% but property rates down 12%, and explains that companies are using buybacks to support valuations and deploy excess capital amid moderating rate growth.
