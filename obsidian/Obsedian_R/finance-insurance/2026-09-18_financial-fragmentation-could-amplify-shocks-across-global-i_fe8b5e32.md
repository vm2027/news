# Financial fragmentation could amplify shocks across global insurance markets

> **Source:** Asia Insurance Review  
> **Published:** 2026-09-18  
> **URL:** https://www.asiainsurancereview.com/News/ViewNewsLetterArticle/id/96936/Type/eDaily/Financial-fragmentation-could-amplify-shocks-across-global-insurance-markets

## Summary

The article warns that growing fragmentation in the global financial system could intensify shocks across insurance markets, especially if it coincides with broader asset market stress. It examines how increasing regulatory divergence and cross‑border frictions may affect insurers’ investment portfolios, capital adequacy and risk transmission, and highlights calls for closer international coordination.
