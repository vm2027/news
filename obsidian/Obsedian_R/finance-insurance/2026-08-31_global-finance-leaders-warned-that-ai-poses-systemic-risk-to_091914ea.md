# Global finance leaders warned that AI poses systemic risk to markets

> **Source:** InvestmentNews  
> **Published:** 2026-08-31  
> **URL:** https://www.investmentnews.com/regulation-legal-compliance/global-finance-leaders-warned-that-ai-poses-systemic-risk-to-markets/268002

## Summary

In a letter to G20 finance ministers and central bank governors, Financial Stability Board chair and Bank of England governor Andrew Bailey identified frontier artificial intelligence as a key emerging systemic risk to the global financial system.[14] The warning urges global regulators to address vulnerabilities in financial markets and infrastructures that could be exacerbated by advanced AI technologies.[14]
