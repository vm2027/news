# AI May Transform How Humans Understand Animals

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-05 12:46 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-05/ai-may-transform-how-humans-understand-animals-video

## Summary

Researchers are using AI to analyze vast collections of animal vocalizations, looking for patterns
that could help humans better understand how species such as crows communicate. Bloomberg Weekend
Senior Writer Morgan Meaker is on Bloomberg This Weekend and says the technology could reshape
humans’ relationship with animals, but researchers warn it also raises ethical questions about
privacy, exploitation and how those discoveries might be used. (Source: Bloomberg)
