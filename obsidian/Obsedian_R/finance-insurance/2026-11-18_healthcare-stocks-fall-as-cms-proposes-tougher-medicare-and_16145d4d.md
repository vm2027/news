# Healthcare Stocks Fall as CMS Proposes Tougher Medicare and Medicaid Oversight Rules

> **Source:** SSBCrack News  
> **Published:** 2026-11-18  
> **URL:** https://news.ssbcrack.com/healthcare-stocks-fall-as-cms-proposes-tougher-medicare-and-medicaid-oversight-rules/

## Summary

Healthcare stocks declined after CMS proposed tougher oversight of Medicare, Medicaid, CHIP, and the health insurance marketplace. Managed-care insurers led the losses, with Humana falling about 8%, Cigna declining more than 2%, and CVS Health losing 3%.
