# Proposed new insurance law restructures insurers' investment framework

> **Source:** Asia Insurance Review  
> **Published:** 2026-09-18  
> **URL:** https://www.asiainsurancereview.com/News/View-NewsLetter-Article/id/96938/type/eCin/Conferences/Gallery

## Summary

China is advancing a major overhaul of its Insurance Law, with a draft revision under public consultation that would significantly reshape how insurers deploy their investment funds. The proposed changes aim to modernize the sector’s regulatory architecture, tighten oversight of capital use, and address issues such as market irregularities and asset-liability management, with broader implications for financial stability and insurance market growth.
