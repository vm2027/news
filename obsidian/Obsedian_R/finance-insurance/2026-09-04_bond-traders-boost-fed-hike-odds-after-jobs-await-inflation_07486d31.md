# Bond Traders Boost Fed-Hike Odds After Jobs, Await Inflation

> **Published:** 2026-09-04 12:43 UTC

## Summary

Treasuries capped a choppy week of trading with higher short-term yields after a stronger-than-
expected employment report on Friday nudged up expectations for a Federal Reserve interest-rate hike
this month. Next week, key inflation data looms.
