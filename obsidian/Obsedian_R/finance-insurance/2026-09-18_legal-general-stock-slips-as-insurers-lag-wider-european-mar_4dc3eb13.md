# Legal & General stock slips as insurers lag wider European market

> **Source:** Ad-hoc News  
> **Published:** 2026-09-18  
> **URL:** https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/corporate-news/legal-and-general-stock-slips-as-insurers-lag-wider-european-market/70127387

## Summary

Shares in Legal & General Group fell around 2% as European insurers underperformed the broader equity market, with sector weakness attributed to shifting positioning and risk sentiment rather than company-specific news. The pullback came despite Legal & General’s recent interim results showing higher operating profit and a strong solvency capital position for the first half of 2026.
