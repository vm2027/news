# HDFC Life, other life insurance companies' stocks rise up to 7% on hope of lower commission payment- Moneycontrol.com

> **Source:** Moneycontrol  
> **Published:** 2026-09-17  
> **URL:** https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/markets/hdfc-life-other-life-insurance-companies-stocks-rise-up-to-7-on-hope-of-lower-commission-payment-14032087.html

## Summary

Shares of HDFC Life, SBI Life and other Indian life insurers rallied up to 7% on expectations that upcoming regulatory changes will reduce commission payments to distributors. Investors are reacting to reports that IRDAI will soon release a consultation paper on insurance commissions and distribution, potentially improving insurers’ profitability.
