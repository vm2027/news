# Lloyd's Insurance Market Sees £1.4 Billion Loss From US-Iran War

> **Source:** Bloomberg  
> **Published:** 2026-09-03  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-03/lloyd-s-insurance-market-sees-1-4-billion-loss-from-us-iran-war

## Summary

Lloyd’s of London estimates that losses arising from the Middle East conflict and US-Iran war amount to about £1.4 billion, providing the market’s first detailed assessment of the impact. Despite these war-related claims, Lloyd’s reported a pretax profit of £3.5 billion for the first six months of 2026, a 17% year-on-year decline but broadly in line with prior guidance.
