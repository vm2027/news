# Moynihan Says Bank Is Still Strong, Even If Trading Revenue Comes In Flat

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-14 19:16 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-14/moynihan-says-bank-is-strong-even-with-flat-trading-video

## Summary

Bank of America Corp. Chief Executive Officer Brian Moynihan said trading revenue will be
"relatively flat" compared with last year's third quarter. But he says even if that happens, the
bank will still have had one of its best third quarters ever. He speaks to Bloomberg's Dani Burger.
