# Hong Kong lists eight ETFs to tap Chinese insurer demand for overseas assets

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-09-28  
> **URL:** https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/hong-kong-lists-eight-etfs-tap-chinese-demand-overseas-assets-2026-09-28/

## Summary

Hong Kong launched eight exchange-traded funds offering exposure to overseas markets, including South Korean chipmakers, U.S. technology firms, and Malaysian large-cap stocks. The listings aim to capture demand from Chinese insurers after regulators permitted mainland insurers to invest in Hong Kong-listed ETFs through Southbound Stock Connect.
