# EU Envoy Urges Maximum Pressure on Russia

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-05 13:55 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-05/eu-envoy-urges-maximum-pressure-on-russia-video

## Summary

EU Ambassador to the United States Jovita Neliupšienė joins Bloomberg This Weekend and discusses
with hosts Christina Ruffini and Jeff Mason that Europe must keep supporting Ukraine while applying
“maximum pressure” on Moscow because Russia has not changed its war aims. The comments come as US
envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner visit Moscow before heading to Kyiv in an effort to revive
stalled peace negotiations. (Source: Bloomberg)
