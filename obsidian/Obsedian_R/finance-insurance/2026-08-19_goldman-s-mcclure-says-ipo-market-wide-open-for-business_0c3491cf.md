# Goldman's McClure Says IPO Market 'Wide Open' for Business

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-19 16:54 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-19/goldman-s-mcclure-ipo-market-wide-open-for-business-video

## Summary

Matt McClure, Goldman Sachs global co-head of investment banking, says the IPO market is "open for
business," with AI-related companies accounting for more than half of capital raised despite
representing about 20% of IPOs this year. Speaking with Dani Burger on "Bloomberg Deals," McClure
says confidence is growing in private equity, with $1.5 trillion of capital waiting to be deployed.
