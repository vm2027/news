# Warren and Hawley press six insurers on surge in claims closed with no payout

> **Source:** Briefs  
> **Published:** 2026-10-05

## Summary

U.S. Senators Elizabeth Warren and Josh Hawley are seeking information from six major insurers about claims closed without payments to policyholders. The inquiry focuses on the frequency and handling of no-payout claim closures.
