# Federal Agencies Rescind Interagency Statement on Special Purpose Credit Programs

> **Source:** Cooley Finsights  
> **Published:** 2026-09-02  
> **URL:** https://finsights.cooley.com/federal-agencies-rescind-interagency-statement-on-special-purpose-credit-programs/

## Summary

A group of federal agencies including the FDIC, NCUA, OCC, CFPB, HUD, DOJ, and FHFA jointly rescinded the 2022 “Interagency Statement on Special Purpose Credit Programs Under the Equal Credit Opportunity Act and Regulation B.”[12] The article explains that the move affects how banks and credit providers structure special-purpose credit programs aimed at underserved borrowers, with implications for compliance and fair lending strategies.[12]
