# Bond Market Selloff in ‘New Chapter,’ JPM’s Berro Says

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-01 11:14 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-10-01/bond-market-selloff-in-new-chapter-jpm-s-berro-says-video

## Summary

Kelsey Berro, fixed income portfolio manager at JPMorgan Asset Management, discusses recent changes
in the bond market selloff, saying it now feels “very technically-driven, very momentum-driven.”
Berro says on “Bloomberg Surveillance” that she expects investors to refocus soon on the
fundamentals and valuations of the market. (Source: Bloomberg)
