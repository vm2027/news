## Summary

This sectoral snapshot explains that Indian equities recovered on September 4, driven by gains in metals, financials, and oil & gas while IT, auto, pharma and realty lagged. It highlights that insurance names like SBI Life Insurance and HDFC Life Insurance contributed to the positive performance of the financials sector.
