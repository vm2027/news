# Blue Owl Plans $513 Million CMBS for Amazon-Leased Data Center

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-11-19 00:54 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-19/blue-owl-plans-513-million-cmbs-for-amazon-leased-data-center

## Summary

Blue Owl Capital Inc. is preparing to sell $513 million of commercial mortgage backed securities to
fund the acquisition of a Northern Virginia data center that’s fully leased to Amazon.com Inc.,
according to people with knowledge of the matter.
