# EU's Insurance Watchdog Warns of Risks Tied to Extreme Heat

> **Source:** Claims Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-10-01  
> **URL:** https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2026/10/01/340490.htm

## Summary

The European Insurance and Occupational Pensions Authority warned that extreme heat could become a material risk for insurers and pension providers. Potentially affected areas include medical costs, income protection, and other insurance lines, although diversification may offset some exposures.
