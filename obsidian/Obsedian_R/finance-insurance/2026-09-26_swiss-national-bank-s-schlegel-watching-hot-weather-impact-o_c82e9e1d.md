# Swiss National Bank's Schlegel watching hot weather impact on food price inflation

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-09-26  
> **URL:** https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/swiss-national-banks-schlegel-watching-hot-weather-impact-food-price-inflation-2026-09-26/

## Summary

Swiss National Bank Chairman Martin Schlegel said the central bank is monitoring how unusually hot weather could affect food prices and inflation. Weather-related supply pressures could complicate the bank's assessment of future monetary policy.
