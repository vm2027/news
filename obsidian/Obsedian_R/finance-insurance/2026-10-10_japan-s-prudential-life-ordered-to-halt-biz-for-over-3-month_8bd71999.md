# Japan's Prudential Life ordered to halt biz for over 3 months

> **Source:** Tuoi Tre News  
> **Published:** 2026-10-10  
> **URL:** https://news.tuoitre.vn/japans-prudential-life-ordered-to-halt-biz-for-over-3-months-103261010140806946.htm

## Summary

Japan’s Financial Services Agency ordered Prudential Life and Gibraltar Life to suspend some operations for more than three months following employee fraud. The insurers cannot enter new insurance contracts or sell products during the suspension and must strengthen governance.
