## Summary

This article examines how the 9/11 terrorist attacks generated roughly $40 billion in claims at the time—around $60 billion in today’s dollars—and fundamentally changed the global insurance and reinsurance markets.[2] It discusses the rise of terrorism insurance programs, shifts in risk modelling, and lasting implications for capital, pricing, and regulatory frameworks in the sector.[2]
