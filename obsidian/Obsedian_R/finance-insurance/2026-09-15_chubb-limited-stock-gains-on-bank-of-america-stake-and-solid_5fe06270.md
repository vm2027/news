# Chubb Limited stock gains on Bank of America stake and solid Q2 figures

> **Source:** Ad-hoc-news  
> **Published:** 2026-09-15  
> **URL:** https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/corporate-news/chubb-limited-stock-gains-on-bank-of-america-stake-and-solid-q2-figures/70105702

## Summary

Chubb Limited shares traded around $341 on the NYSE after disclosures that Bank of America Corp DE acquired roughly 4.53 million shares, a $1.5 billion stake equal to about 1.18% of the insurer. The article notes that strong second-quarter 2026 earnings and target price increases from major banks such as JPMorgan have supported the stock, reflecting investor confidence in the global property and casualty insurer amid favorable underwriting and pricing trends.
