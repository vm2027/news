# A $2,000 Sweater Reveals Fashion’s True Cost

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-29 14:54 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-29/a-2-000-sweater-reveals-fashion-s-true-cost-video

## Summary

Content creator Macy Gilliam spent 90-hours over four months and about $2,000 making a sweater from
scratch, starting with wool from a sheep at New York City’s Queens County Farm Museum. Gilliam joins
Christina Ruffini on Bloomberg This Weekend where she explains that the project gave her a new
appreciation for the labor behind clothing and raised questions about transparency, quality and
ethics across fashion’s complex supply chains. (Source: Bloomberg)
