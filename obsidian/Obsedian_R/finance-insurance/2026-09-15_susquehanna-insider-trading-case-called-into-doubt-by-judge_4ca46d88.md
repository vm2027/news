# Susquehanna Insider Trading Case Called Into Doubt by Judge

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-15 14:58 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-15/judge-casts-doubt-on-susquehanna-suit-alleging-insider-trading

## Summary

A federal judge dealt a setback to Susquehanna International Group’s lawsuit claiming it lost tens
of millions of dollars to insider trading on a Chinese regulatory crackdown, denying a request to
keep the alleged traders’ accounts frozen.
