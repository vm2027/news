# Energy Markets Strained by Iran, Ukraine Wars

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-05 11:54 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-05/energy-markets-strained-by-iran-ukraine-wars-video

## Summary

Renewed US-Iran fighting and uncertainty over shipping through the Strait of Hormuz are adding
pressure to global energy markets, while the threat of mines continues to deter some traffic.
Ukraine’s attacks on Russian refineries are compounding tight diesel supplies, creating what
Bloomberg News Oil Products Reporter Will Kubzansky describes as a distillates crisis heading into
peak demand season. Also joining Bloomberg This Weekend to discuss the situation is Bloomberg News
Middle East Correspondent Abeer Abu Omar. (Source: Bloomberg)
