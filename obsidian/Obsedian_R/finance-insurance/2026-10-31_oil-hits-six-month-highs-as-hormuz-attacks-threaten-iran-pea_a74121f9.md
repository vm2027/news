# Oil hits six-month highs as Hormuz attacks threaten Iran peace efforts

> **Source:** Investing.com  
> **Published:** 2026-10-31  
> **URL:** https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/oil-hits-six-month-highs-063746684.html

## Summary

Oil prices ended October at six-month highs after attacks on shipping in and around the Strait of Hormuz intensified. Brent crude settled at $117.64 per barrel and West Texas Intermediate at $104.12, with October gains exceeding 13% for both benchmarks.
