# Global bonds reel as hot U.S. CPI and hawkish Fed revive 50-bp hike fears

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-11-11  
> **URL:** https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/global-bonds-fall-hot-u-094408085.html

## Summary

Global sovereign bonds sold off sharply after stronger U.S. inflation data and hawkish Federal Reserve commentary. Markets increased the probability of a December rate hike and began assigning a meaningful, though limited, probability to a half-point move.
