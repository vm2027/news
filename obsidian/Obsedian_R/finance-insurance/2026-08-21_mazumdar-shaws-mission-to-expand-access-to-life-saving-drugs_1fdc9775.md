# Mazumdar-Shaw’s Mission to Expand Access to Life-Saving Drugs

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-21 00:06 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-21/mazumdar-shaw-s-mission-to-expand-access-to-medicines-video

## Summary

Known as India’s ‘Queen of Biotech’, Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw built Biocon from a rented garage almost 50
years ago. Today, her multibillion-dollar company is one of the world’s biggest producers of
insulin, and a leading force in biosimilars. As she looks at handing over her life’s work to
successors, Mazumdar-Shaw talks to Bloomberg’s Haslinda Amin about her pioneering journey as a
female entrepreneur in India, her mission to deliver life-saving therapies to patients and what she
believes India must do to tackle an AI future. (Source: Bloomberg)
