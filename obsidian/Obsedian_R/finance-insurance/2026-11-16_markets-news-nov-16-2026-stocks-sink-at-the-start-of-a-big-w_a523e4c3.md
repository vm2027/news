U.S. stocks fell sharply as investors reduced exposure to artificial-intelligence companies ahead of Nvidia’s earnings. The Nasdaq declined 2.5%, the S&P 500 fell 1.9%, and the Dow lost 1.4%, while bitcoin, oil, and gold also weakened.
