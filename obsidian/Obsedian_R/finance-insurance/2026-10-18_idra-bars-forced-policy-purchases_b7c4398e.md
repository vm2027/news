# IDRA bars forced policy purchases

> **Source:** The Daily Star  
> **Published:** 2026-10-18  
> **URL:** https://www.thedailystar.net/business/economy/news/idra-bars-forced-policy-purchases-4301181

## Summary

Bangladesh's Insurance Development and Regulatory Authority barred life insurers from requiring claimants or nominees to purchase new policies before receiving claim payments. Insurers must transfer the full claim amount in one transaction and face restrictions on issuing new policies after delayed settlements.
