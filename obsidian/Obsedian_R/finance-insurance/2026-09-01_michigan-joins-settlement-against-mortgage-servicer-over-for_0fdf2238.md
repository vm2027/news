# Michigan Joins Settlement Against Mortgage Servicer Over ‘Force-Placed’ Insurance

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/midwest/2026/09/01/766010.htm

## Summary

The Michigan Department of Insurance and Financial Services has joined a multistate settlement with a mortgage servicer accused of improperly force-placing insurance on borrowers. The agreement requires restitution and changes to servicing practices, and is part of a broader crackdown on consumer abuses at the intersection of housing finance and insurance.
