# Insurers Turn to M&A and Capital Raises as Home Market Shrinks

> **Source:** Seoul Economic Daily  
> **Published:** 2026-09-03  
> **URL:** https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/09/03/insurers-turn-to-ma-and-capital-raises-as-home-market

## Summary

Korean insurers are increasingly pursuing mergers and acquisitions alongside capital-raising measures as their domestic market shrinks and regulatory pressures intensify. The article focuses on the upcoming introduction of a 50% threshold for the basic capital solvency ratio under the Korean Insurance Capital Standard, which is pushing insurers to bolster solvency through share issues, retained earnings, and strategic restructuring.
