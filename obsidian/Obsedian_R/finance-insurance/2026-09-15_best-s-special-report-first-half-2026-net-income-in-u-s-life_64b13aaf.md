# Best's Special Report: First-Half 2026 Net Income in U.S. Life/Annuity Insurance Industry Dips Slightly

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance / AM Best  
> **Published:** 2026-09-15  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/best-special-report-first-half-123000348.html

## Summary

AM Best reports that net income for the U.S. life/annuity insurance industry was relatively flat in the first half of 2026 compared with the same period a year earlier, with total income down 8.1% due to lower premiums and annuity considerations. The decline was driven largely by a sharp reduction at Voya Retirement Insurance & Annuity Co. and lower other income tied to reserve adjustments on reinsurance ceded at American United Life Insurance Co., highlighting profit pressures despite stable overall earnings.
