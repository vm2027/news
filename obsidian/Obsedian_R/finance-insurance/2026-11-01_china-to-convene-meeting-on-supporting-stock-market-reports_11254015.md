# China to Convene Meeting on Supporting Stock Market, Reports Say

> **Source:** Bloomberg  
> **Published:** 2026-11-01  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-01/china-to-convene-meeting-on-supporting-stock-market-reports-say

## Summary

China’s central bank, securities regulator, financial regulator, and major banks are expected to discuss measures to support the capital market. The meeting reportedly will consider improving a relending program for share buybacks and increases in shareholder stakes.
