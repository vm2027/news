## Summary

This market report explains that Indian benchmarks Sensex and Nifty50 declined for a second consecutive session on September 1 as surging crude prices and selling in banking and auto stocks dampened sentiment. Several sectoral indices, including healthcare, consumer durables, PSU bank, pharma, auto, bank and financial services, closed lower, signaling broad weakness across financial and cyclical sectors.
