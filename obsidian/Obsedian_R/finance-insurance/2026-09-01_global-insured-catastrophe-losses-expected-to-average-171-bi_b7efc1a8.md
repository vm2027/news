# Global Insured Catastrophe Losses Expected to Average $171 Billion Annually: Verisk

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/09/01/779001.htm

## Summary

Verisk projects that global insured catastrophe losses will average about $171 billion per year, underscoring rising climate and disaster risks to the insurance industry.[1] The report warns that carriers must factor increasingly frequent and severe events into pricing, capital planning, and reinsurance strategies.[1]
