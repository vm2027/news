# ICICI Bank Buys 2% More in ICICI Life; Prudential Cuts Stake

> **Source:** HDFC Sky News  
> **Published:** 2026-09-03  
> **URL:** https://hdfcsky.com/news/icici-bank-acquires-2percent-stake-from-joint-venture-partner-of-its-life-insurance-arm-for-rs-1470-cr

## Summary

ICICI Bank announces it has raised its stake in its life insurance subsidiary by acquiring an additional 2% from its joint venture partner Prudential. Executed in multiple tranches between July 22 and September 2, 2026, the deal involved purchasing over 29 million shares for around Rs 14.70 billion via the stock exchange, signaling ICICI’s strategic commitment to its life insurance business. The article also briefly situates the move in the context of Indian equity market performance on September 3.
