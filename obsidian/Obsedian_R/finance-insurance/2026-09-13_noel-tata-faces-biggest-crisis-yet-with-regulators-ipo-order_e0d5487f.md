## Summary

The Indian banking regulator’s refusal to exempt Tata Sons Pvt. from a public listing-rule comes as
an unexpected blow for group patriarch Noel Tata, who’s already struggling to address leadership
uncertainty at the top of the $185 billion conglomerate.
