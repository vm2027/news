# Carney Expands Tax Break to Broad Range of Investments

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-15 13:01 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-15/carney-expands-business-tax-break-to-broad-range-of-investments

## Summary

Prime Minister Mark Carney is expanding the scope of a major investment tax write-off, adding oil
and gas pipelines, oil production equipment, mining property and more to the list of assets eligible
for immediate expensing in Canada.
