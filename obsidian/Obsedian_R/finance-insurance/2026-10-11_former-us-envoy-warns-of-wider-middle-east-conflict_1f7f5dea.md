# Former US Envoy Warns of Wider Middle East Conflict

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-11 17:12 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-10-11/former-us-envoy-warns-of-wider-middle-east-conflict-video

## Summary

Former White House Middle East Coordinator Brett McGurk tells Bloomberg This Weekend that the deadly
attack on Riyadh's international airport underscores the growing threat from Iran-backed Houthi
militants, while hardline leadership in Tehran complicates efforts to negotiate an end to the
broader regional conflict. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, McGurk discusses
his new book, Brink, which recounts negotiations to free the October 7 hostages, and warns that the
Trump administration's Russian diesel agreement could undermine efforts to end the war in Ukraine.
