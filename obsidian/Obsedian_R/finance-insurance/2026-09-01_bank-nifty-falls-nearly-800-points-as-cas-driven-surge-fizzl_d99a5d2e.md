# Bank Nifty falls nearly 800 points as CAS-driven surge fizzles out; Yes Bank, Axis Bank lead losses

> **Source:** Moneycontrol  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01  
> **URL:** https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/markets/bank-nifty-falls-400-points-today-as-cas-driven-surge-fizzles-out-axis-bank-yes-bank-lead-losses-14019756.html

## Summary

The article reports that India's Bank Nifty index fell more than 1% on September 1, reversing a previous closing auction session-driven jump as broad-based selling hit banking stocks. Yes Bank, Axis Bank, SBI, IndusInd Bank and several other lenders led the declines, while Kotak Mahindra Bank and PNB were among the few gainers, underscoring renewed pressure in the financial sector.
