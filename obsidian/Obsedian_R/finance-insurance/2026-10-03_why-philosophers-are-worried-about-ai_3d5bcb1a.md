# Why Philosophers Are Worried About AI

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-03 14:03 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-10-03/why-philosophers-are-worried-about-ai-video

## Summary

Debate over artificial intelligence has largely focused on whether machines could become too
powerful. Harvard philosopher Michael Sandel argues that a more immediate question is whether AI
changes humans themselves: weakening authentic connection, independent thought and the ways people
understand meaning and purpose. Universities are already confronting that challenge as students
increasingly use AI to learn and communicate, while Vanderbilt University Chancellor Daniel
Diermeier argues that technological fluency must be paired with the ability to think independently.
University of Notre Dame philosophy professor Meghan Sullivan takes that question beyond the
classroom, examining the ethical responsibility of the companies building AI and the role
individuals have in deciding how the technology enters their everyday lives. (Source: Bloomberg)
