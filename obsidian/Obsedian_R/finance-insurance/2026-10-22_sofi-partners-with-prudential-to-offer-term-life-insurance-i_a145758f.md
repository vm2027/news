# SoFi partners with Prudential to offer term life insurance in its app

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-10-22  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/news/sofi-partners-prudential-offer-term-141601042.html

## Summary

SoFi Technologies partnered with Prudential Financial to offer eligible members access to term life insurance through the SoFi app. Customers can assess coverage, receive quotes, apply, and purchase Prudential’s SimplyTerm product digitally.
