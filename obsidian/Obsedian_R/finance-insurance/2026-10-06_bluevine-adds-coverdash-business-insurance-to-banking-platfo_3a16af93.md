# Bluevine adds Coverdash business insurance to banking platform

> **Source:** ROI-NJ  
> **Published:** 2026-10-06  
> **URL:** https://www.roi-nj.com/2026/10/06/finance/bluevine-adds-coverdash-business-insurance-to-banking-platform/

## Summary

Bluevine partnered with Coverdash to allow small-business customers to compare, purchase, and manage commercial insurance through its banking platform. The offering provides access to products from more than 40 carriers, including liability, workers’ compensation, commercial auto, professional liability, and cyber coverage.
