# Iran Nuclear Activity Raises New Concerns

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-12 13:57 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-12/iran-nuclear-activity-raises-new-concerns-video

## Summary

On Bloomberg This Weekend, Council on Foreign Relations senior fellow Ray Takeyh says renewed Houthi
attacks represent a significant setback for the US strategy of stabilizing regional oil flows and
may indicate closer coordination between the group and Iran. Takeyh also tells hosts David Gura and
Christina Ruffini that economic pressure has failed to force Tehran to change course, as new
construction at the deeply buried Pickaxe Mountain complex raises questions about the future of
Iran's nuclear program. (Source: Bloomberg)
