# Financial Group Inc Stock (PFG) Moved Up by 8.12% on Sep 3

> **Source:** TradingKey  
> **Published:** 2026-09-03  
> **URL:** https://www.tradingkey.com/news/market-movers/262149645-market-movers-pfg-20260903

## Summary

Principal Financial Group’s stock surged 8.12% on September 3, significantly outperforming the broader insurance sector. The article attributes the move to media reports that Samsung Life is seeking a strategic 15% equity stake, the completion of Principal’s acquisition of Beam Benefits, and strong quarterly results with an increased dividend.
