# People: Builders Insurance Names Jasmund Board Chairman

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-09-02 16:41 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/southeast/2026/09/02/883727.htm

## Summary

Atlanta-based Builders Insurance Group has named David Jasmund, a longtime board member and
investment banker, as chairman of the board. Jasmund succeeds Charles Potok, who retired last month
after three years as chairman, the company said in a news release. &#8230;
