# Rising Premiums and Stalled Digital Progress Keep Insurance Satisfaction Under Pressure, ACSI Data Show

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-10-27  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/rising-premiums-stalled-digital-progress-120400952.html

## Summary

The 2026 American Customer Satisfaction Index insurance study found that satisfaction with health and life insurance declined by 1%. Property and casualty insurance satisfaction was unchanged, as higher premiums and limited digital improvements continued to weigh on customers.
