## Summary

This piece reports that MetLife Inc. shares traded around $94.11, down about 1.11% intraday, leaving the insurer below the $100 mark despite a consensus buy rating and an average analyst target near $102.21. It also highlights MetLife’s publication of its 2025 Sustainability Report, emphasizing ESG initiatives and community-focused strategies as part of its broader corporate narrative.
