## Summary

On this episode of the Odd Lots podcast, Adam Posen, president of the Peterson Institute for
International Economics, tells Joe Weisenthal and Tracy Alloway what Fed Chairman Kevin Warsh means
when he uses the term, "family fight," to describe debate on policy decisions and why he thinks some
dissents may be beneficial. (Source: Bloomberg)
