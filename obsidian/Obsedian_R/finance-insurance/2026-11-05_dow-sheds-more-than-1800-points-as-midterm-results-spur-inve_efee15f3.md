# Dow sheds more than 1800 points as midterm results spur investor exodus

> **Source:** CBS News

> **URL:** https://www.cbsnews.com/news/stocks-midterm-elections-tech-companies-ai-data-centers/

## Summary

U.S. stocks suffered a steep decline amid concerns that changed congressional control could slow data-center construction supporting artificial intelligence. The Nasdaq fell 5.5%, the S&P 500 dropped 4.4%, and the Dow lost 3.4%.
