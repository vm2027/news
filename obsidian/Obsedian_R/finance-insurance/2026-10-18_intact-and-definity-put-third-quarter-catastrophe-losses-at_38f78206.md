# Intact and Definity Put Third-Quarter Catastrophe Losses at About C$850 Million Combined, With Intact's Trailing-Year Total Above Its Guidance

> **Source:** PolicyRix  
> **Published:** 2026-10-18  
> **URL:** https://www.policyrix.com/news/2026-10-18/intact-definity-q3-2026-catastrophe-losses-850-million-ontario-quebec-floods-bc-wildfires

## Summary

Intact Financial estimated third-quarter catastrophe losses of approximately C$660 million, while Definity estimated about C$190 million. The losses were driven by floods in Ontario and Quebec, British Columbia wildfires, and a September storm.
