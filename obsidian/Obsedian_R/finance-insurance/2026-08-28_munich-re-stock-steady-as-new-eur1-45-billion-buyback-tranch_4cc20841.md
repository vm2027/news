# Munich Re stock steady as new €1.45 billion buyback tranche starts

> **Source:** Ad-hoc-news.de  
> **Published:** 2026-08-28  
> **URL:** https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/corporate-news/munich-re-stock-steady-as-new-1-45-billion-buyback-tranche-starts/70012482

## Summary

Munich Re has launched a new share repurchase tranche worth approximately €1.45 billion, scheduled to run from August 28, 2026 through January 29, 2027. The buyback is backed by record profits in the first half of 2026, underscoring the reinsurer’s strong capital position and shareholder-return strategy. The company’s stock traded steadily around the announcement, suggesting investor confidence in its earnings and capital management plans.
