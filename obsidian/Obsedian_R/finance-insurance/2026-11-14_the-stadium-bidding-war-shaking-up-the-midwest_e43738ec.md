# The Stadium Bidding War Shaking Up the Midwest

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-11-14 15:04 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-11-14/the-stadium-bidding-war-shaking-up-the-midwest-video

## Summary

The Chicago Bears are weighing three possible homes for a new stadium: downtown Chicago, suburban
Arlington Heights and Hammond, Indiana. The competition has turned into a high-stakes economic
development battle involving billions of dollars in potential investment, tax incentives and public
financing. Supporters say a new stadium could generate jobs, development and tax revenue, but former
Indiana Governor Mitch Daniels warns that emotion can push politicians into deals that leave
taxpayers paying the bill long after the ribbon cutting. (Source: Bloomberg)
