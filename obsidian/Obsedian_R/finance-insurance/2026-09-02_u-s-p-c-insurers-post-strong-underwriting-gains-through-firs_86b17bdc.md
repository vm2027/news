# U.S. P&C Insurers Post Strong Underwriting Gains Through First Half of 2026; Line-Specific and Geographic Challenges Persist

> **Source:** Verisk / APCIA  
> **Published:** 2026-09-02  
> **URL:** https://www.verisk.com/company/newsroom/u.s.-pc-insurers-post-strong-underwriting-gains-through-first-half-of-2026-line-specific-and-geographic-challenges-persist/

## Summary

Verisk and the American Property Casualty Insurance Association report that U.S. property-casualty insurers generated an estimated $31.7 billion net underwriting gain in the first half of 2026, up sharply from mid-2025. Net income after taxes climbed to $77.8 billion and policyholders’ surplus reached about $1.3 trillion, although certain lines and regions still face profitability and catastrophe risk pressures.
