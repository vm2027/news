# Three Finance Ministers on Avoiding Debt Crises

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-29 12:12 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-29/three-finance-ministers-on-avoiding-debt-crises-video

## Summary

US public debt just crossed $40 trillion, but the country is hardly the only one facing fiscal
troubles. Nations around the world took on record debt during Covid, and many have struggled to
bring spending back under control. Chrystia Freeland, who faced this problem herself as Canada's
Finance Minister, speaks with former Dutch Finance Minister Sigrid Kaag and former German Finance
Minister Christian Lindner about why fiscal discipline is so politically difficult and what the rest
of the world can learn before markets force the issue. (Source: Bloomberg)
