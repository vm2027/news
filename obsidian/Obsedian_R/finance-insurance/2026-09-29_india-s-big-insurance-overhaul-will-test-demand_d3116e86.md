# India's big insurance overhaul will test demand

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-09-29  
> **URL:** https://www.reuters.com/commentary/breakingviews/indias-big-insurance-overhaul-will-test-demand-2026-09-29/

## Summary

India’s insurance regulator has proposed substantial reductions in commissions paid to agents, distributors, and banks. The proposal also eliminates commissions on third-party insurance for new vehicles, creating uncertainty about whether lower distribution costs will increase insurance demand.
