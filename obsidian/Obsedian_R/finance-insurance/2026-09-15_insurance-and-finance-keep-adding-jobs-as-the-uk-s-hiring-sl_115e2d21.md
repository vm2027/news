# Insurance and finance keep adding jobs as the UK's hiring slide runs into a seventh month

> **Source:** Insurance Business UK  
> **Published:** 2026-09-15  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/uk/news/breaking-news/insurance-and-finance-keep-adding-jobs-as-the-uks-hiring-slide-runs-into-a-seventh-month-589799.aspx

## Summary

Official UK labour market data show that overall payrolled employment has declined for seven consecutive months, but the insurance and finance sectors remain among the few areas still adding staff. The article details that sector headcount reached about 1.1 million in August, up roughly 3,900 year-on-year, and contrasts this resilience with falling vacancies and modest real wage growth across the broader economy.
