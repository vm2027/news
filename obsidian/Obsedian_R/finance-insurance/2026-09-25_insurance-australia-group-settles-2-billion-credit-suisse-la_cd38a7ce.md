# Insurance Australia Group settles $2 billion Credit Suisse lawsuit over Greensill

> **Source:** Global Banking & Finance Review  
> **Published:** 2026-09-25  
> **URL:** https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/insurance-australia-group-settles-2-billion-credit-suisse/

## Summary

Insurance Australia Group agreed to settle an approximately A$2.8 billion Credit Suisse lawsuit concerning Greensill-related losses. The insurer said the settlement would not materially affect its 2027 financial results, while its shares rose during the reported session.
