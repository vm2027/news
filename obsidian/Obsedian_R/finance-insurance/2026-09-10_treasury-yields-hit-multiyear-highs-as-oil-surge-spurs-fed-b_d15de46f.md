# Treasury Yields Hit Multiyear Highs as Oil Surge Spurs Fed Bets

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-10 13:18 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-10/us-bond-yields-extend-climb-as-oil-advance-stokes-rate-hike-bets

## Summary

Yields on US government bonds rose to fresh multiyear highs as oil prices extended their surge,
prompting traders to add to bets that the Federal Reserve will raise interest rates as soon as next
week.
