# Coalition Launches Cyber Insurance for Large UK Enterprises

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/09/01/766004.htm

## Summary

Cyber insurer Coalition has introduced new cyber insurance products targeted at large UK enterprises, extending its footprint beyond small and mid-sized businesses. The offering combines active cyber risk monitoring with coverage for ransomware and data breaches, reflecting growing demand for cyber protection among big corporates.
