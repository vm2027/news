# Hot inflation report drops tech, drives S&P 500, Nasdaq down

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-10-14  
> **URL:** https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/live/stock-market-today-hot-inflation-report-drops-tech-drives-sp-500-nasdaq-down-152206632.html

## Summary

U.S. stocks fell after September consumer-price inflation came in hotter than expected, weakening expectations that the Federal Reserve will keep rates unchanged. Bank earnings added to market volatility, with Bank of America declining after a regulatory penalty while BlackRock rose following a stronger-than-expected quarter.
