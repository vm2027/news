# India Cockroach Leaders Remain in Custody as Release Delayed

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-18 01:45 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-18/two-india-cockroach-leaders-set-to-be-freed-as-protests-spread

## Summary

India’s Cockroach Janta Party raised concerns over what it described as “systematic delays and
procedural obstruction” by Delhi Police and other authorities in securing the release of two of its
leaders who have now spent five days in custody.
