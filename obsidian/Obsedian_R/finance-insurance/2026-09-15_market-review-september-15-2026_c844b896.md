## Summary

This market review summarizes performance of major U.S. equity indices on September 15, 2026 and highlights sector-specific developments in financials and insurance. It notes that crypto-related stocks and Bitcoin fell after the U.S. Senate failed to advance the Clarity Act, a comprehensive cryptocurrency bill, and that mortgage insurers weakened following public comments by FHFA head Bill Pulte about borrowers’ ability to drop extra mortgage insurance as home values rise.
