## Summary

Professor James Robinson of the University of Chicago joins Joe Weisenthal and Tracy Alloway to
discuss the role that inclusivity, innovation and creativity play in a successful economy and
whether the US has become a failing state. They spoke on the Odd Lots podcast, recorded live at
Chicago's City Winery. (Source: Bloomberg)
