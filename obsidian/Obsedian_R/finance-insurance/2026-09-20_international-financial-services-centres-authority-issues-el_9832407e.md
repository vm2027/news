# International Financial Services Centres Authority issues Electronic Trading Platforms Regulations, 2026

> **Source:** TeamLease RegTech  
> **Published:** 2026-09-20  
> **URL:** https://teamleaseregtech.com/updates/article/59662/international-financial-services-centres-authority-electronic-trading-/

## Summary

This regulatory update details the International Financial Services Centres Authority’s new Electronic Trading Platforms Regulations, 2026, which came into force on September 18, 2026.[15] The rules establish a framework for electronic trading platforms in IFSCs, governing the trading of eligible securities, money market instruments, foreign exchange, and derivatives to enhance oversight of cross-border financial market infrastructure.[15]
