# Morning Bid: Markets shaken, not stirred, as US-China tech war heats up

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-11-17  
> **URL:** https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/morning-bid-markets-shaken-not-053338144.html

## Summary

Markets came under pressure as new U.S. restrictions on China’s access to advanced AI chips intensified the technology conflict between the two countries. Asian technology indexes fell, while the dollar reached its highest level against a currency basket since April 2025 amid expectations of tighter Federal Reserve policy.
