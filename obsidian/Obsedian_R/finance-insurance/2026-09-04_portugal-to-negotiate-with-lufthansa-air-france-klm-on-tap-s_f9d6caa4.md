# Portugal to Negotiate With Lufthansa, Air France-KLM on TAP Sale

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-04 14:43 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-04/portugal-to-negotiate-with-lufthansa-air-france-klm-on-tap-sale

## Summary

Portugal’s government will enter an additional round of negotiations with Deutsche Lufthansa AG and
Air France-KLM over the sale of a minority stake in TAP as it seeks to secure improved offers from
both airline groups.
