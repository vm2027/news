# Iceland Rejects Restarting EU Membership Talks

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-30 12:44 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-30/iceland-rejects-restarting-eu-membership-talks-video

## Summary

Icelandic voters rejected restarting EU accession talks, with opposition particularly strong outside
Reykjavik as concerns over sovereignty, fisheries and agriculture outweighed arguments for closer
European ties. RÚV Iceland News Defense & Security Correspondent Ingólfur Sigfússon is on Bloomberg
This Weekend and says says the result puts membership talks on hold and could shift attention toward
Norway as Nordic countries reassess their relationships with Europe amid geopolitical uncertainty.
