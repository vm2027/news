# Insurance Industry Labor Market Study Shows Slowing but Still Positive Growth

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-08-27  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/national/2026/08/27/883108.htm

## Summary

A new labor market study of the U.S. insurance industry finds that while growth is slowing, most carriers still anticipate modest revenue increases over the next year. Around 78% of insurers expect revenue growth and roughly half plan to expand staffing, although hiring intentions are more moderate than in previous cycles. The results highlight an industry that remains fundamentally healthy but is becoming more cautious about expansion amid macroeconomic and regulatory uncertainty.
