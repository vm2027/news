# Bond yields fall, stocks rally as Fed's Waller comments curb rate-hike fears

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-09-03  
> **URL:** https://www.reuters.com/world/china/global-markets-global-markets-2026-09-03/

## Summary

Stocks rallied and bond yields eased after comments from Federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller reduced expectations for an imminent rate hike. The report says market pricing shifted lower for a September Fed increase.
