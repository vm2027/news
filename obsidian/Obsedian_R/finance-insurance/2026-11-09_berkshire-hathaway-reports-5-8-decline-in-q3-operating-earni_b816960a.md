# Berkshire Hathaway Reports 5.8% Decline in Q3 Operating Earnings to $12.71 Billion

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-11-09  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/berkshire-hathaway-reports-5-8-113543502.html

## Summary

Berkshire Hathaway’s third-quarter operating earnings fell 5.8% to $12.705 billion. Insurance underwriting earnings dropped about 43%, while insurance investment income declined roughly 14% from the prior year.
