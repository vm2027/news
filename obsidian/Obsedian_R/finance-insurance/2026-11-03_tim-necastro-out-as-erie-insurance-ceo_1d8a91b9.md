# Tim NeCastro out as Erie Insurance CEO

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-11-03  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/tim-necastro-erie-insurance-ceo-151727521.html?fr=sycsrp_catchall

## Summary

Tim NeCastro stepped down as Erie Insurance’s president and chief executive immediately, earlier than his planned retirement at the end of 2026. Executive Vice President, Secretary, and General Counsel Brian Bolash was named interim CEO while the board begins a search for a permanent successor.
