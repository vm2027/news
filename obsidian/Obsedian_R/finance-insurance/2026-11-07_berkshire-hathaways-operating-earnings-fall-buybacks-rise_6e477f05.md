# Berkshire Hathaway’s Operating Earnings Fall, Buybacks Rise

> **Source:** Bloomberg Law  
> **Published:** 2026-11-07  
> **URL:** https://news.bloomberglaw.com/insurance/berkshire-hathaways-operating-earnings-fall-buybacks-rise

## Summary

Berkshire Hathaway spent approximately $9.6 billion repurchasing shares during the third quarter. Operating earnings fell to $12.7 billion as insurance underwriting declined 43%, partly because Geico experienced higher losses and underwriting expenses.
