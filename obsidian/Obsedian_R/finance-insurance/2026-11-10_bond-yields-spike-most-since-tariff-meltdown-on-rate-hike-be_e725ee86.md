# Bond Yields Spike Most Since Tariff Meltdown on Rate-Hike Bets

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-11-10 13:41 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/treasuries-slide-as-hot-us-inflation-cements-december-rate-bets

## Summary

The US bond market tumbled, sending short-term yields surging by the most in 19 months, after
consumer prices rose at a faster-than-expected pace and Federal Reserve officials warned the central
bank may need to increase interest rates more aggressively.
