# US-Iran Clashes Hit Stocks as Oil Rises

> **Source:** Bloomberg  
> **Published:** 2026-09-02  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-02/us-iran-clashes-hit-stocks-as-oil-rises-video

## Summary

Renewed hostilities between the U.S. and Iran have pushed oil prices higher and weighed on global equities, with futures and major indexes falling as traders reassess risk. Analysts say the tensions complicate central bank policy choices, with higher energy costs feeding inflation while bond yields remain elevated on expectations of further rate hikes.
