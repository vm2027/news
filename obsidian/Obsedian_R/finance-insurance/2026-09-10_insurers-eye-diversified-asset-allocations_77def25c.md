# Insurers eye diversified asset allocations

> **Source:** China Daily  
> **Published:** 2026-09-10  
> **URL:** https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202609/10/WS6aa2001de4b06d4aa055d396.html

## Summary

China’s five major A-share listed insurers reported strong first-half 2026 earnings, driven largely by improved investment returns and higher equity exposure.[14] The article explains that the industry is accelerating a shift toward more diversified asset allocation, as insurers seek yield in a low interest rate environment while overall sector premium income continues to grow.[14]
