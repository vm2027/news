# CFOs Revamp Strategies in Face of AI

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-24 21:44 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-24/cfos-face-a-tougher-call-on-when-to-borrow-video

## Summary

Mozilla, known for its Firefox web browser and the Mozilla VPN, has set itself no lesser goal than
to further the health of the internet. That mission is particularly relevant as artificial
intelligence changes the way the internet works and how people use it. The software firm — which is
a subsidiary of Mozilla.org, a nonprofit that promotes openness, innovation and participation on the
internet — recently appointed a new chief executive to help revamp its strategy. Eric Muhlheim, who
became CFO in 2021 after stints at Buzzfeed and Disney, talks about balancing mission and returns,
diversifying Mozilla’s revenue streams and AI costs. Mozilla CFO Eric Muhlheim and Bloomberg's Nina
Trentmann discuss the impact of AI on capex spend, corporate strategy, and company principles in our
CFO Briefing. They speak with Bloomberg Businessweek Daily hosts Carol Massar and Tim Stenovec.
