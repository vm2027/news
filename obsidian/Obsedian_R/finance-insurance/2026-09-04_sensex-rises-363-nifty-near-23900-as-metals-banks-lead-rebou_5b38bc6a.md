> **Source:** HDFCSky

## Summary

The report describes how Indian benchmarks Sensex and Nifty snapped a four-session losing streak on September 4, closing modestly higher on strength in metal and banking stocks. It notes that life insurers SBI Life and HDFC Life were among the top Nifty gainers, reflecting investor interest in the insurance segment within the broader market rebound.
