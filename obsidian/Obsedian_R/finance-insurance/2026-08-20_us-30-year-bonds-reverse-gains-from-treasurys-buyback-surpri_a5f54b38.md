# US 30-Year Bonds Reverse Gains From Treasury’s Buyback Surprise

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-20 09:23 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/bessent-s-plan-at-best-circuit-breaker-for-global-bond-slump

## Summary

US Treasuries fell a day after the Trump administration’s surprise decision to increase buybacks of
longer-dated bonds, showing the move did little to counter angst about the surging government debt
that pushed some yields to a 19-year high.
