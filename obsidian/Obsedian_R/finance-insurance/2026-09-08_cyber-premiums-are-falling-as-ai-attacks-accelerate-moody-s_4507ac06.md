# Cyber premiums are falling as AI attacks accelerate – Moody's

> **Source:** Insurance Business / Moody's analysis  
> **Published:** 2026-09-08  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/sitemap/2026-09/cyber-premiums-are-falling-as-ai-attacks-accelerate--moodys-497868.aspx

## Summary

Moody's research shows that cyber insurance premiums are declining even as AI‑enabled cyberattacks become more frequent and sophisticated. The report suggests increased competition, improved risk selection and alternative risk transfer are pressuring prices, raising questions about the sustainability of underwriting margins in the cyber line.
