# Earnings, Not the Fed, Are Driving Stocks: UBS's Lovell

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-18 17:26 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-18/earnings-not-the-fed-are-driving-stocks-ubs-s-lovell-video

## Summary

Nadia Lovell, head of global equity strategy at UBS Global Wealth Management, says strong earnings
and broadening profit growth should keep stocks moving higher even as rising rates create near-term
volatility. “What’s gonna drive this market is earnings. It’s not valuation expansion,” Lovell says
on “Bloomberg Surveillance.” (Source: Bloomberg)
