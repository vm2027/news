# Tata Sons, Tata Trusts Query Insurance Companies on Potential Liability Claims

> **Source:** Bloomberg  
> **Published:** 2026-10-22  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-22/tata-sons-tata-trusts-query-insurance-companies-on-potential-liability-claims

## Summary

Tata Sons and Tata Trusts are consulting insurers about potential coverage for claims arising from their dispute over control of the Indian conglomerate. The discussions reportedly focus on legal-expense protection under directors-and-officers liability policies.
