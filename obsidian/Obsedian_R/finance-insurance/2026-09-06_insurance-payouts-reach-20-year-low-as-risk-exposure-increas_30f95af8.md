# Insurance Payouts Reach 20-Year Low as Risk Exposure Increases

> **Source:** Big Market Moves  
> **Published:** 2026-09-06  
> **URL:** https://www.bigmarketmoves.com/latest-news/insurance-payouts-reach-20-year-low-as-risk-exposure-increases

## Summary

Insurance companies’ total payouts have fallen to their lowest level in two decades even as they take on greater risk exposure, boosting short-term profitability and investable capital. Firms are using the additional capital to seek higher returns in financial markets, which could increase earnings volatility and investor risk. The shift in underwriting strategy is significant for investors in insurance stocks and for broader market stability.
