## Summary

This market update outlines hiring, M&A and strategic trends across the UK general insurance market, noting that insurance and finance vacancies have fallen to a four‑year low even as brokers continue recruiting. It also covers private equity’s focus on specialty and MGA businesses, the creation of Gallagher Re’s digital risk unit, and key executive moves at Aon, HSBC and Arch Insurance.
