# Insurance pricing coefficient ranges to be aligned between NEVs and fossil-fuel vehicles

> **Source:** Asia Insurance Review  
> **Published:** 2026-09-18  
> **URL:** https://www.asiainsurancereview.com/News/View-NewsLetter-Article/id/96940/type/eCin/Conferences/Gallery

## Summary

Chinese regulators are moving to harmonize auto insurance pricing coefficient ranges for new-energy vehicles and traditional fossil-fuel cars, aiming to reduce distortions and ensure fair competition in motor insurance. The change is part of a broader effort to refine pricing mechanisms, better reflect risk profiles, and support sustainable growth in both the insurance sector and the rapidly expanding EV market.
