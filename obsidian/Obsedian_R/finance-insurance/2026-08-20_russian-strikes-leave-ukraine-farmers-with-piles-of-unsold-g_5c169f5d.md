## Summary

More than four years ago, Ravil Dzhamally fled his farm in Ukraine’s Kherson region after it was
overrun by invading Russian troops. When Moscow’s forces were driven back, he returned to clear his
fields of mines and plant wheat, barley and sunflowers.
