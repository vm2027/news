# Investor Sentiment Plunges to 16-Month Low, AAII Survey Shows

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-18 17:19 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-18/investor-sentiment-plunges-to-16-month-low-aaii-survey-shows

## Summary

Investor sentiment fell to its lowest point since 2025, a weekly survey from the American
Association of Individual Investors showed, as oil prices remained elevated while the Federal
Reserve raised interest rates for the first time since 2023.
