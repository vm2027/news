# Michigan Joins Settlement Against Mortgage Servicer Over ‘Force-Placed’ Insurance

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/midwest/2026/09/01/779006.htm

## Summary

Michigan’s insurance regulator joined 48 other states in a settlement with a mortgage servicer accused of improperly using ‘force-placed’ insurance on borrowers.[1] The agreement addresses allegations that the servicer overcharged homeowners by placing costly insurance policies when coverage lapsed, raising consumer protection concerns.[1]
