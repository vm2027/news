# US halts Obamacare enrollment for over 760,000 enrollees amid fraud crackdown

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-09-22  
> **URL:** https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-says-it-canceled-315000-obamacare-policies-last-month-2026-09-22/

## Summary

The Trump administration is halting enrollment in Obamacare plans for more than 760,000 people it believes were fraudulently enrolled. Vice President JD Vance said the anti-fraud effort could save taxpayers approximately $2.2 billion.
