# 五大險企銀保渠道分化加劇！平安保費增長249億元，3家險企負增長

> **Source:** Futunn News  
> **Published:** 2026-09-03  
> **URL:** https://news.futunn.com/hk/post/78759840/divergence-intensifies-in-the-bancassurance-channel-among-top-five-insurers

## Summary

The article analyzes the diverging performance of bancassurance channels among China’s top five insurers in their 2026 interim results. It highlights that Ping An recorded premium growth of 24.9 billion yuan via bank partnerships, while three peer insurers experienced negative premium growth, and notes that on the banking side, lower volume but higher pricing has become the main theme, with institutions like China Merchants Bank and Ping An Bank seeing insurance distribution income grow despite shrinking premium volume.
