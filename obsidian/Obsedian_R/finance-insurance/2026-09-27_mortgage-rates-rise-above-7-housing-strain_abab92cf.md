# Mortgage Rates Rise Above 7% - Housing Strain

> **Source:** Briefs  
> **Published:** 2026-09-27

## Summary

The average U.S. 30-year fixed mortgage rate rose above 7% for the first time since January 2025. Freddie Mac attributed the increase to renewed war-related concerns and inflation fears, adding pressure to housing affordability.
