# See 'Skittishness' Around Credit: Annie Seelaus

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-01 19:24 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-10-01/see-skittishness-around-credit-annie-seelaus-video

## Summary

Annie Seelaus, CEO of R. Seelaus, and John Lloyd, global head of multi-sector and corporate credit
at Janus Henderson, join Emily Graffeo on "Bloomberg Real Yield." The spread on the riskiest US
corporate bonds has jumped above 1,000 basis points over Treasuries for the first time since the
regional banking crisis in 2023. That level of risk premium typically implies high probability of
default, restructuring or loss. (Source: Bloomberg)
