# Wall Street drops to its worst day since June after the sell-off

> **Source:** LNP | LancasterOnline  
> **Published:** 2026-10-30  
> **URL:** https://lancasteronline.com/business/stock_market/global-trading-is-mixed-as-worries-about-ai-related-issues-grow/article_846f90f8-5646-56b2-b3d0-fba510034e.html

## Summary

The S&P 500 fell 1.8% in its worst session since June, while the Nasdaq dropped 2.9% and the Dow declined 1.1%. Technology weakness and a reported hack affecting the largest cryptocurrency exchange drove risk aversion, while Treasury yields eased and oil prices rose.
