## Summary

This weekly report outlines key regulatory developments in the global insurance industry, focusing on the EU where insurers face overlapping implementation timelines for the Solvency II review, the Insurance Recovery and Resolution Directive (IRRD), and the January 2027 risk-free rate (RFR) deadline. It emphasizes that the regulatory drafting phase is largely complete, shifting focus to execution and compliance, which will materially affect capital, risk management, and product strategies for European insurers.
