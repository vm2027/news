# Vingroup Among Vietnam Stocks to Debut on FTSE’s EM Index

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-21 13:50 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-21/vingroup-among-vietnam-stocks-to-make-debut-on-ftse-s-em-index

## Summary

Vingroup JSC is among over 100 Vietnamese companies added to FTSE Russell’s Global Equity Indexes
late Friday, marking a key step in the country’s long-awaited transition from frontier to secondary
emerging market status.
