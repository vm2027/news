# Nepal Rescuers Race to Find Thousands Missing

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-29 12:41 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-29/nepal-rescuers-race-to-find-thousands-missing-video

## Summary

Rescue and recovery operations are continuing in Nepal after a glacial collapse triggered
catastrophic flooding near the border with China, killing more than 600 people and leaving thousands
missing. Bloomberg News Senior Reporter Dan Strumpf joins Christina Ruffini and Bailey Lipschultz on
Bloomberg This Weekend and reports that destroyed roads and bridges are complicating efforts to
reach isolated communities as Nepal seeks specialized international assistance for tunnel rescues,
victim identification and recovery. (Source: Bloomberg)
