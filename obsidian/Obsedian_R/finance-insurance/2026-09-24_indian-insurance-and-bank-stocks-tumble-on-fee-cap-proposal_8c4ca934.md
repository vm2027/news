# Indian Insurance and Bank Stocks Tumble on Fee Cap Proposal

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-09-24  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/09/24/886672.htm

## Summary

Indian insurers, digital brokers, and banks suffered losses after the insurance regulator proposed commission caps and tighter limits on management expenses. Analysts said the changes could cut insurance fee income by as much as 90% in some high-margin categories.
