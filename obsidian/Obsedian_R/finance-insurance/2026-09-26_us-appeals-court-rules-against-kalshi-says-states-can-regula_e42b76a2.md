# US appeals court rules against Kalshi, says states can regulate prediction markets

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-09-26  
> **URL:** https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-appeals-court-rules-against-kalshi-says-states-can-regulate-prediction-markets-2026-09-26/

## Summary

A U.S. appeals court ruled that states can regulate prediction markets, dealing a setback to Kalshi's effort to operate under federal oversight alone. The decision could affect the legal framework governing event-based financial contracts.
