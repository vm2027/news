# Abercrombie Soars on Guidance Lift, Flagship Brand Growth

## Summary

Abercrombie & Fitch Co. shares rallied after revenue topped estimates and the retailer raised its
annual earnings guidance, suggesting the preppy apparel company is regaining some momentum even
after another quarter of slow sales growth. Bloomberg's Mary Ross Gilbert joined Bloomberg
Intelligence to discuss their earnings. (Source: Bloomberg)
