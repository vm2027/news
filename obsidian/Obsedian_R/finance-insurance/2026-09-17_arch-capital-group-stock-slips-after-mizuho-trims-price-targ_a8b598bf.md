# Arch Capital Group stock slips after Mizuho trims price target

> **Source:** Ad-hoc News  
> **Published:** 2026-09-17  
> **URL:** https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/corporate-news/arch-capital-group-stock-slips-after-mizuho-trims-price-target/70116776

## Summary

Shares of Bermuda-based insurer Arch Capital Group traded below USD 100 after Mizuho lowered its price target to USD 100, below the wider consensus target of roughly USD 111.87. The article frames the move within broader sector valuations, noting that analysts still see double-digit upside potential if earnings expectations are met.
