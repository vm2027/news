# Nasdaq starts November with 1% loss as tech melts down, Dow gains more than 200 points

> **Source:** CNBC  
> **Published:** 2026-11-01  
> **URL:** https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/01/stock-market-today-live-updates.html

## Summary

The Nasdaq and S&P 500 declined as investors reduced exposure to technology stocks and rotated into other sectors. The Dow outperformed, gaining more than 200 points, while the major indexes gave back sizable early-session advances.
