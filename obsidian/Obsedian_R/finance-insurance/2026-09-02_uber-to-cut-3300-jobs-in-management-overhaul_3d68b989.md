# Uber to Cut 3,300 Jobs in Management Overhaul

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-02 14:11 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-02/uber-to-cut-3-300-jobs-in-management-overhaul-video

## Summary

Uber Technologies is cutting about 3,300 roles, or 10% of its staff globally, in a restructuring
aimed at reducing management layers and reallocating spending into its ride-sharing, delivery and
robotaxi businesses. Carmen Reinicke has more on "Bloomberg Open Interest." (Source: Bloomberg)
