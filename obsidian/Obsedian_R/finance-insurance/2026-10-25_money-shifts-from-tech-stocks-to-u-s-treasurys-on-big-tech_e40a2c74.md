# Money Shifts From Tech Stocks to U.S. Treasurys on Big Tech

> **Source:** Seoul Economic Daily  
> **Published:** 2026-10-25  
> **URL:** https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/10/25/money-shifts-from-tech-stocks-to-us-treasurys-on-big-tech

## Summary

Investors moved from technology stocks into U.S. Treasurys as concerns about Big Tech’s AI capital spending increased risk aversion. The 10-year Treasury yield fell below 5%, while credit-risk concerns intensified around large technology companies.
