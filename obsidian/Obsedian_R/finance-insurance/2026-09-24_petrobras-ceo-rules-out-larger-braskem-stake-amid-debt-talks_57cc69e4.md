# Petrobras CEO Rules Out Larger Braskem Stake Amid Debt Talks

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-24 16:34 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-24/petrobras-executive-says-braskem-to-reach-deal-with-creditors

## Summary

Petrobras SA Chief Executive Officer Magda Chambriard ruled out raising the state-controlled oil
producer’s stake in troubled petrochemicals firm Braskem SA, saying any additional capital must be
matched by other investors.
