# CVS to split into two companies, in major breakup of its $117 billion healthcare empire

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-11-04  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/cvs-split-two-companies-major-134013060.html

## Summary

CVS Health announced plans to separate its businesses into two companies, reversing the earlier combination of its retail pharmacy, Aetna insurance, and Caremark pharmacy-benefit operations. The company also reported higher third-quarter revenue and earnings, led largely by improved performance in its Health Care Benefits segment.
