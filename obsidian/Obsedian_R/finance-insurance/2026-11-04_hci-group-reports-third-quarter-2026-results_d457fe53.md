# HCI Group Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-11-04  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/hci-group-reports-third-quarter-211500840.html

## Summary

HCI Group reported third-quarter pre-tax income of $116 million and net income of $86 million, up from $91 million and $68 million respectively a year earlier. Gross premiums earned rose 5% to $316 million as the number of policies in force increased.
