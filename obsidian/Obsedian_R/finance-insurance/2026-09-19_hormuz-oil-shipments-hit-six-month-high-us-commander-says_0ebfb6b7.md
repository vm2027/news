# Hormuz Oil Shipments Hit Six-Month High, US Commander Says

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-19 14:47 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-19/hormuz-oil-shipments-hit-six-month-high-us-commander-says

## Summary

Oil and liquefied natural gas shipments through the Strait of Hormuz in the past two weeks reached
the highest level in six months, signaling that US naval protection and mine clearance efforts are
“paying off,” a regional US commander said.
