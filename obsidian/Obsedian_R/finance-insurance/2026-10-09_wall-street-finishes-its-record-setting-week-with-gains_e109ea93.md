# Wall Street finishes its record-setting week with gains

> **Source:** WHDH / Associated Press  
> **Published:** 2026-10-09  
> **URL:** https://whdh.com/news/wall-street-finishes-its-record-setting-week-with-gains/

## Summary

U.S. stocks ended a record-setting week higher, with the S&P 500 gaining 0.6%, the Dow rising 0.8%, and the Nasdaq advancing 0.6%. Humana rose 11.6% after reporting encouraging data on its Medicare Advantage quality ratings, helping lift the broader market.
