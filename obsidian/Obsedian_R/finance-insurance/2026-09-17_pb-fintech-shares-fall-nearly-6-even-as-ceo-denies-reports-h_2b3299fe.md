## Summary

PB Fintech, the parent of Policybazaar, saw its shares drop nearly 6% amid market speculation about the CEO’s possible exit, which the executive has publicly denied. The article also notes broader sector pressure as IRDAI prepares a consultation paper on insurance commissions that could reduce banks’ commission income while modestly affecting retail insurance products.
