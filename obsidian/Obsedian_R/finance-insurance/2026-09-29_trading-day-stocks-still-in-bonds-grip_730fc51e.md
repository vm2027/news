# Trading Day: Stocks still in bonds' grip

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-09-29  
> **URL:** https://www.reuters.com/commentary/reuters-open-interest/global-markets-trading-day-graphic-2026-09-29/

## Summary

Global equities declined as elevated bond yields weighed on investor sentiment ahead of U.S. inflation data and potential quarter-end volatility. The U.S. 30-year yield reached its highest level since 2002, while the French-German bond spread widened to a 14-year high.
