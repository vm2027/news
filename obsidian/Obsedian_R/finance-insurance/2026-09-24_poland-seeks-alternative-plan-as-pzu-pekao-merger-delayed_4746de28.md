# Poland Seeks Alternative Plan as PZU-Pekao Merger Delayed

> **Source:** Global Banking & Finance Review  
> **Published:** 2026-09-24  
> **URL:** https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/poland-seeks-alternative-plan-delayed-pzu-pekao-insurance/

## Summary

Poland is exploring an alternative structure for reorganizing insurer PZU after legislative changes needed for the planned PZU-Pekao merger faced political obstacles. The proposed holding-based approach could require fewer amendments and help preserve state control.
