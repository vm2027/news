# Chemicals, Construction, Insurance Earnings Outlooks Jump

> **Source:** Seoul Economic Daily  
> **Published:** 2026-09-09  
> **URL:** https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/09/09/chemicals-construction-insurance-earnings-outlooks-jump

## Summary

Net profit forecasts for 2026 and 2027 have been revised upward for many KOSPI sectors, with the insurance sector’s outlook for next year surging 13.3% in a single month, the largest gain among all groups.[9] The article attributes improved insurance earnings expectations to better investment returns from rising long-term interest rates, even as the KOSPI struggles to hold above the 7,000 level.[9]
