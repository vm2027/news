# NGX edges lower as insurance gains soften market losses

> **Source:** Punch  
> **Published:** 2026-10-05  
> **URL:** https://punchng.com/ngx-edges-lower-as-insurance-gains-soften-market-losses/

## Summary

Nigeria’s NGX index edged lower, although gains in insurance stocks helped limit the market’s decline. Sovereign Trust Insurance was the leading gainer, rising 10% as financial-services stocks dominated trading volume.
