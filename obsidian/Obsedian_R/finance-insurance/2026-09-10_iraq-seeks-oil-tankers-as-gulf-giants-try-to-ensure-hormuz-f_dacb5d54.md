# Iraq Seeks Oil Tankers as Gulf Giants Try to Ensure Hormuz Flows

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-10 11:12 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-10/iraq-tendering-for-two-oil-supertankers-to-go-through-hormuz

## Summary

Iraq is holding a tender to hire two or more oil supertankers to perform transits through the Strait
of Hormuz, the latest evidence that producers in the region are trying to take greater control of
their exports through the chokepoint.
