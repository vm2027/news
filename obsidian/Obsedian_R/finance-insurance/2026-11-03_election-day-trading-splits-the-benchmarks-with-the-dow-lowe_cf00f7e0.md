# Election Day Trading Splits the Benchmarks, With the Dow Lower and the Nasdaq Higher

## Summary

Election Day trading saw the S&P 500, Nasdaq, and Russell 2000 rise while the Dow declined. Treasury yields were higher, and investors also monitored the government’s expected borrowing needs and the Federal Reserve’s recent decision to keep rates unchanged.
