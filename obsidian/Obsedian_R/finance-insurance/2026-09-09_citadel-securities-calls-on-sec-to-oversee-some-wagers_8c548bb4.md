# Citadel Securities Calls on SEC to Oversee Some Wagers

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-09 22:18 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-09/citadel-securities-urges-sec-to-take-oversight-of-some-wagers

## Summary

Citadel Securities told regulators that prediction market contracts linked to publicly-traded
companies should be overseen by the Securities and Exchange Commission instead of the agency that
has taken responsibility for them so far, the Commodity Futures Trading Commission.
