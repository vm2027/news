# Sweden to Donate 10 Coast Guard Vessels to Ukraine

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-27 15:35 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-27/sweden-to-donate-10-coast-guard-vessels-to-ukraine

## Summary

Sweden will donate 10 coast guard boats to Ukraine to be used for water-based rescue operations,
including mine clearance, the Nordic country’s government said.
