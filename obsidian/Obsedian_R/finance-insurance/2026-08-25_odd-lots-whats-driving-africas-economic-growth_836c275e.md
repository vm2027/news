# Odd Lots: What’s Driving Africa’s Economic Growth?

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-25 16:00 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-25/odd-lots-what-s-driving-africa-s-economic-growth-video

## Summary

Africa has seen lots of economic growth in the last few years. According to the United Nations,
growth across the continent is expected to reach 4.0% in 2026. Joe Studwell, author of How Africa
Works: Success and Failure on the World's Last Developmental Frontier, joins Tracy Alloway and Joe
Weisenthal on the Odd Lots podcast to discuss what is driving this economic growth and how it
compares to Asian counterparts. (Source: Bloomberg)
