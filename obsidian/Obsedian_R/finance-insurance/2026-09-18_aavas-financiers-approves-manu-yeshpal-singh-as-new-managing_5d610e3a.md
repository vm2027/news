# Aavas Financiers Approves Manu Yeshpal Singh as New Managing Director

> **Source:** SAHI News  
> **Published:** 2026-09-18  
> **URL:** https://www.sahi.com/news/aavas-financiers-approves-manu-yeshpal-singh-as-new-managing-director

## Summary

Aavas Financiers’ board has approved Manu Yeshpal Singh as the company’s new Managing Director and CEO for a five-year term starting September 19, 2026, following regulatory clearance from the Reserve Bank of India. The appointment is seen as a significant leadership change for the housing finance company, with potential implications for its strategic direction, lending growth, and risk management in India’s retail credit markets.
