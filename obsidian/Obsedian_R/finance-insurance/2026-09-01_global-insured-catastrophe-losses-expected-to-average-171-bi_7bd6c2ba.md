# Global Insured Catastrophe Losses Expected to Average $171 Billion Annually: Verisk

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/09/01/766001.htm

## Summary

Verisk projects that global insured catastrophe losses will average about $171 billion per year, underscoring rising climate and disaster risks for property and casualty insurers. The analysis suggests insurers must prepare for higher volatility and capital strain, with implications for pricing, reinsurance demand, and regulatory scrutiny.
