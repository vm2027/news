# Global Commercial Insurance Rates Drop 5% in Q3

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-10-21  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/national/2026/10/21/889818.htm

## Summary

Global commercial insurance rates fell 5% in the third quarter, marking a ninth consecutive quarterly decline, according to Marsh. Cyber rates dropped 4%, extending their streak of declines to 13 quarters.
