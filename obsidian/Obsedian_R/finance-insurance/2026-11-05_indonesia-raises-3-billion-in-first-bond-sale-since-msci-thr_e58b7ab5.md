# Indonesia Raises $3 Billion in First Bond Sale Since MSCI Threat

## Summary

Indonesia sold $3 billion of dollar bonds on Thursday, its first such issuance since MSCI Inc.
warned it may remove the nation’s equities from its global indexes.
