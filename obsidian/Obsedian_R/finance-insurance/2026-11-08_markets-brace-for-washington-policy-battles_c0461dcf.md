# Markets Brace for Washington Policy Battles

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-11-08 16:17 UTC

## Summary

On Bloomberg This Weekend, SMBC Nikko Securities Chief Economist Joe Lavorgna says affordability
helped drive the midterm results but argues the underlying US economy remains healthy and major
policy changes may be limited by divided government. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina
Ruffini, Lavorgna discusses the outlook for AI investment, inflation and markets as Washington
prepares for two years of potential policy. (Source: Bloomberg)
