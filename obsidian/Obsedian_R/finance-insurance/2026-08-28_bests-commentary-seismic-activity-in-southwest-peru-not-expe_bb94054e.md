# Best’s Commentary: Seismic Activity in Southwest Peru Not Expected to Generate Severe Insured Losses

> **Source:** Business Wire / AM Best  
> **Published:** 2026-08-28  
> **URL:** https://www.businesswire.com/newsroom/industry/professional-services/insurance

## Summary

AM Best comments that a recent earthquake in southwest Peru is unlikely to result in severe insured losses due to low insurance penetration in the affected region. The piece discusses the broader implications for reinsurers and primary insurers, highlighting how limited coverage in high-risk areas constrains loss exposure but underscores protection gaps in emerging markets.
