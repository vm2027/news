# Insurers pay N347bn claims despite N10.9bn premium decline

> **Source:** Vanguard Nigeria  
> **Published:** 2026-10-09  
> **URL:** https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/10/insurers-pay-n347bn-claims-despite-n10-9bn-premium-decline/

## Summary

Nigerian insurers paid ₦346.87 billion in claims during the first half of 2026, a slight increase from the prior year. The increase occurred despite a 1.8% decline in gross written premiums, raising concerns about pressure on underwriting margins.
