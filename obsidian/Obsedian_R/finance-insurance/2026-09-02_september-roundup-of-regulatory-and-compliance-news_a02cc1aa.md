# September roundup of regulatory and compliance news

> **Source:** QA Financial  
> **Published:** 2026-09-02

## Summary

This regulatory roundup details how the Financial Stability Board has flagged frontier AI’s impact on cyber risk as the most immediate AI-related threat to the financial system. It covers new consultations on operational risk frameworks under the EU’s Capital Requirements Regulation, the EU Cyber Resilience Act’s upcoming reporting obligations, strengthened AI Act enforcement, and the UK FCA’s selection of firms for its Supercharged Sandbox, highlighting evolving compliance demands on financial institutions.
