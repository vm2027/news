# Paschi Top 10 Investor Goldstone Backs Intesa Takeover Bid

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-16 16:13 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-16/paschi-top-10-investor-goldstone-backs-intesa-takeover-bid

## Summary

Goldstone Capital Limited, a shareholder in Banca Monte dei Paschi di Siena SpA backed by Qatari
investors, said it supports Intesa Sanpaolo SpA’s takeover bid for the Tuscan lender, emerging as
the latest investor to support the deal.
