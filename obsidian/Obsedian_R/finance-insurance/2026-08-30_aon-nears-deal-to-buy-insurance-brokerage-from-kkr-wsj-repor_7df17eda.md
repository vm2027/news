# Aon Nears Deal to Buy Insurance Brokerage From KKR, WSJ Reports

> **Source:** Bloomberg  
> **Published:** 2026-08-30  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-30/aon-nears-deal-to-buy-insurance-brokerage-from-kkr-wsj-reports

## Summary

Aon is reportedly close to an agreement to acquire insurance brokerage USI Insurance Services from private equity firm KKR for about $17 billion, including debt. The deal, based on sources cited by the Wall Street Journal, could be announced as soon as Monday and would significantly expand Aon's brokerage footprint.
