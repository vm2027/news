# Nicolas Cage's Malibu sinkhole exposes a coverage gap most homeowners don't know exists

> **Source:** Insurance Business US  
> **Published:** 2026-09-10  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/breaking-news/nicolas-cages-malibu-sinkhole-exposes-a-coverage-gap-most-homeowners-dont-know-exists-5888yy.aspx

## Summary

Damage to Nicolas Cage’s Malibu property from a sinkhole has highlighted a little-known coverage gap in standard homeowners’ insurance policies. The article explains that many policies exclude certain forms of earth movement or land subsidence, leaving homeowners exposed to significant losses unless they purchase specialised endorsements. The case underscores growing concerns about property risk coverage in high-value markets amid increasing climate and geological events.
