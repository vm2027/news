# Executives Take Pay Cuts Over Staff Misconduct at Prudential Japan

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-10-09  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/10/09/888560.htm

## Summary

Senior executives at Prudential Financial’s Japan unit will take 30% pay cuts for three months following an investigation into widespread employee misconduct involving customer funds. The investigation identified structural problems in governance and the business model, while Japan’s financial regulator is considering penalties, including a possible temporary business suspension.
