# Inside the Enrollment Cliff Hitting US Colleges

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-04 14:04 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-10-04/inside-the-enrollment-cliff-hitting-us-colleges-video

## Summary

America’s college-age population is shrinking, putting pressure on universities with high fixed
costs and fewer students to fill their campuses. Schools like Bowling Green State are responding by
adding career-focused programs and trying to stand out in a crowded market, while institutions that
fail to adapt are facing cuts, closures and economic fallout for the towns that depend on them.
