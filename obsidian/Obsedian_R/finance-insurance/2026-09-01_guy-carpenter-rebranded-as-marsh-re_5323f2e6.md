# Guy Carpenter Rebranded as Marsh Re

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/national/2026/09/01/779003.htm

## Summary

Reinsurance broker Guy Carpenter has been rebranded as Marsh Re, aligning the operation more closely with parent company Marsh McLennan.[1] The move reflects a strategy to integrate brokerage, advisory, and reinsurance services under a unified brand to better serve global insurance and capital markets clients.[1]
