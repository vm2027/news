# Insurance Loss Estimates for Microsoft Outage Range From $100 Million to $700 Million

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-11-16  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/national/2026/11/16/892737.htm

## Summary

Industry observers estimated global cyber-insured losses from the Microsoft 365 outage at between $100 million and $700 million. The outage occurred from November 12 through November 14 and affected insured businesses worldwide.
