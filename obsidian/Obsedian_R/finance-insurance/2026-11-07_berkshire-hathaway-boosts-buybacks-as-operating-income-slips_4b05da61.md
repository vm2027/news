# Berkshire Hathaway Boosts Buybacks as Operating Income Slips

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-11-07 13:01 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-07/berkshire-hathaway-s-operating-earnings-fall-buybacks-rise

## Summary

Berkshire Hathaway Inc. spent about $9.6 billion to buy back its own shares in the third quarter, a
sign the company’s executives viewed the stock as undervalued. But declining insurance underwriting
weighed on its operating earnings in a mixed quarter for the company.
