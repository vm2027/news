# Federal Judge Cautions State Farm Against Gambling With Courts

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/southeast/2026/09/01/766008.htm

## Summary

A federal judge in Alabama sharply criticized State Farm’s litigation strategy in a coverage dispute, warning the insurer against “gambling” with court processes. The order signals judicial impatience with aggressive defense tactics in insurance cases and could influence how carriers manage contentious claims in the Southeast.
