# How China’s Intelligence Operations Target Tech

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-11-14 15:41 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-11-14/how-china-s-intelligence-operations-target-tech-video

## Summary

Former CIA counterintelligence analyst Peter Mattis tells Bloomberg This Weekend how China’s
Ministry of State Security can use business and personal relationships to gather intelligence and
potentially influence decision-making in strategically important industries. Speaking with hosts
David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Mattis explains why technology executives can become valuable
targets even without access to classified information and how AI is expanding the capabilities of
Chinese intelligence operations. (Source: Bloomberg)
