# Global Insured Catastrophe Losses Fall to Lowest 9-Month Level Since 2019: Report

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-10-21  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/10/21/889834.htm

## Summary

Global insured natural-catastrophe losses totaled $92 billion during the first nine months of 2026, according to Gallagher Re. The figure was 29% below the 10-year average and marked the lowest year-to-date total since 2019.
