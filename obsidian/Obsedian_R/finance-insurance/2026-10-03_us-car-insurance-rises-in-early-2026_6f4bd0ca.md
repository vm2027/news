# US Car Insurance Rises in Early 2026

> **Source:** Briefs  
> **Published:** 2026-10-03  
> **URL:** https://www.briefs.co/news/car-insurance-turns-higher-in-early-2026-after-last-year-s-b/

## Summary

Average U.S. full-coverage car insurance premiums rose 1% to $2,237 during the first half of 2026 after falling in 2025. Increases were recorded in 27 states, and Insurify projects that 32 states will finish the year with higher premiums.
