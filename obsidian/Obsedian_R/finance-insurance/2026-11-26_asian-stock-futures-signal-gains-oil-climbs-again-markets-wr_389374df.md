## Summary

Asian stocks may look to build on a tech rally that powered gains in US stock futures on Thursday,
with signs of a revival in risk appetite lifting assets including copper and Bitcoin. Oil climbed
after Yemen’s Houthi rebels struck a key Saudi Refinery.
