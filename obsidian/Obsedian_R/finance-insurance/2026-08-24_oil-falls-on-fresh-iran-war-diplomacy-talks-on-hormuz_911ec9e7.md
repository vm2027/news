# Oil Falls on Fresh Iran War Diplomacy, Talks on Hormuz

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-24 22:02 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-aug-25

## Summary

Oil extended declines as fresh diplomatic efforts helped renew optimism the Iran war would not
escalate, while a US plan to ramp up economic pressure on Tehran spared the country’s trading
partners from harsher measures for now.
