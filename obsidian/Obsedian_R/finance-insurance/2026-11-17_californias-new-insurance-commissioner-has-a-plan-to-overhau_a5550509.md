# California’s new insurance commissioner has a plan to overhaul the troubled home insurance market. Will it work?

> **Source:** East Bay Times  
> **Published:** 2026-11-17  
> **URL:** https://www.eastbaytimes.com/2026/11/17/the-new-state-insurance-commissioner-has-a-plan-to-overhaul-the-troubled-home-insurance-market-will-it-work-3/

## Summary

California’s incoming insurance commissioner Jane Kim has proposed a government-run wildfire insurance program to address the state’s troubled homeowners insurance market. The proposal would make wildfire coverage mandatory while shifting claim risk and financing responsibility to the state.
