# Connecticut health insurers seek second year of double-digit premium hikes

> **Source:** Insurance Business  
> **Published:** 2026-08-27 12:30 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/benefits/connecticut-health-insurers-seek-second-year-of-doubledigit-premium-hikes-587718.aspx

## Summary

Connecticut carriers are seeking more than 16% on individual plans and more than 17% on small group
for 2027 - stacked on a 16.8% approved increase from 2025. The September ruling lands six weeks
before open enrollment. Brokers with small group clients in the state should not be waiting for the
finalized number
