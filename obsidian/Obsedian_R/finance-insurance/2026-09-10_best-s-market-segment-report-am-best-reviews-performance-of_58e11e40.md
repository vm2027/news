> **Published:** 2026-09-10  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/bests-market-segment-report-am-070000640.html

## Summary

AM Best’s new Market Segment Report finds that the Lloyd’s insurance market delivered an excellent return on capital above 20% for a third consecutive year in 2025. The analysis shows that, despite shifts in rankings among the largest syndicates over the past decade, the top players remain broadly consistent, indicating structural stability in the market. The strong profitability and capital position of Lloyd’s syndicates are key for global specialty insurance capacity.
