# Vendors caused most of June's biggest health data breaches

> **Source:** Insurance Business  
> **Published:** 2026-09-08  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/sitemap/2026-09/vendors-caused-most-of-junes-biggest-health-data-breaches-497869.aspx

## Summary

An industry review of major U.S. health data breaches in June finds that third‑party vendors were responsible for the majority of large incidents. The findings underscore growing supply‑chain and vendor‑management risk for health insurers and providers, with implications for cyber coverage, liability and regulatory exposure.
