## Summary

Bloomberg’s markets wrap reports that global stocks and bonds rose while oil prices fell below $100 as hopes for diplomatic progress on the Iran conflict eased inflation worries.[14] The article notes the S&P 500’s strong advance, a 2.5% climb in the Nasdaq 100 led by chipmakers, 10-year U.S. Treasury yields dropping below 5%, and Bitcoin surpassing $85,000, reflecting improved risk sentiment across asset classes.[14]
