# Parliamentary Joint Committee on Corporations and Financial Services Opening Statement – 4 September 2026

> **Source:** Australian Securities and Investments Commission (ASIC)  
> **Published:** 2026-09-04  
> **URL:** https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/speeches/parliamentary-joint-committee-on-corporations-and-financial-services-opening-statement-4-september-2026

## Summary

ASIC’s opening statement to the Parliamentary Joint Committee outlines regulatory priorities across corporations and financial services, including insurance. It highlights findings from a motor vehicle insurance review showing poor disclosure around premium drivers and potential savings from annual payments, and describes ongoing work to improve consumer outcomes in financial products.
