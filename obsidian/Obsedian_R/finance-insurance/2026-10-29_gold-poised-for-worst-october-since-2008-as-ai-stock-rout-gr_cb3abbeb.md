# Gold Poised for Worst October Since 2008 as AI Stock Rout Grows

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-29 23:32 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-29/gold-steadies-near-4-000-as-inflation-keeps-rate-hike-bets-firm

## Summary

Gold tumbled to a one-year low and headed for its worst October since the global financial crisis as
rising oil prices stoked inflation concerns and a global tech rout spurred investors to sell bullion
to cover losses elsewhere.
