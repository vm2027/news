# Europe’s Solar Boom Has a Storage Problem

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-12 14:05 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-12/europe-s-solar-boom-has-a-storage-problem-video

## Summary

Solar has become Europe’s largest source of power during summer months, helped by government
incentives, falling technology costs and a rapid buildout across rooftops and utility-scale
projects. But that success is creating a new problem: on sunny days, Europe can produce more solar
power than the system can absorb, pushing wholesale electricity prices to zero or below and leaving
some renewable generators suffering from their own success. Industry experts say the answer is not
less solar, but more storage, more flexible demand and stronger grids that can move power from where
it is generated to where it is needed. (Source: Bloomberg)
