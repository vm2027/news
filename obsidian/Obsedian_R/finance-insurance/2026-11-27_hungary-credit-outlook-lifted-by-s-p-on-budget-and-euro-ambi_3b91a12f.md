# Hungary Credit Outlook Lifted by S&P on Budget and Euro Ambition

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-11-27 21:19 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-27/hungary-credit-outlook-lifted-by-s-p-on-budget-and-euro-ambition

## Summary

S&amp;P Global Ratings lifted the outlook on Hungary’s sovereign debt to stable from negative,
citing planned steps by Prime Minister Peter Magyar’s cabinet to cut the budget deficit and
eventually adopt the euro.
