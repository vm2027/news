# Indiana Residential Services Provider to Pay $65K Over Discrimination Suit

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-09-04 16:05 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/midwest/2026/09/04/884103.htm

## Summary

Damar Services, Inc., an Indianapolis residential services provider for individuals with
developmental disabilities, will pay $65,000 and provide other relief to settle a disability
discrimination lawsuit brought by the U.S. Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC), the
federal agency announced today. &#8230;
