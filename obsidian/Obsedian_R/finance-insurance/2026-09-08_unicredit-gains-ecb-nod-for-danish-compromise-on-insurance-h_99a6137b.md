# UniCredit Gains ECB Nod for Danish Compromise on Insurance Holdings

> **Source:** Global Banking & Finance Review  
> **Published:** 2026-09-08  
> **URL:** https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/unicredit-gets-ecb-approval-use-danish-compromise-insurance/

## Summary

UniCredit has obtained European Central Bank approval to apply the 'Danish Compromise' prudential treatment to its insurance holdings from Q3 2026. The change allows the bank to risk‑weight its insurance stakes instead of fully deducting them from capital, reducing the regulatory capital impact and aligning with EU rules for bancassurance groups.
