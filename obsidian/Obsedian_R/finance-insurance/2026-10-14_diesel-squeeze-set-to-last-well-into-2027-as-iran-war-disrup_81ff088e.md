# Diesel squeeze set to last well into 2027 as Iran war disrupts supply, IEA says

> **Source:** Energy  
> **Published:** 2026-10-14 10:37 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/14/diesel-deal-oil-price-iea-report.html

## Summary

Diesel supply is expected to remain tight into 2027 as Iran war-related disruption, falling
inventories and resilient demand strain the market, the IEA said.
