# Prudential Financial (PRU) Stock Sees Modest Fair Value Lift After Q2 Analyst Revisions

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-09-04  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/prudential-financial-pru-stock-sees-070937058.html

## Summary

Analysts have modestly raised their fair value estimate for Prudential Financial’s stock to about $113.40, up from roughly $110.53, following revisions after second-quarter results. The story discusses the valuation changes in the context of Prudential’s earnings, capital position, and key business segments, providing investors with updated guidance on the life insurer’s market outlook.
