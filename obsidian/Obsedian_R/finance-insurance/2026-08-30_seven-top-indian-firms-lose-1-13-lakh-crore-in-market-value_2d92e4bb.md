# Seven top Indian firms lose ₹1.13 lakh crore in market value as equities extend corrective phase; Reliance and Bharti Airtel hit hardest

> **Source:** Fortune India  
> **Published:** 2026-08-30  
> **URL:** https://www.fortuneindia.com/markets/seven-top-indian-firms-lose-113-lakh-crore-in-market-value-as-equities-extend-corrective-phase-reliance-and-bharti-airtel-hit-hardest/156490

## Summary

Seven of India’s ten most valuable listed companies together lost ₹1.13 lakh crore in market capitalisation over the past week as the equity market’s corrective phase continued. Reliance Industries and Bharti Airtel were among the biggest losers, while concerns over global interest rates, geopolitical risks and auction-session volatility weighed on investor sentiment.
