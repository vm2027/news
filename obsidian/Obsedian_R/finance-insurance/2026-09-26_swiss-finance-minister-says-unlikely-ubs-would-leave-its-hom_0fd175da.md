# Swiss finance minister says unlikely UBS would leave its home base

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-09-26  
> **URL:** https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/swiss-finance-minister-says-unlikely-ubs-would-leave-its-home-base-2026-09-26/

## Summary

Switzerland's finance minister said it was unlikely that UBS would leave the country, addressing concerns about the bank's domicile and relationship with Swiss authorities. The comments come as Switzerland continues to assess the implications of UBS's expanded role after its acquisition of Credit Suisse.
