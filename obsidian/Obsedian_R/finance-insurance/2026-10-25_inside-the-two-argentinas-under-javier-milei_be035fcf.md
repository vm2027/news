# Inside The Two Argentinas Under Javier Milei

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-25 14:04 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-10-25/inside-the-two-argentinas-under-javier-milei-video

## Summary

President Javier Milei has brought inflation down and opened Argentina to more investment, helping
fuel a boom in energy and other export industries. But manufacturing, retail and small businesses
are struggling, leaving many Argentines feeling that the recovery is passing them by. One year
before the election, Milei’s biggest test may be unifying an increasingly fragmented economy.
