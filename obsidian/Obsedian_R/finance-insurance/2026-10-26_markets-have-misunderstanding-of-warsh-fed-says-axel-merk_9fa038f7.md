# Markets Have 'Misunderstanding' of Warsh Fed, Says Axel Merk

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-26 19:43 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-10-26/markets-have-misunderstanding-of-warsh-fed-says-merk-video

## Summary

Axel Merk, president and CIO of Merk Investments, said that the market misinterprets Warsh's
comments on the Fed's current monetary policy as being in support of those policies, including the
size of the Fed's balance sheet. Merk, who recently met with Warsh, said that he is rooting for him,
despite his past criticism of Fed chairs. (Source: Bloomberg)
