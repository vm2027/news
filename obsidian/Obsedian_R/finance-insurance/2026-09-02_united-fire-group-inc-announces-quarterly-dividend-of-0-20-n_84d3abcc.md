# United Fire Group, Inc Announces Quarterly Dividend of $0.20 (NASDAQ:UFCS)

> **Source:** MarketBeat  
> **Published:** 2026-09-02  
> **URL:** https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/dividend-united-fire-group-inc-announces-quarterly-dividend-of-020-nasdaq-ufcs-2026-09-02/

## Summary

United Fire Group declared a quarterly dividend of $0.20 per share, payable on September 18 to shareholders of record as of September 4, reinforcing its capital return policy to investors. The piece also highlights that the insurer substantially beat quarterly earnings expectations, posting $1.30 EPS versus the $0.69 consensus and revenue of $383.73 million against forecasts of $347.76 million.
