# Stocks Tumble as US-Iran Stalemate Stokes Inflation Concerns

## Summary

Stocks declined on Monday as a stalemate between the US and Iran sparked concerns that inflation
would persist because of higher energy prices, forcing the Federal Reserve to raise interest rates
more aggressively.
