# Nvidia turns to insurers to spread the risk of AI build-out

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-09-29  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/nvidia-turns-insurers-spread-risk-040431309.html

## Summary

Nvidia has held discussions with insurers about transferring some of the risks associated with financing chips supplied to emerging cloud-computing companies. Potential structures could cover losses if borrowers default and pledged Nvidia chips cannot be resold for enough to repay lenders.
