# S&P 500 Steadies After Three-Day Skid as Oil Prices Halt Advance

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-02 14:08 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-02/s-p-500-steadies-after-three-day-skid-as-oil-prices-halt-advance

## Summary

US stocks flipped between small gains and losses on Wednesday after a three-day skid, as oil prices
halted a sharp advance, easing inflation concerns as traders weigh the Federal Reserve’s potential
path for interest-rate hikes.
