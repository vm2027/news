# European shares slip on fresh U.S. chip curbs

> **Source:** Investing.com  
> **Published:** 2026-11-17  
> **URL:** https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/european-shares-slip-fresh-u-083821310.html

## Summary

European equities declined as tighter U.S. semiconductor export controls and worsening concerns around Nvidia weighed on technology stocks. The STOXX 600 fell 0.5%, while Germany’s DAX, France’s CAC 40, and the FTSE 100 also moved lower.
