# India Frees ‘Cockroach’ Leader As Voter-List Row Simmers

## Summary

The founder of India’s “Cockroach” protest movement was released on Saturday after being taken into
police custody, according to its legal affairs head, as a widening fight over the country’s voter
rolls keeps New Delhi on edge.
