# Cyber insurance premiums climb as fridge attacks and Binance expose rising uncovered losses: GlobalData

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-11-17  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/news/cyber-insurance-premiums-climb-fridge-054927361.html

## Summary

Global cyber-insurance premiums are forecast to grow as attacks become more frequent and businesses seek additional protection. GlobalData expects premiums to reach $16.6 billion in 2026 and $18.3 billion in 2027, while noting that some major cyber losses remain outside standard policy coverage.
