# UniCredit gets ECB approval to use lighter capital rules on insurance holdings

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-09-08  
> **URL:** https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/unicredit-gets-ecb-approval-use-danish-compromise-insurance-holdings-2026-09-08/

## Summary

The European Central Bank has authorised UniCredit to apply the 'Danish Compromise', a more favourable regulatory treatment for insurance holdings when calculating capital requirements. This approval allows the Italian lender to reduce the impact of its insurance investments on its capital reserves, potentially freeing up capacity for lending or shareholder returns. The move underscores ongoing regulatory flexibility around bank–insurance group structures in European financial markets.
