# Mota-Engil Wins Deal to Operate US-Backed Congo Copper Railway

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-26 09:27 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/mota-engil-said-to-win-deal-for-us-backed-congo-copper-railway

## Summary

Mota-Engil SGPS SA secured a 30-year concession with the Democratic Republic of Congo to operate a
key copper and cobalt railway, potentially backed with up to $1 billion of US financing.
