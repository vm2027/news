# Lloyd's £1.4bn Iran war loss estimate is largely based on exposures, CFO says

> **Source:** Insurance Business  
> **Published:** 2026-09-04  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/breaking-news/lloyds-14bn-iran-war-loss-estimate-is-largely-based-on-exposures-cfo-says-502973.aspx

## Summary

Lloyd’s CFO explains that the market’s £1.4 billion estimated loss from the Iran war is primarily derived from exposure analysis rather than fully developed claims data, reflecting the early stage of loss emergence. The article discusses how delegated underwriting, broker dynamics and evolving war‑risk coverage are reshaping Lloyd’s risk profile and market strategy amid heightened geopolitical volatility.
