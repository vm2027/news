# US Natural Gas Falls as Iran Pressure Plan Pulls Down Energy

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-25 13:48 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-25/us-natural-gas-drops-with-oil-on-effects-of-iran-pressure-plan

## Summary

US natural gas futures ended lower, but pared back most losses, after earlier being pulled down by
crude and European gas futures. Those global energy prices fell on a US plan to increase economic
pressure on Iran that seemed less likely to affect physical supply than investors expected.
