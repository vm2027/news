# Hertz Taps PJT Partners to Extend Debt as Maturities Loom

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-30 19:07 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-30/hertz-taps-pjt-partners-to-extend-debt-as-maturities-loom

## Summary

Hertz Global Holdings Inc., the car rental company, is working with adviser PJT Partners to extend
the maturities of its debt, some of which will come due in two months, according to people familiar
with the matter.
