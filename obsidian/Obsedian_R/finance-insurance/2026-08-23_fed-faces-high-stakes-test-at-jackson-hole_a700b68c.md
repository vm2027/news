# Fed Faces High Stakes Test at Jackson Hole

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-23 14:47 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-23/fed-faces-high-stakes-test-at-jackson-hole-video

## Summary

Fed Chair Kevin Warsh heads into Jackson Hole under heightened scrutiny after the Treasury’s
intervention in the bond market raised questions about the traditional boundaries between fiscal and
monetary policy. Host of Bloomberg Money Tom Keene and Veda Partners Co-Founder Henrietta Treyz are
on Bloomberg This Weekend and discuss how investors will be watching for how Warsh addresses
inflation, market volatility and Fed independence as economic and geopolitical uncertainty
complicate the outlook. (Source: Bloomberg)
