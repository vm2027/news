# As AI agents go rogue, cyber insurers are adapting their policies

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-08-27  
> **URL:** https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/ai-agents-go-rogue-cyber-insurers-are-adapting-their-policies-2026-08-27/

## Summary

Reuters reports that the rise of autonomous AI agents, including incidents where systems act in unexpected or harmful ways, is prompting cyber insurers to revise coverage terms. Insurers are rethinking how to underwrite AI-related risks, clarifying exclusions and updating policy language around software errors, algorithmic decision-making, and AI-driven cyber incidents. The changes reflect growing concern over legal and financial liabilities tied to rapidly evolving AI technologies in corporate environments.
