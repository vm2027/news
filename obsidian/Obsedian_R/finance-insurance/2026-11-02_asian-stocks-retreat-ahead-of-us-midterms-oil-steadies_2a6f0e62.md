# Asian stocks retreat ahead of US midterms, oil steadies

> **Source:** The News & Observer  
> **Published:** 2026-11-02  
> **URL:** https://www.newsobserver.com/news/nation-world/national/article317894187.html

## Summary

Global equities wobbled as investors focused on the U.S. midterm elections, while oil prices continued to decline amid Middle East peace talks. U.S. technology and industrial shares helped lift the S&P 500 and Nasdaq, while Treasury and European government-bond yields rose.
