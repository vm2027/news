# Samsung Fire & Marine to acquire control of Canopius with KKR and KIC

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-11-10  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/news/samsung-fire-marine-acquire-control-142108933.html

## Summary

Samsung Fire & Marine Insurance agreed to acquire an additional 40.6% stake in specialty insurer Canopius, increasing its ownership to 80.6%. The transaction, valued at approximately 2.26 trillion won, involves KKR and the Korea Investment Corporation.
