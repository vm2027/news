# Incoming Swiss bank lobby chief warns against over-regulation, as India plans AI agent payments framework

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-08-30  
> **URL:** https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/incoming-swiss-bank-lobby-chief-warns-against-over-regulation-ft-says-2026-08-30/

## Summary

The incoming head of Switzerland’s banking lobby cautioned against excessive regulation, arguing it could undermine the competitiveness of the sector, according to a Financial Times interview.[15] The article also reports that India is preparing a framework to allow AI agents to make small digital payments via UPI without individual transaction approval, potentially creating one of the world’s largest networks for agentic payments.[15]
