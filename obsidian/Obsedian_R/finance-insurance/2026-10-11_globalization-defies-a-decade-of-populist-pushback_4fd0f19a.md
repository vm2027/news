# Globalization Defies a Decade of Populist Pushback

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-11 17:03 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-10-11/globalization-defies-a-decade-of-populist-pushback-video

## Summary

Bloomberg News Senior Economics Writer Shawn Donnan tells Bloomberg This Weekend that globalization
has continued to expand despite a decade of populist policies, tariffs and efforts to reshape
international trade following Brexit and President Donald Trump's first election victory. Speaking
with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Donnan says growing cross-border investment, global
supply chains and digital technology demonstrate that the world economy remains increasingly
interconnected despite political resistance. (Source: Bloomberg)
