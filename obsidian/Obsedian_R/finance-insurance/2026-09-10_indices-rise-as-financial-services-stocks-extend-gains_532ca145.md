# Indices rise as financial services stocks extend gains

> **Source:** Informist  
> **Published:** 2026-09-10  
> **URL:** https://informistmedia.com/EquityWire/59470/Equity-Alert-Indices-rise-as-financial-services-stocks-extend-gains

## Summary

Indian benchmark indices Nifty 50 and BSE Sensex edged higher as financial services stocks, including major banks and insurers, extended gains. Power Grid Corporation and HDFC Life Insurance led the rally, with other financial names such as Axis Bank and Bajaj Finance also contributing. The move underscores the current leadership of financial and insurance stocks in the Indian equity markets and their role in driving index performance.
