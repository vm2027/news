# Can the Fed Fight Inflation Without Hurting Farmers?

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-17 14:03 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-10-17/can-the-fed-fight-inflation-without-hurting-farmers-video

## Summary

Chicago Fed President Austan Goolsbee traveled to rural Illinois to hear directly from farmers
facing lower crop prices and rising costs for fuel, fertilizer and other inputs. Those conversations
offer the Fed a ground-level view of inflation pressures that may emerge before they are fully
reflected in national data. (Source: Bloomberg)
