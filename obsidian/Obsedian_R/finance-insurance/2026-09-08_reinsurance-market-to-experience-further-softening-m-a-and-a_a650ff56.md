# Reinsurance Market to Experience Further Softening, M&A and Alternative Capital Growth

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-09-08  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/09/08/884291.htm

## Summary

Insurance Journal reports that the global reinsurance market is expected to see further softening in pricing alongside increased merger and acquisition activity. Alternative capital outstanding reached a record high of over $144 billion as of June 30, 2026, underscoring the growing role of capital markets in reinsurance capacity.
