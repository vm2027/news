# Shipping's two-tier system is now a structural risk, nations warn

> **Source:** Insurance Business  
> **Published:** 2026-09-08  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/sitemap/2026-09/shippings-two-tier-system-is-now-a-structural-risk-nations-warn-497861.aspx

## Summary

Governments and industry bodies are warning that a two‑tier global shipping system, divided by safety and compliance standards, has become a structural risk. The trend raises concerns for marine insurers and P&I clubs, which face increasingly complex risk profiles and potential concentration of high‑risk fleets in certain market segments.
