# NBA Suspends Clippers Owner Ballmer After Leonard Salary Probe

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-02 21:04 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-02/nba-suspends-clippers-owner-ballmer-after-leonard-probe-video

## Summary

The NBA stripped the Los Angeles Clippers of five first-round draft picks and suspended owner Steve
Ballmer for one year after finding the team had circumvented salary cap rules by facilitating
sponsorship agreements between outside companies and star player Kawhi Leonard. Randall Williams
reports on "Bloomberg The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
