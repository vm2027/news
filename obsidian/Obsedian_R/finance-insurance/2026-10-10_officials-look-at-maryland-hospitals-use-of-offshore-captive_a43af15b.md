# Officials look at Maryland hospitals’ use of offshore ‘captive insurance’ companies

> **Source:** WTOP  
> **Published:** 2026-10-10  
> **URL:** https://wtop.com/maryland/2026/10/officials-take-another-look-at-hospital-use-of-offshore-captive-insurance-companies/

## Summary

Maryland officials are reviewing nonprofit hospitals’ use of captive insurance companies based in the Cayman Islands. The review will consider whether billions of dollars held by those entities should be subject to state taxes.
