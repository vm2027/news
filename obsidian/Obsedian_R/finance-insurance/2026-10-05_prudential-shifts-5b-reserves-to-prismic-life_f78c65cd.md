# Prudential Shifts $5B Reserves to Prismic Life

> **Source:** Briefs  
> **Published:** 2026-10-05  
> **URL:** https://www.briefs.co/news/prudential-shifts-about-5-billion-of-reserves-to-prismic-lif/

## Summary

Prudential Financial is transferring approximately $5 billion in reserves through a new reinsurance agreement with its Bermuda-based affiliate, Prismic Life. The arrangement covers more than $22 billion of U.S.-dollar liabilities connected to whole-life policies sold through Prudential’s Japanese affiliates.
