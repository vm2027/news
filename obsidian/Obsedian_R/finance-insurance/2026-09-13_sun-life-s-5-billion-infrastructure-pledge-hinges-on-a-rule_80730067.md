# Sun Life's $5 billion infrastructure pledge hinges on a rule change

> **Source:** Insurance Business Canada  
> **Published:** 2026-09-13  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/ca/news/breaking-news/sun-lifes-5-billion-infrastructure-pledge-hinges-on-a-rule-change-589560.aspx

## Summary

Sun Life has pledged $5 billion in infrastructure investment, but the commitment depends on regulatory changes that would affect how Canadian insurers can allocate capital to long-term projects. The article places the pledge alongside more than $85 billion in recent private capital commitments announced ahead of Prime Minister Mark Carney’s Canada Investment Summit, illustrating how policy adjustments could unlock substantial insurance-sector investment in national infrastructure.
