# Odd Lots: Why Is the Entertainment Industry Leaving LA?

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-25 18:22 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-25/odd-lots-why-is-the-entertainment-industry-leaving-la-video

## Summary

For Hollywood, business has been declining since the pandemic. Fewer shoots are taking place in Los
Angeles while movie productions are happening elsewhere in the world. On this episode of the Odd
Lots podcast, creative director of LA Material, Hayes Davenport, joins Tracy Alloway and Joe
Weisenthal at a live recording at the Vermont Hollywood theater to unpack why productions are taking
place outside of the historic heart of the American film industry. (Source: Bloomberg)
