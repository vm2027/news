# Illinois Launches Cover Crop Insurance Savings Program for 2027

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-10-14 14:23 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/midwest/2026/10/14/888877.htm

## Summary

The Illinois Department of Agriculture (IDOA) is offering its insurance discount program for cover
crops for the eighth year. The program, called Fall Covers for Spring Savings Program, encourages
cover crop acres not supported by other state or federal incentives. &#8230;
