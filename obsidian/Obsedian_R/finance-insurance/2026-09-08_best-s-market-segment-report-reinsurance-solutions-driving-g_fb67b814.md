# Best's Market Segment Report: Reinsurance Solutions Driving Growth in U.S. Health Insurance

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance / Business Wire  
> **Published:** 2026-09-08  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/best-market-segment-report-reinsurance-123000918.html

## Summary

A new AM Best report finds ceded U.S. health insurance premiums to the reinsurance market have surged to $203 billion in 2025 from $59 billion in 2016, over 300% growth in a decade. The study highlights how reinsurance solutions are increasingly used to manage risk, capital and volatility in the health insurance segment.
