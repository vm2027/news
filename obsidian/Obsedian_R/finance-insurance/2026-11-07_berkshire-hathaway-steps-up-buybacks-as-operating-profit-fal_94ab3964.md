# Berkshire Hathaway steps up buybacks as operating profit falls

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-11-07  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/berkshire-hathaway-steps-buybacks-operating-134600463.html

## Summary

Berkshire Hathaway increased share repurchases as third-quarter operating earnings declined 5.8%. Weaker insurance results were the main drag, including a 43% fall in underwriting profit and rising claims and expenses at Geico.
