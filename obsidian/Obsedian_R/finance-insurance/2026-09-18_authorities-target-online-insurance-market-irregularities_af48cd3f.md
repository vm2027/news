# Authorities target online insurance market irregularities

> **Source:** Asia Insurance Review  
> **Published:** 2026-09-18  
> **URL:** https://www.asiainsurancereview.com/News/View-NewsLetter-Article/id/96939/Type/eCin/Conferences/Gallery

## Summary

Regulators in China have issued a 10-point "negative list" to clamp down on irregularities in the online insurance market, covering sales practices, product management, payments, and distribution channels. The measures seek to curb misconduct in digital insurance platforms, strengthen consumer protection, and align pricing and risk management standards across new-energy and traditional vehicle coverage.
