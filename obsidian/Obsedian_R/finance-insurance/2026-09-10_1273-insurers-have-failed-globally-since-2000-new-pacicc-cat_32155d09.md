# 1,273 insurers have failed globally since 2000, new PACICC catalogue finds

> **Source:** Insurance Business US  
> **Published:** 2026-09-10  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/breaking-news/1273-insurers-have-failed-globally-since-2000-new-pacicc-catalogue-finds-5888xx.aspx

## Summary

A new catalogue from the Property and Casualty Insurance Compensation Corporation (PACICC) reports that 1,273 insurers worldwide have failed since 2000. The study catalogues insolvencies across multiple jurisdictions, shedding light on common causes such as inadequate capital, mispriced risk, and governance weaknesses. The findings have implications for regulators, investors, and policyholders in assessing systemic risk and the resilience of the global insurance industry.
