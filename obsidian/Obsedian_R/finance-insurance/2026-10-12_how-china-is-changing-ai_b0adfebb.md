# How China Is Changing AI

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-12 21:15 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-10-12/how-china-is-changing-ai

## Summary

As calls for an AI slowdown mount, Chinese and US governments are taking different approaches to AI
development. Our latest episode comes a week ahead of Presidents Trump and Xi's meeting and compares
the trajectory of China's AI models against their US counterparts. In this episode of Bloomberg
Tech: Asia, Shery Ahn speaks with Bloomberg Intelligence's Rob Lea and Bloomberg Economics' Michael
Deng to examine the factors that enable or hinder China's AI advancement. We also hear from Phancy
Group, a Chinese AI firm deeply embedded in the country's ecosystem. (Source: Bloomberg)
