# The Entrepreneurs Behind Poppi Start Over

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-11-14 16:13 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-11-14/the-entrepreneurs-behind-poppi-start-over-video

## Summary

Poppi founders Allison and Stephen Ellsworth tell Bloomberg This Weekend how they turned a farmers
market business into a nearly $2 billion beverage brand and what they learned from the decade-long
journey. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Lisa Mateo, the couple explains why they are starting
over with Jetty, a new hydration brand, and how lessons about social media, hiring and building a
team are shaping their second act. (Source: Bloomberg)
