# Sensex Gains 244 Points as Banking Stocks Rally on $127 Billion Forex Inflow

> **Source:** Whalesbook  
> **Published:** 2026-09-03  
> **URL:** https://www.whalesbook.com/news/English/bankingfinance/Sensex-Gains-244-Points-as-Banking-Stocks-Rally-on-dollar127-Billion-Forex-Inflow/6a98f50988c82d946721e45e

## Summary

This report details a strong rebound in Indian equities as the Sensex rises 244 points in early trade on September 3, 2026, led by banking stocks. The rally is linked to the Reserve Bank of India’s disclosure that $127.23 billion was mobilized through the FCNR(B) swap facility before its closure on August 31, boosting liquidity and supporting both the Sensex and Nifty toward higher levels.
