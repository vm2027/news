# China Life stock gains as profit jumps and sector support boosts sentiment

> **Source:** Ad-hoc News  
> **Published:** 2026-09-18  
> **URL:** https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/corporate-news/china-life-stock-gains-as-profit-jumps-and-sector-support-boosts-sentiment/70123974

## Summary

China Life’s shares rose after the insurer reported a sharp increase in first-half net profit to CNY 134.5 billion, up more than 220% year on year, supported by strong investment income. Investor sentiment was further buoyed by a planned CNY 35 billion capital injection from the Ministry of Finance and robust solvency ratios, helping drive gains across Chinese insurance stocks and related exchange-traded funds.
