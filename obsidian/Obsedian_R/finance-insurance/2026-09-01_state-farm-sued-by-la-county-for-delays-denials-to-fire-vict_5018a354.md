# State Farm Sued by LA County for Delays, Denials to Fire Victims

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/west/2026/09/01/766007.htm

## Summary

Los Angeles County has filed a lawsuit against State Farm, alleging that the insurer delayed and denied claims to wildfire victims, exacerbating financial hardship for affected homeowners. The case highlights tensions between local governments and major carriers over claims handling practices in catastrophe-prone regions and may spur regulatory review.
