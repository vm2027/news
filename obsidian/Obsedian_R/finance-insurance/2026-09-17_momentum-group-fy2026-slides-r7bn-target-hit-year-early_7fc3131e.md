# Momentum Group FY2026 slides: R7bn target hit year early

> **Source:** Investing.com  
> **Published:** 2026-09-17  
> **URL:** https://ca.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/momentum-group-fy2026-slides-r7bn-target-hit-year-early-93CH-4843089

## Summary

South African insurer and investment group Momentum reported normalized headline earnings of R7.06 billion for FY2026, achieving its R7 billion target a year ahead of plan and marking a 13% increase from the prior year. The results presentation notes that every business unit was profitable, with five contributing more than R1 billion, though the share price remains well below its 52-week high.
