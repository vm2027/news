# Thailand Shifts Disaster Risk to Insurers With $467 Million Plan

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/09/01/766002.htm

## Summary

Thailand unveiled a $467 million disaster risk transfer plan that moves more of the country’s natural catastrophe exposure onto private insurers and global reinsurance markets. The program is designed to improve fiscal resilience to storms and floods, while expanding the role of insurance-backed risk financing in national disaster policy.
