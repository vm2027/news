# TRIA has never paid a claim. A cyber terrorism scenario shows what happens when it does

## Summary

The article examines the U.S. Terrorism Risk Insurance Act (TRIA), noting that it has never yet paid a claim, and models a hypothetical cyber terrorism event to illustrate how the program would respond. It explores how losses would be shared between private insurers and the federal backstop, and the potential impact on insurance markets and corporate risk management. The scenario analysis is relevant for financial market participants monitoring systemic cyber risk and government–market risk-sharing mechanisms.
