# S&P 500 ends marginally lower as investors focus on US-Middle East conflict, Trump-Xi talks

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-09-24  
> **URL:** https://www.reuters.com/business/wall-st-futures-fall-middle-east-uncertainties-ahead-trump-xi-talks-2026-09-24/

## Summary

U.S. stocks ended slightly lower as investors weighed the unresolved Middle East conflict and an upcoming meeting between Presidents Donald Trump and Xi Jinping. Earlier losses in futures reflected concerns about inflation, oil prices, and higher interest rates.
