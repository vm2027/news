# Next 20 Days Is ‘Mission Critical’ for Retailers: Yih

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-11-25 22:31 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-11-25/next-20-days-is-mission-critical-for-retailers-yih-video

## Summary

Adrienne Yih, Barclays Managing Director and Senior Retail Analyst, says consumer spending is
holding up despite shoppers’ concerns about high prices. She argues that household income and
purchasing power have adjusted alongside inflation and wage growth, helping explain continued
spending. She speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg)
