# Mexico Governor Accused by US of Cartel Ties Returns to Post

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-21 18:01 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-21/mexican-governor-accused-by-us-of-cartel-ties-returns-to-his-job

## Summary

The Mexican governor accused by the US of conspiring with a major criminal cartel to traffic drugs
is ending his leave of absence, pledging to finish out his term through next year in a move that
could inflame tensions with Washington.
