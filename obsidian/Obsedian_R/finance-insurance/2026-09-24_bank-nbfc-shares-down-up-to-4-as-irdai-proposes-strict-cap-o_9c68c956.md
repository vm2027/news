# Bank, NBFC shares down up to 4% as IRDAI proposes strict cap on loan-insurance bundling and banca commissions

> **Source:** Moneycontrol  
> **Published:** 2026-09-24  
> **URL:** https://www.moneycontrol.com/banking/bank-nbfc-shares-down-up-to-4-as-irdai-proposes-strict-cap-on-loan-insurance-bundling-and-banca-commissions-article-14037076.html

## Summary

Shares of banks and non-bank financial companies declined after India’s insurance regulator proposed limits on insurance commissions and restrictions on bundling insurance with loans. The draft rules would prohibit lenders from making insurance coverage mandatory for loan approval and could change distribution economics across the sector.
