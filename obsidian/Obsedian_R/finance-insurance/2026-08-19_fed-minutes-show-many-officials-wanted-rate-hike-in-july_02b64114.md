# Fed Minutes Show Many Officials Wanted Rate Hike in July

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-19 18:29 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-19/fed-minutes-show-many-officials-wanted-a-rate-hike-video

## Summary

Several Federal Reserve officials favored an interest-rate hike last month and many indicated that
policy tightening would be necessary if inflation didn’t decline, a record of the central bank’s
most recent policy debate showed. The next Fed decision comes in September. Bloomberg's Michael
McKee reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
