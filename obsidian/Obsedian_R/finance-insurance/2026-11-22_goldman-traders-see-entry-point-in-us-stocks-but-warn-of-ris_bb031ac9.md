# Goldman Traders See Entry Point in US Stocks But Warn of Risks

## Summary

Goldman Sachs Group Inc.’s trading desk says last week’s selloff has created an attractive
opportunity to snap up US stocks. Still, the bank cautions that overall investor positioning remains
elevated, signaling the broader capitulation needed for a meaningful rally could be missing.
