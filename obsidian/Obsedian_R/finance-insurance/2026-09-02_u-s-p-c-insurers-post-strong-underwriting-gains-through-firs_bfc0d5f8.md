# U.S. P&C Insurers Post Strong Underwriting Gains Through First Half of 2026; Line-Specific and Geographic Challenges Persist

> **Source:** Markets Insider  
> **Published:** 2026-09-02  
> **URL:** https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/stocks/u-s-p-c-insurers-post-strong-underwriting-gains-through-first-half-of-2026-line-specific-and-geographic-challenges-persist-1036515201

## Summary

The U.S. property and casualty insurance sector generated an estimated $31.7 billion net underwriting gain in the first half of 2026, nearly triple the $11.6 billion recorded a year earlier when Los Angeles wildfire losses weighed on results. Despite the strong aggregate performance, the article highlights persistent pressure in certain lines and regions, underscoring uneven risk trends across catastrophe‑exposed and litigation‑heavy markets.
