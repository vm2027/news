# Italy’s Eni Caps Fuel Prices as Meloni Seeks to Shield Voters

> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-25/italy-to-convene-oil-refiners-as-it-seeks-to-boost-fuels-output

## Summary

Italy’s Eni SpA cut pump prices for at least a month, offering some respite for Prime Minister
Giorgia Meloni, who is trying to shield consumers from spiraling costs ahead of a general election
next year.
