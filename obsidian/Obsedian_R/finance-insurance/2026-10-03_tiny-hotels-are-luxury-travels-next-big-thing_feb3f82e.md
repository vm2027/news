# Tiny Hotels Are Luxury Travel’s Next Big Thing

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-03 16:35 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-10-03/tiny-hotels-are-luxury-travel-s-next-big-thing-video

## Summary

Bloomberg Pursuits reporter Sarah Rappaport tells Bloomberg This Weekend that luxury travelers are
increasingly seeking tiny hotels with as few as one to six rooms, combining the privacy of a villa
with personalized high-end hotel service. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini,
Rappaport says the trend is spreading across Europe and the US as travelers seek exclusivity, fewer
crowds and unusual experiences in restaurants, museums and historic properties. (Source: Bloomberg)
