## Summary

“This diesel price is a colossal problem,” says Paul Sankey, president at Sankey Research, as he
examines US plans to control more than 65 billion barrels of Venezuelan oil. Sankey says the
industry is short of refining and that’s “the big issue” for the global market. (Source: Bloomberg)
