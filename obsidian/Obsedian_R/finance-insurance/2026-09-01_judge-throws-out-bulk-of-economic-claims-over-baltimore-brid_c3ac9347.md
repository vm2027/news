# Judge Throws Out Bulk of Economic Claims Over Baltimore Bridge Tragedy

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/east/2026/09/01/766005.htm

## Summary

A federal judge has dismissed most of the economic damage claims stemming from the Baltimore bridge collapse, significantly narrowing potential liability for insurers and ship owners. The ruling affects businesses and municipalities seeking recovery, and is likely to shape how marine and infrastructure risks are insured and litigated in future disasters.
