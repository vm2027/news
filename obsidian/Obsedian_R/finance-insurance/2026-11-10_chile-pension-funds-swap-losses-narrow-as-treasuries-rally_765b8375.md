# Chile Pension Funds’ Swap Losses Narrow as Treasuries Rally

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-11-10 20:26 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/chile-pension-funds-swap-losses-narrow-as-treasuries-rally

## Summary

Chilean pension funds narrowed their market-value losses on foreign interest-rate swaps in October
as a rally in US Treasuries provided some relief to a system under heavy pressure from politicians
to reduce risks and boost payments.
