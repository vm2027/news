# Globe Life downgraded to Neutral from Overweight at Piper Sandler

## Summary

Piper Sandler downgraded Globe Life to Neutral from Overweight and lowered its price target to $165 from $200. The firm cited medical-claims severity, irregular Medicare Supplement claims, and less favorable underlying 2027 guidance.
