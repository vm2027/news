# Rates are falling faster than claims are – the UK industry knows what happens next

> **Source:** Insurance Business UK  
> **Published:** 2026-09-07  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/uk/news/breaking-news/rates-are-falling-faster-than-claims-are--the-uk-industry-knows-what-happens-next-588857.aspx

## Summary

Commercial insurance rates in the UK are declining sharply, with Marsh reporting composite commercial rates down 8% in the first quarter of 2026, including a 10% fall in property and 8% in financial and professional lines. Industry leaders warn that premium reductions outpacing claims trends could erode margins and lead to a classic soft market scenario. This dynamic may pressure insurers’ earnings and influence valuations of UK insurance stocks and related financial instruments.
