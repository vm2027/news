# Aon completes $17bn acquisition of US brokerage USI

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-11-17  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/news/aon-completes-17bn-acquisition-us-141908391.html

## Summary

Aon completed its acquisition of USI, a major US middle-market insurance brokerage and consulting company. The transaction values USI at approximately $17 billion and expands Aon’s position in the US brokerage market.
