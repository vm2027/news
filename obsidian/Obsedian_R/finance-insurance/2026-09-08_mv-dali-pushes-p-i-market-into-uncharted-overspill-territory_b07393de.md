## Summary

The fallout from the MV Dali incident is testing the limits of the protection and indemnity (P&I) insurance market's overspill mechanisms. Industry experts warn that large‑scale maritime losses are exposing vulnerabilities in mutual P&I structures and could trigger re‑examination of capital, reinsurance and pricing frameworks.
