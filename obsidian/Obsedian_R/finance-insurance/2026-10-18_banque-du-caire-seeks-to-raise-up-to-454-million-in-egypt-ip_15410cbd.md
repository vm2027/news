# Banque du Caire Seeks to Raise Up to $454 Million in Egypt IPO

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-18 14:40 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-18/banque-du-caire-seeks-to-raise-up-to-454-million-in-egypt-ipo

## Summary

Banque du Caire is looking to raise as much as 24.25 billion Egyptian pounds ($454 million) via the
sale of a 30% stake, in what would be the North African country’s largest initial public offering in
years.
