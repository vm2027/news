# Yields rise, oil jumps; US and Iran resume military attacks

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-08-31  
> **URL:** https://www.reuters.com/world/china/global-markets-global-markets-2026-08-31/

## Summary

Bond yields rose and global stocks fell as oil prices jumped more than 2% following a resumption of military clashes between the U.S. and Iran, heightening inflation concerns and the prospect of further rate hikes.[1] Despite the day’s losses, major equity indexes still posted gains for August, with the Nasdaq and MSCI global index both ending the month higher.[1]
