# House Passes Short-Term Spending Bill, Staving Off Another Fund Fight During Midterms

> **Source:** PBS NewsHour / AP  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01  
> **URL:** https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/watch-live-house-passes-short-term-spending-bill-staving-off-another-fund-fight-during-midterms

## Summary

The U.S. House of Representatives has approved a short-term spending bill that averts an immediate government funding standoff as midterm elections approach. The measure reduces near-term shutdown risk and provides temporary certainty for financial markets and federal programs, though underlying fiscal and debt-limit issues remain unresolved.
