# After booting 760K off ACA health plans, the Trump administration adds some people back

> **Source:** NPR  
> **Published:** 2026-10-15  
> **URL:** https://www.npr.org/2026/10/15/nx-s1-6002957/aca-fraud-trump-health-insurance

## Summary

The Trump administration has reinstated some people who were removed from Affordable Care Act health plans during an anti-fraud effort. Insurance industry sources said not all of the roughly 760,000 disenrolled people were improperly enrolled.
