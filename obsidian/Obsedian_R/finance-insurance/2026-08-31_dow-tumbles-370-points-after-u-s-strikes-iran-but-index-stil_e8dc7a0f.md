# Dow tumbles 370 points after U.S. strikes Iran, but index still heads for monthly gain

> **Source:** CNBC  
> **Published:** 2026-08-31  
> **URL:** https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/30/stock-market-today-live-updates.html

## Summary

Stocks fell and the Dow dropped about 370 points after the U.S. and Iran exchanged fire for the first time in a month, driving oil higher and adding volatility to markets.[13] Despite the geopolitical shock, Wall Street remained on track to close August with broad gains, led by the technology sector.[13]
