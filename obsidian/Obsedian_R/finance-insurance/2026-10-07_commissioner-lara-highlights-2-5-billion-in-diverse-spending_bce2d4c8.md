# Commissioner Lara highlights $2.5 billion in diverse spending ...

> **Source:** California Department of Insurance  
> **Published:** 2026-10-07  
> **URL:** https://www.insurance.ca.gov/0400-news/0100-press-releases/2026/release036-2026.cfm

## Summary

California insurers reported more than $2.5 billion in spending with over 4,300 diverse businesses during 2025. The figures were released at the 2026 Insurance Diversity Summit and covered businesses owned by women, veterans, LGBTQ+ people, people with disabilities, and historically underrepresented groups.
