# 'Windows of Opportunity' to End War in Ukraine, SACEUR Says

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-11 17:41 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-10-11/digi-full-intv-gen-alexus-g-grynkewich-nato-supreme-allied-commander-europe

## Summary

NATO Supreme Allied Commander Europe (SACEUR) General Alexus G. Grynkewich - the alliance's top
military commander - says the alliance should increase support to Ukraine when they're hit by
Russian hybrid attacks. On the "brutal" war, he said there are "windows of opportunity" to bring the
conflict to an end and that the alliance should seize them where possible to bring both sides to the
table. He made the comments in his first interview with Bloomberg TV's Oliver Crook in Warsaw on
Sunday Oct. 11. (Source: Bloomberg)
