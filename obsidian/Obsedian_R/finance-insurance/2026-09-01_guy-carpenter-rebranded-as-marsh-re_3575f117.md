# Guy Carpenter Rebranded as Marsh Re

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/09/01/766003.htm

## Summary

Guy Carpenter, a major reinsurance broker, is being rebranded under the Marsh Re name as part of a broader restructuring within Marsh McLennan. The move consolidates branding across the group’s risk and insurance operations, signaling strategic alignment in how reinsurance and capital advisory services are marketed to global insurers.
