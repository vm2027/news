# Oil Companies Taking Advantage of American Public, Says Rep. Burchett

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-23 18:13 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-23/big-oil-taking-advantage-of-american-public-burchett-video

## Summary

Representative Tim Burchett, a Tennessee Republican, wants to restrict diesel exports and says 'the
American public is getting gouged.' Burchett said that big oil companies are 'compounding the
problem' with prices at the pump and taking advantage of the American public because they have a
monopoly on the industry. (Source: Bloomberg)
