# Cigna raises annual profit forecast on strength in health services unit

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-11-05  
> **URL:** https://sg.finance.yahoo.com/news/cigna-raises-annual-profit-forecast-121601774.html

## Summary

Cigna raised its 2026 adjusted profit forecast after beating third-quarter expectations, supported by specialty pharmacy and health-services growth. Quarterly adjusted revenue increased 6% to $73.79 billion, and adjusted earnings per share reached $7.98.
