# Europe’s Diesel Crunch Draws First Mexican Supply in Seven Years

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-26 16:05 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/europe-s-diesel-crunch-draws-first-mexican-supply-in-seven-years

## Summary

Europe’s diesel supply situation has gotten so tight that the region is drawing on imports from
Mexico for the first time in seven years as the Iran war and a halt in Russian cargoes upends
historical trade flows.
