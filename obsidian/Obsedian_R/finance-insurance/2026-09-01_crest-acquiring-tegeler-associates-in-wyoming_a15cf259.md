# Crest Acquiring Tegeler & Associates in Wyoming

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/west/2026/09/01/766006.htm

## Summary

Crest Insurance Group announced it is acquiring Tegeler & Associates, an agency based in Wyoming, in a deal that expands Crest’s regional presence in the western U.S. The acquisition adds commercial and personal lines accounts to Crest’s portfolio and continues the consolidation trend among mid-market insurance brokers.
