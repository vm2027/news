## Summary

The rapid expansion of AI data centers is becoming a political issue in Ohio as residents raise
concerns about higher electricity bills, tax incentives and secrecy around local development deals.
Representative Greg Landsman joins Bloomberg This Weekend to discuss the issue with hosts David Gura
and Christina Ruffini. (Source: Bloomberg)
