## Summary

The article states that CNA Financial Corporation shares fell to about $48.71, down 1.22% intraday from the prior close, despite reporting solid insurance earnings. It links the insurer’s stock weakness to broader U.S. market nervousness, noting that indices like the Nasdaq and S&P 500 dropped amid bond selloffs and higher oil prices fueling inflation concerns.
