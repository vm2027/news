# Braskem Shareholders Pull Funding Offer as Debt Talks Hit Snags

## Summary

Talks between Brazil’s Braskem SA and its creditors regarding an out-of-court debt rework have
soured, with shareholders taking a proposed capital injection off the table, according to people
familiar with the matter.
