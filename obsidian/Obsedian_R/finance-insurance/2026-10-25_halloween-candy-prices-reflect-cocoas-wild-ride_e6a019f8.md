# Halloween Candy Prices Reflect Cocoa’s Wild Ride

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-25 13:55 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-10-25/halloween-candy-prices-reflect-cocoa-s-wild-ride-video

## Summary

On Bloomberg This Weekend, Ignacio Canalas Polo explains why chocolate and Halloween candy remain
expensive even after cocoa prices retreated from their peaks, pointing to persistent supply
pressures in Ivory Coast and Ghana and manufacturers’ efforts to manage costs. Speaking with hosts
David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Polo says consumers may see more promotions, but structurally
higher cocoa costs and product reformulation mean chocolate prices are unlikely to return quickly to
previous levels. (Source: Bloomberg)
