# USS Lincoln Sailors Describe Toll of Deployment

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-06 13:52 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-06/uss-lincoln-sailors-describe-toll-of-deployment-video

## Summary

The USS Abraham Lincoln’s crew received a long-awaited port visit in Thailand after a 286-day
deployment that included a modern-era record of 264 consecutive days at sea and months of combat
operations in the Middle East. Joining Bloomberg This Weekend is Bloomberg News Southeast Asia
Reporter Philip Heijmans who recently returned from Thailand and says sailors described food
shortages, limited communications with family and uncertainty over their return, while many also
expressed pride in their mission. (Source: Bloomberg)
