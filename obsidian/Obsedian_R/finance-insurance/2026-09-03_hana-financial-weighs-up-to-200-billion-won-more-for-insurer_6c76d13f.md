# Hana Financial Weighs Up to 200 Billion Won More for Insurer

> **Source:** Seoul Economic Daily (Sedaily)  
> **Published:** 2026-09-03  
> **URL:** https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/09/03/hana-financial-plans-up-to-200-billion-won-more-for

## Summary

Hana Financial Group is considering an additional capital injection of up to 200 billion won into its insurance unit Hana Insurance next year. The story discusses the rationale for the potential infusion, including strengthening the insurer’s solvency and supporting growth plans in a competitive Korean insurance market.
