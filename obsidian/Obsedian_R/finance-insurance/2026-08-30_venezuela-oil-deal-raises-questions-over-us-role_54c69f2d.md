# Venezuela Oil Deal Raises Questions Over US Role

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-30 13:08 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-30/venezuela-oil-deal-raises-questions-over-us-role-video

## Summary

The Trump administration’s deal giving a US-led venture access to 65 billion barrels of Venezuelan
oil could reshape the countries’ economic relationship, but questions remain over its political
durability and whether it will provoke a backlash inside Venezuela. Former Deputy National Secuirty
Advisor for Middle East Affairs and the Heritage Foundation's Victoria Coates is on Bloomberg This
Weekend and argues the agreement could encourage US investment and restore Venezuela’s energy
sector, but says economic development must ultimately be accompanied by political liberalization and
self-determination. (Source: Bloomberg)
