# Moderna, Merck Cancer Vax Gives Hope

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-21 21:53 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-21/moderna-merck-cancer-vax-gives-hope-video

## Summary

Scientists are on the cusp of fulfilling a decades-long quest: to use vaccines to teach the immune
system to spot and destroy cancer cells. News that an mRNA cancer vaccine developed by Merck & Co.
and Moderna Inc. kept melanoma from returning in a large, late-stage trial marks a critical next
chapter for a technology beleaguered by post-pandemic political backlash and years of false starts
before then. Bloomberg Opinion Columnist Lisa Jarvis writes "Moderna and Merck's Cancer Vaccine Is a
Marvel." (Source: Bloomberg)
