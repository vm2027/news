# Canadian Oil at Widest Discount to US Prices Since 2018

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-11-03 20:12 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-03/canadian-oil-at-widest-discount-to-us-prices-since-2018

## Summary

Canadian heavy crude prices reached their weakest relative to US oil futures since 2018, when a
massive pipline bottleneck prompted Alberta’s government to impose production limits on the largest
producers.
