# Treasuries Gain as Oil Drop Eases Pressure on Inflation, Bessent

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-25 13:30 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-25/treasuries-gain-as-oil-drop-eases-pressure-on-inflation-bessent

## Summary

Treasuries gained as a decline in crude-oil prices eased inflation concerns and pressure on Treasury
Secretary Scott Bessent, who has been trying to halt a months-long selloff that pushed the longest-
dated yields to the highest in almost two decades.
