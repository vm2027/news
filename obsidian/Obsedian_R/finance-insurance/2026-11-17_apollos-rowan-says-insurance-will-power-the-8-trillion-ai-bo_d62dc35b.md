# Apollo’s Rowan Says Insurance Will Power the $8 Trillion AI Boom

> **Source:** Bloomberg  
> **Published:** 2026-11-17  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-17/apollo-s-rowan-says-insurance-will-power-the-8-trillion-ai-boom

## Summary

Apollo CEO Marc Rowan said insurance and retirement capital could become a major funding source for artificial-intelligence infrastructure. He argued that private markets and insurers are well positioned to provide complex project financing for an AI buildout that could require as much as $8 trillion.
