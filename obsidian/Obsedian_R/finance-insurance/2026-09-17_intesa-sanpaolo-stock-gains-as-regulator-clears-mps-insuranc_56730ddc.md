# Intesa Sanpaolo stock gains as regulator clears MPS insurance stakes

> **Source:** Ad-hoc News  
> **Published:** 2026-09-17  
> **URL:** https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/corporate-news/intesa-sanpaolo-stock-gains-as-regulator-clears-mps-insurance-stakes/70120901

## Summary

The report says Intesa Sanpaolo shares rose after Italy’s insurance regulator IVASS granted preliminary clearance related to insurance stakes tied to Intesa’s takeover offer for Monte dei Paschi di Siena. The approval, which addresses indirect holdings in Generali and joint ventures with AXA, is seen as reducing regulatory uncertainty around the transaction and has kept investor focus on Italian banking stocks.
