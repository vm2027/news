# Mercury Insurance founder George Joseph dies at 104

## Summary

Joseph founded Mercury in 1961 on the premise that actuarial discipline could identify drivers being
overcharged by carriers using blunt rating factors. He built the company through independent agents,
and it stayed that way for 64 years
