# The Morning Risk Report: U.S. Hits Bank With 'Outcast' Action

> **Source:** The Wall Street Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-08-31  
> **URL:** https://www.wsj.com/risk-compliance-journal/the-morning-risk-report-u-s-hits-bank-with-outcast-action-e1976fd0

## Summary

The U.S. Treasury’s Financial Crimes Enforcement Network moved to revoke correspondent banking access for the UAE arm of Egypt’s Banque Misr under Operation Economic Outcast, effectively cutting it off from U.S. financial institutions.[11] Treasury also imposed sanctions on a Bank Melli manager in Dubai and a Hong Kong firm alleged to be laundering money for Iran, underscoring rising compliance risks for banks.[11]
