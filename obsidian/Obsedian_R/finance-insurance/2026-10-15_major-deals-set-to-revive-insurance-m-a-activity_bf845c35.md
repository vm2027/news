# Major deals set to revive insurance M&A activity

> **Source:** Valor International  
> **Published:** 2026-10-15  
> **URL:** https://valorinternational.globo.com/business/news/2026/10/15/mergers-and-acquisitions-regain-momentum-in-the-insurance-sector.ghtml

## Summary

Liberty Mutual’s acquisition of Brazilian insurer Austral and a potential sale of Prudential’s local operations could revive insurance-sector mergers and acquisitions in Brazil. Deal activity had slowed, with seven transactions in the first half of the year compared with 13 during the same period in 2025.
