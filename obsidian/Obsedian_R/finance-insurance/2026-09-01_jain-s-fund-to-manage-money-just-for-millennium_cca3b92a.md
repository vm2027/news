# Jain's Fund to Manage Money Just for Millennium

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01 14:16 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-01/jain-s-fund-makes-1-8-billion-profit-in-two-years-video

## Summary

Bobby Jain's multistrategy hedge fund made about $1.8 billion in trading profits over two years
while managing other clients' capital. Now it's going to manage money exclusively for Millennium
Management. Bloomberg's Hema Parmar reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
