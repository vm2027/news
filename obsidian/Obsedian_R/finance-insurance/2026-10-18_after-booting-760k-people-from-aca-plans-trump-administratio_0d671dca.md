# After booting 760K people from ACA Plans, Trump administration has added some back

> **Source:** Yelm Online  
> **Published:** 2026-10-18  
> **URL:** https://www.yelmonline.com/stories/after-booting-760k-people-from-aca-plans-trump-administration-has-added-some-back,410261

## Summary

The Trump administration removed about 760,000 people from Affordable Care Act coverage, citing fraud and improper enrollment. Insurance industry sources said the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services has since reinstated some people whose coverage was canceled incorrectly.
