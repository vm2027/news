# Aon stock falls 1.57 percent as USD 7 billion financing lands

> **Source:** AD HOC NEWS  
> **Published:** 2026-09-24  
> **URL:** https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/corporate-news/aon-stock-falls-1-57-percent-as-usd-7-billion-financing-lands/70176034

## Summary

Aon shares declined after the insurance broker arranged a $7 billion financing package connected with its planned USI acquisition. The financing includes $4 billion of delayed loan capacity, while recent results showed stronger adjusted earnings but weaker cash conversion.
