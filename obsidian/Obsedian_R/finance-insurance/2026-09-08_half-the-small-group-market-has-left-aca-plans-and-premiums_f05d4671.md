# Half the small-group market has left ACA plans — and premiums show it

> **Source:** Insurance Business America  
> **Published:** 2026-09-08  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/sitemap/2026-09/half-the-small-group-market-has-left-aca-plans--and-premiums-show-it-497865.aspx

## Summary

A recent analysis finds that roughly half of the U.S. small‑group health insurance market has moved away from Affordable Care Act‑compliant plans. The shift is reflected in premium trends, with employers increasingly turning to alternative funding arrangements and non‑ACA products to manage costs and coverage flexibility.
