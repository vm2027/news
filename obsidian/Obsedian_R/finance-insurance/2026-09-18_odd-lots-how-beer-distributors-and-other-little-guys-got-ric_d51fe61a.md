# Odd Lots: How Beer Distributors and Other “Little Guys” Got Rich

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-09-18 17:22 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-18/odd-lots-how-beer-distributors-and-other-little-guys-got-rich

## Summary

America's “Main Street Millionaires” comprise about three million private business owners who have
amassed one of the country's most concentrated pools of wealth. That's according to a study
conducted by Owen Zidar and Eric Zwick. On this episode of the Odd Lots podcast, economists and
authors of The Everywhere Millionaire: Who Is Really Rich in America and How They Got There, tell
Tracy Alloway and Joe Weisenthal how "little guys" like beverage distributors, dentists and car wash
owners have become millionaires hiding in plain sight. (Source: Bloomberg)
