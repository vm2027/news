## Summary

Insurance Journal reflects on 25 years of coverage of the insurance fallout from the 9/11 attacks, noting that insured losses total about $60 billion in today’s dollars.[11] The article highlights how the Terrorism Risk Insurance Act (TRIA) became central to managing terrorism risk and reports on recent legislation passed by the U.S. House to extend the public‑private partnership through 2034.[11]
