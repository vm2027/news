# Barclays turns bearish on European insurers as rally outpaces earnings growth

> **Source:** Investing.com  
> **Published:** 2026-11-17  
> **URL:** https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/barclays-turns-bearish-european-insurers-103137287.html

## Summary

Barclays downgraded the European insurance sector to negative from neutral, arguing that its recent share-price rally has exceeded expected earnings growth. The bank forecasts insurer earnings-per-share growth of 7% to 8%, below broader market and financial-sector expectations.
