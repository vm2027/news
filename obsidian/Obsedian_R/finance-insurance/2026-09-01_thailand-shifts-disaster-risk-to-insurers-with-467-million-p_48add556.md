# Thailand Shifts Disaster Risk to Insurers With $467 Million Plan

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-09-01  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/09/01/779002.htm

## Summary

Thailand announced a $467 million program to transfer more of its disaster risk to private insurers and global reinsurance markets.[1] The plan aims to reduce the fiscal burden of natural catastrophes on the government while strengthening the country’s resilience through structured insurance and risk-transfer mechanisms.[1]
