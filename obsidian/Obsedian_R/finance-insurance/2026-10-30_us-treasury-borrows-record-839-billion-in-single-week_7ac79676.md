# US Treasury Borrows Record $839 Billion in Single Week

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-30 13:00 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-30/us-treasury-borrows-record-839-billion-in-auctions-this-week

## Summary

The US government borrowed a record $839 billion in a single week via its sales of Treasury bills
and notes — both to repay holders of maturing debt and to raise the additional capital needed to
finance the federal deficit.
