# Mexican Peso Rout Deepens as Banxico, Fed Policies Diverge

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-11-19 15:34 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-19/mexican-peso-hits-17-month-low-as-fed-outlook-rattles-markets

## Summary

The Mexican peso sank past the 19-per-dollar mark to its lowest in over a year, as the prospect of
further Federal Reserve interest-rate hikes intensified a selloff triggered by Mexico’s dovish
monetary policy.
