# AI Revenue Questions Pull Semiconductor Stocks Off October Highs

> **Source:** Yahoo Finance  
> **Published:** 2026-10-10  
> **URL:** https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/ai-revenue-questions-pull-semiconductor-163800808.html

## Summary

Questions about the scale of OpenAI’s revenue contributed to a decline in semiconductor stocks, whose valuations depend heavily on expectations for AI infrastructure spending. Rising long-term Treasury yields and crude-oil prices added pressure to the sector.
