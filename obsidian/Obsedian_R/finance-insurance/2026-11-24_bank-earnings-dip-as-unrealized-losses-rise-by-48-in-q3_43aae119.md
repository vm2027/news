# Bank earnings dip as unrealized losses rise by 48% in Q3

> **Source:** American Banker  
> **Published:** 2026-11-24  
> **URL:** https://www.americanbanker.com/news/bank-earnings-dip-as-unrealized-losses-rise-by-48-in-q3

## Summary

U.S. banks earned $84.8 billion in the third quarter, down from the previous quarter but above the year-earlier result. Unrealized losses on securities increased sharply, intensifying concerns about banks’ exposure to market-rate movements.
