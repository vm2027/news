# Insurer profits hit 20-year high – so why are bosses nervous?

> **Source:** Insurance Business Australia  
> **Published:** 2026-09-07  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/au/news/breaking-news/insurer-profits-hit-20year-high--so-why-are-bosses-nervous-588813.aspx

## Summary

Australian Prudential Regulation Authority data show the general insurance industry’s after-tax profits have surged, marking the highest levels in roughly two decades. Despite the profit recovery from prior weak quarters, executives remain cautious due to concerns over underwriting cycles, inflation, natural catastrophe risk, and regulatory scrutiny. The tension between strong current profitability and future risk is shaping strategy and investor sentiment in the Australian insurance market.
