## Summary

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent vowed to ramp up pressure on Iran by targeting its economic
partners, saying "You are either with us, or against us." He is scheduled to talk more about the
plans on Monday. Bloomberg's Tyler Kendall reports. (Source: Bloomberg)
