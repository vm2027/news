# Traders’ Cost of Hedging Against Big Treasuries Rally Is Soaring

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-10-22 18:14 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-22/traders-cost-of-hedging-against-big-treasuries-rally-is-soaring-mvjv24q7

## Summary

The cost of options hedges against more gains in Treasuries surged to its highest level since March
on Thursday, as investors sought the safety of US government bonds following a spike in oil prices
and a deepening fiscal crisis in France.
