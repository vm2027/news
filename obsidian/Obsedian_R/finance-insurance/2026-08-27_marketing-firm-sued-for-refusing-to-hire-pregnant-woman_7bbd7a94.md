> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-08-27 15:44 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/midwest/2026/08/27/883143.htm

## Summary

International advertising and marketing firm Dentsu International Americas, LLC, violated federal
law when it refused to hire a pregnant woman for a Minneapolis-based position promoting alcoholic
beverage brands, the U.S. Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC) charged in a lawsuit
announced. &#8230;
