## Summary

AXA is rolling out a governed artificial intelligence infrastructure across its worldwide operations to support underwriting, claims and customer engagement. The initiative aims to balance innovation with robust controls around data protection, model risk and regulatory compliance in the insurer’s AI‑driven processes.
