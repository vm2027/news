# India Bars JPMorgan Unit for Alleged Market Manipulation

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-20 01:29 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-20/india-bars-jpmorgan-unit-over-alleged-stock-market-manipulation

## Summary

India’s securities regulator banned a Mauritius-based unit of JPMorgan Chase &amp; Co. from the
capital market in its first enforcement action over alleged manipulation of the country’s newly
introduced auction-based system for determining closing share prices.
