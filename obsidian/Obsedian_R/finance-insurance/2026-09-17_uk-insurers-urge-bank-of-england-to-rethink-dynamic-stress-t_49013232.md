# UK insurers urge Bank of England to rethink 'dynamic' stress test

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-09-17  
> **URL:** https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/uk-insurers-urge-bank-england-to-rethink-dynamic-stress-test-2026-09-17/

## Summary

Britain’s insurance industry is pressing the Bank of England’s supervisory arm to revise its live crisis simulation, known as a ‘dynamic’ stress test. Insurers argue that the exercise imposes heavy reporting burdens and relies on unrealistic scenarios that push firms “to their absolute limit,” raising concerns about proportionality and the design of prudential oversight.
