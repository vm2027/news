# Europe's Insurers Cut Coverage of Weather Losses, Report Says

> **Source:** Insurance Journal  
> **Published:** 2026-10-14  
> **URL:** https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/10/14/888874.htm

## Summary

European insurers are reducing or withdrawing coverage for climate-related risks and shifting more losses to governments and homeowners. The report highlights a widening protection gap, with only about one-quarter of natural-catastrophe losses in Europe currently insured.
