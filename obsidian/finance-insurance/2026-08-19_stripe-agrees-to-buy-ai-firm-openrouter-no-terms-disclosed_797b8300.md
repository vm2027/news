# Stripe Agrees to Buy AI Firm OpenRouter; No Terms Disclosed

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-19 17:09 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/stripe-agrees-to-buy-ai-firm-openrouter-no-terms-disclosed

## Summary

Stripe Inc. has agreed to buy OpenRouter Inc., adding one of the fastest-growing gateways to
artificial intelligence models as the payments giant pushes deeper into the infrastructure powering
the AI boom. Terms weren’t disclosed.
