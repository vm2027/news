# Carvana Up as Report on Walter’s Pledged Stake Allays Fear

> **Source:** Bloomberg Markets  
> **Published:** 2026-08-19 16:14 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/carvana-rebounds-as-report-on-walter-s-pledged-stake-allays-fear

## Summary

Shares of online used-car dealer Carvana Co. recovered some losses from a two-day selloff, after a
report showed billionaire Mark Walter’s stake in the company was pledged, meaning he wouldn’t be
able to immediately sell it as he contends with a federal probe of his investment empire.
