# EU will 'stand firmly' behind ICC chief as the US levies new sanctions

> **Source:** International news from the Americas - France 24  
> **Published:** 2026-08-19 00:59 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.france24.com/en/americas/20260819-eu-will-stand-firmly-behind-icc-chief-as-the-us-levies-new-sanctions

## Summary

European Commission president Ursula von der Leyen said Wednesday the EU would "stand firmly" behind
the International Criminal Court after the US levied new sanctions against its president, Tomoko
Akane, in a move the ICC called a "flagrant attack" on its independence.
