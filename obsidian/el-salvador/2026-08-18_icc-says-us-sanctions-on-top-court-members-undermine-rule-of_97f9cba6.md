# ICC says US sanctions on top court members 'undermine rule of law'

> **Source:** International news from the Americas - France 24  
> **Published:** 2026-08-18 20:31 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.france24.com/en/americas/20260818-icc-says-us-sanctions-on-top-court-members-undermine-rule-of-law

## Summary

The US on Tuesday imposed sanctions against two top members of the International Criminal Court
(ICC) which the court said "undermine the rule of law". The move represents the Trump
administration's latest salvo against the court based in The Hague, which was set up in 2002 by the
international community to prosecute war crimes, genocide and crimes against humanity.
