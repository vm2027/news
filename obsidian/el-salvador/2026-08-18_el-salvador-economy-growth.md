# El Salvador Reports Economic Growth Driven by Remittances and Tourism

> **Source:** Reuters  
> **Published:** 2026-08-18 09:30 UTC  
> **URL:** https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/el-salvador-economy-2026-08-18/

## Summary

El Salvador's economy posted continued growth in the first half of 2026, buoyed by record remittance inflows from
Salvadorans living abroad and a rebound in tourist arrivals. The Central Reserve Bank reported a 4.2% GDP
expansion year-on-year, above initial projections. Government officials cited infrastructure investment and
expanded digital payment infrastructure as contributing factors.
