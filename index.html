Immigration & TPS Update

The Temporary Protected Status (TPS) designation for El Salvador is set to terminate on September 9, 2026. USCIS is sending notices to TPS recipients who haven't yet received renewal EADs (category codes A-12 or C-19) confirming their expired TPS-based EADs are extended until the termination date.

Source: USCIS

Political Developments

President Bukele's party has officially filed his 2027 candidacy, following a legal overhaul that eliminated term limits — clearing his path to remain in power through 2033.

Source: Latin Times

CECOT Deportation Lawsuit

A group of Venezuelan men deported from the United States to El Salvador's CECOT maximum-security prison have filed a proposed class-action lawsuit against two U.S. aviation companies, alleging the carriers knowingly participated in unlawful deportation flights that resulted in arbitrary detention and torture.

Source: Ground News
